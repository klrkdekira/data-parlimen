## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Copyright Act 1987.

2. In view of the Government's decision for Malaysia to become a party to the Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works 1886 (including all acts, protocols and revisions thereto) (hereinafter referred to as "the Beme Convention"), the proposed Bill primarily seeks to make the provisions of the Act not only compatible with the provisions of the Berne Convention but also to enable Malaysia to fulfill her obligations, upon becoming a party to the Berne Convention, under the Berne Convention. Clauses 2, 6,7,8,19,20 and 21 therefore seek to amend the Copyright Act 1987 towards these ends.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section2oftheActsoastomakeitclear that though the Act is therein stated to apply to works made before the commencement of the Act as it applies to works made after the commencement of the Act, the Act's application to "foreign works" by virtue of the Berne Convention will have to be subjected to the provisions of the new section 59A, as introduced by clause 21, and regulations made under section 59A.

<!-- page 14 -->

4. Clause6 seeks to amend section I3oftheAct to firstly, provide more specifically copyright in the activities described in paragraphs (b) and (e) of subsection (1) of section 13, Secondly, to delete the proviso to sub- section (1) in view of the difficulties posed by having to comply with its provisions. Thirdly, to amend subsection (2) so as to provide in subsection

(2) as many areas of activities as are permissable under the terms of the Berne Convention which are not subject to copyright control. Of particular note is the introduction of new paragraphs (ff), (gg) and (ggg) where any use of a work for the purpose of an examination by way of setting the questions, communicating the questions to the candidates or answering the questions; the making of a sound recording of a broadcast, a literary, dramatic or musical work, sound recording or a film included in a broadcast insofar as it consists of sounds if such sound recording is for the private and domestic use of the person by whom

<!-- marginal: the sound -->

recording is made; and the making of a film of a broadcast, or a literary, artistic, dramatic or musical work or a film included in the broadcast insofar as it consists of visual images if such making of a film of the broadcast is for the private and domestic use of the person by whom the film is made, respectively, are exempted from copyright control. Paragraph

(l)of subsection (2) of section 13 of the Act has been amended to provide further for the exclusion of copyright control over any use of a work for the purposes of the proceedings of a royal commission, a legislative body, a statutory or Governmental inquiry or for the purpose of reporting any such proceedings. Fourthly, to make consequential amendments to section 13.

5. Clauses 7 and 19 seek to make consequential amendments to sections 15 and 58 (2) of the Act respectively in view of the amendments made to section 13.

6. Clause 8 seeks to delete the whole of section 24 which has been generally viewed as being incompatible with the Berne Convention.

7. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 59 of the Act by taking out the aspect of the Minister making regulations extending the application of the Act in respect of such works referred to in subsection (1) of section 7 to individuals in, bodies corporate established in or under the laws of, works first published in, works of architecture erected in a building located in, broadcasts transmitted from, a country which is a party to a treaty or a member of any Convention or Union to which Malaysia is also a party or a member and which treaty or Convention provides for protection of copyright in works. This aspect of section 59 is provided for by clause 21 which seeks to introduce a new section 59A to the Act. The new section 59A essentially seeks to provide more clearly the powers of the Minister to make regulations in order to put into effect Malaysia's obligation to extend copyright protection under the Act to "foreign works" under a treaty or a Convention or Union relating to copyright should Malaysia become a party to such a treaty or a member of such a Convention or Union.

<!-- page 15 -->

8. Clauses 3, 4, 5,10,11,15,17,18 and 22 generally seek to make consequential amendments to the Act as well as to clarify or provide more specifically certain provisions of the Act.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection (2) of section 36 of the Act to make it an act of copyright infringement for a person to import any article into Malaysia for the purposes enumerated in that subsection if the importation is without the consent or licence of the copyright owner and the importer knows or ought reasonably to know that the making of the article was carried out without the consent or licence of the copyright owner.

10. Clauses 12,13,14 and 16 seek to amend sections 44,46 (1), 48 and 54 of the Act respectively in order to generally enhance the powers of enforcement under the Act.
