## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the grant of a remembrance allowance to the recipients of the Decoration of Sen Pahlawan Gagah Perkasa and for matters connected therewith.

2. Clause 2 contains the definitions of several terms used in this Bill.

<!-- page 7 -->

## (REMEMBRANCE ALLOWANCE)

3. Clause 3 seeks to provide for the grant of a remembrance allowance of two hundred ringgit per month to a recipient of the Decoration of Seri Pahlawan Gagah Perkasa. The remembrance allowance shall be paid retrospectively commencing with the month in which the Decoration is conferred on the recipient, but not earlier than June 1983.

4. Clause 4 seeks to provide for the cessation of a grant of remembrance allowance and its restoration on the occurrences stipulated therein.

5. Clause 5 seeks to provide for the grant of a total sum of twenty thousand ringgit once only to the next-of-kin of a recipient where the Decoration of Seri Pahlawan Gagah Perkasa is conferred posthumously.

6. Clause 6 seeks to provide for the grant of a total sum of twenty thousand ringgit once only to the next-of-kin of a recipient where the Decoration of Seri Pahlawan Gagah Perkasa was conferred before the commencement of this Bill and the recipient had died before such date.

7. Clause 7 empowers the Prime Minister to apportion the amounts to be paid to a deceased recipient's next-of-kin.

8. Clause 8 provides that the remembrance allowances and sums granted under this Bill shall be charged on the Federal Consolidated Fund, while clause 9 provides that such remembrance allowances and sums shall not be assignable, transferable, etc.
