## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Medicines (Advertisement and Sale) Act 1956 (after this referred to as the "Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to insert into the definition of "advertisement" the words "report, commentary" so as to make it clear that they are within the ambit of such definition. This clause also seeks to amend the definition of "authorized officer" by deleting the word "registered" which qualifies "pharmacist" in that definition. The term "registered pharmacist" at the moment means a pharmacist registered under the Registration of Pharmacists Ordinance 1951, which is applicable only in West Malaysia. A pharmacist in the public service in Sabah or Sarawak therefore may not be appointed as an authorized officer under the Act. This amendment will remedy that situation.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of the Act so that the prohibition in that section does not only extend to advertisements calculated to lead to the use of any article as a medicine but also to advertisements calculated to lead to the use of any article as an appliance or remedy for, inter alia, the prevention or treatment of diseases.

Clause 3 also seeks to add a new paragraph (d) to subsection (1) of section 3 to prohibit advertisement calculated to lead to the use of any article for the purpose of diagnosing diseases specified in the Schedule to the Act.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 4A of the Act to make the prohibition in that section clearer and to extend the prohibition to advertisements referring to any skill or service relating to the

<!-- page 5 -->

## (AMENDMENT)

prevention and diagnosis of any ailment, disease, injury, infirmity or condition affecting the human body. At the moment it only extends to the treatment of such ailment, disease, injury, infirmity or condition. This would enable advertisements relating to radiological services and clinical analyses to be controlled. This clause also seeks to allow hospitals registered under the Private Hospitals Act 1971 as well as private medical clinics, private radiological clinics and private medical laboratories to advertise their skills or services subject to certain conditions.

5. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 6B of the Act to empower an authorized officer who is investigating the commission of an offence under this Act to order any person who appears to be acquainted with the circumstances of the case to attend before such officer for the purpose of an examination under the Act. The object of this amendment is to facilitate such investigation.

6- Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection (2) of section 6F of the Act to enable any authorized officer to conduct prosecutions in respect of offences under the Act. At present only a registered pharmacist in the public service who is authorized in writing by the Public Prosecutor may conduct such prosecutions.

7. Other amendments are minor or consequential in nature only.
