## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Audit Act 1957.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend the Act by introducing a new section 11 to empower the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to amend the provisions of the Schedules to the Act by order published in the Gazette.

<!-- page 2 -->

## BILL

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend the Act to provide for the implementation of the salary adjustment in respect of the Auditor General as approved by the Cabinet. The rate of the said salary adjustment is $110.00 per mensem.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
