## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Elections Act 1958. [ ]

BE IT ENACTED by the Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

<!-- marginal: Short title -->

1. This Act may be cited as the Elections

<!-- marginal: and com- -->

(Amendment) Act 1990 and shall come into force on

<!-- marginal: mencement. -->

such date as the Yang di-Pertuan Agong may, by notification in the Gazette, appoint.

<!-- marginal: Amendment -->

2. Section 2 of the Elections Act 1958, which in this

<!-- marginal: of section 2, -->

Act is referred to as "the principal Act", is amended by

<!-- marginal: Act 19. -->

substituting for the definition of "polling station" the following new definition:

"polling station" means any room, structure, vehicle or vessel, at the place appointed under section 7, set apart and equipped for the purpose of polling and counting of votes;'.

<!-- marginal: Amendment -->

3. Section 16 of the principal Act is amended by

<!-- marginal: of section 16. -->

inserting after the word "officer" in paragraph (j) of subsection (2) the words "or a presiding officer".

<!-- marginal: General -->

4. The principal Act is amended, in the Bahasa

<!-- marginal: amendment. -->

Malaysia text, by substituting for the words "pegawai pengetua" wherever they appear therein the words "ketua tempat mengundi".

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Elections Act 1958.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend the definition of "polling station" with a view of extending its use as a place for the counting of votes.

<!-- page 2 -->

3. Clause 3 seeks to empower the Election Commission to prescribe the circumstances in which votes counted by a presiding officer may be rejected by such officer as invalid.

4. Clause 4 seeks to change the designation of "pegawai pengetua" to "ketua tempat mengundi".

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial implication.
