## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the years 1990 and 1991 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1990, the Supplementary Supply (1989 and 1990) Act 1990, the Supplementary Supply (1989 and 1990} (No. 2) Act 1990 and the Supply Act 1991. [PN. (U2) 1675.]
