## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend certain provisions of the Racing Club (Public Sweepstakes) Act 1965, and to extend the operation of that Act, as amended, throughout Malaysia. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 (2) of the Act to introduce the definition of "racing club". The purpose of this amendment is to ensure that all horse races in the country are conducted by racing clubs which are members of the Malayan Racing Association. This will ensure uniformity in the racing standards.
