## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to enact a law to enable Malaysia to become a member of the International Fund for Agricultural Development by acceptance of the Agreement Establishing the International Fund for Agricultural Development.

2. Clause 1 provides for the short title and application of this Bill.

3. Clause 2 defines certain words and expressions used in the Bill.

4. Clause 3 provides for the authorization of Signature of the Agreement.

5. Clause 4 requires contribution to the Fund to be charged on the Consolidated Fund.

6. Clause 5 requires any increase in the contribution to the Fund to have prior approval of the Dewan Rakyat, signified by a resolution.

1. Clause 6 empowers the Minister of Finance, on behalf of the Government, to create and issue non-negotiable and non- interest bearing notes to carry out its obligations under the Agreement.

8. Clause 7 requires all other payments to the Fund to be charged on the Consolidated Fund.

9. Clause 8 requires all receipts from the Fund to be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

10. Clause 9 empowers the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to issue orders necessary for carrying the Agreement into effect.
