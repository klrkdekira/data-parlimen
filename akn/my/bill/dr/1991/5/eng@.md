## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to make consequential amendments to the Companies Act 1965 which are made necessary by the introduction of the proposed Securities Industry (Central Depositories) Bill.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 6A (10) by inserting a new paragraph (d) so that an interest in a share shall not be disregarded only by the fact that the share is held by, or in the name of, the central depository or its nominee company.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 39 (1) relating to the contents of prospectuses. The new paragraph (j) provides that where shares, notes or other securities have been specified by a stock exchange as a prescribed security under section 14 of the proposed Securities Industry (Central Depositories) Act 1991, the prospectus shall state that such shares, notes or other securities have been so prescribed and that applicants are required to have securities accounts when making their applications.
