## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to introduce some amendments to the Penang Port Commission (Amendment) Act 1984 (hereinafter referred to as "the principal Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 1 of the principal Act by introducing a deeming provision to allow the said Act to come into force with effect from the 20th January 1984, that is, the day after the publication of the principal Act in the Gazette.

Penalties for offences are however excluded from

<!-- marginal: the -->

retrospective operation.

The new subsection (3) seeks to validate every act or thing done by the Penang Port Commission before the commencement of this Act.
