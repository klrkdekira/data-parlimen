### intituled

### An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for

### the service of the year 1993 and to appropriate that

### sum for the service of that year.

### BE IT ENACTED by the Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri

### Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice

### and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in

### Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same,

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill makes provision for the issue from the Consolidated Fund a sum of twenty-one billion seven hundred and thirty-seven million fourteen thousand ynd sixty ringgit ($21,737,014,060) for the service of the year 1993 and for that sum to be appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule. [PN. (U2) 1728.]
