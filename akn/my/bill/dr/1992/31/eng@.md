## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Telecommunications Act 1950 (in this Statement referred to as the "Act").

2. Clause 1 provides for the short title.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 3 in order to clarify the additional requirement for a licence under section 37 whenever the telecommunications network involves a radiocommunication station. It also clarifies the need for Ministerial consent whenever a licence is to be assigned, transferred or sublet.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 35 to include the definition of "television receive only station" in the interpretation provision for Part V and to make consequential amendments thereto.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce a new subsection (2A) to section 37 to empower the Minister to exempt either absolutely or

conditionally, any low power radiocommunication apparatus or any class thereof from the requirement of a licence under that section.

6. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 3?A to enable the possibility of implementing systems licensing for certain specific services in which case the licence fee is collected only from the radiocommunications network operator, and a subscriber to that network will not be required to have a separate licence.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 38 to expressly exclude a "television receive only station" from the application of the proviso to subsection (1), and to introduce a new subsection (6) for the admissibility of a certificate given by an authorized telecom- munication officer as prima facie evidence of the facts stated therein.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 40 to empower the Minister to make regulations for prohibiting or regulating the working or using of any radiocommunication apparatus in any premises in Malaysia, and for giving effect to international treaties relating to radiocommunication to which Malaysia is a party.

9. Clause 8 seeks to increase the maximum fine which may be imposed under section 43 to fifty thousand ringgit in order to be consistently proportionate with the maximum fine of one hundred thousand ringgit provided under section 39 of the Act.

10. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new section 44A to provide for the powers of examination exerciseable by an authorized telecommunication officer for the purpose of ensuring an interference-free environment.

11. Clause 10 seeks to introduce a new section 47 A to provide for the admissibility of statements in evidence.
