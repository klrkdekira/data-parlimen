## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Companies Act 1965.

2. Clause 1 deals with the commencement date of this Bill.

3. Clauses 2 and 4 seek to amend sections 37 and 137 consequential to the deletion of section 179 by the Securities Commission Act 1992.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 132G to specifically provide for exceptions to the prohibition under that section.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend paragraph 364A(l)(d) consequential to the enactment of the Securities Commission Act 1992.
