## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Fisheries Act 1985. [ ]

WHEREAS it is expedient to amend the written law relating to fisheries in Malaysian fisheries waters and to turtles and riverine fishing in Malaysia;

AND WHEREAS by Clause (1) of Article 74 of the Federal Constitution, Parliament may make laws with respect to any of the matters enumerated in the Federal List or the Concurrent List, and whereas fisheries, including maritime and estuarine fishing and fisheries (excluding turtles), is a matter enumerated in the Federal List under item 9 of List I of the Ninth Schedule to the Federal Constitution and maritime and estuatine fishing and fisheries are also matters enumerated in the Concurrent List under item 12 of List IIIA of the said Ninth Schedule in respect of the States of Sabah and Sarawak;

AND WHEREAS by Clause (1) (b) of Article 76 of the Federal Constitution, Parliament may make laws with respect to any matter enumerated in the State List for the purpose of promoting uniformity of the laws of two or more States, and whereas turtles and riverine fishing are matters enumerated in the State List under item 12 of List II of the Ninth Schedule of the Federation Constitution:

Now, THEREFORE, pursuant to the relevant provisions of the Federal Constitution BE IT ENACTED by the Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consert of the Dewan Negara

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Fisheries Act 1985 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend the definitions of "fish-aggregation device", "fishing vessel", "local fishing vessel" and "pollutant" and to introduce the definition of "traditional fishing appliance". The proposed amendment to the definition offish-aggregation device" is

<!-- page 15 -->

to clarify the meaning of a fish-aggregation device. The proposed amendment to the definition of "fishing vessel" includes vessels used to carry fish feed in cage culture projects under the definition, thus bringing such vessels within the purview of the licensing provisions. The proposed amendment to the definition of "local fishing vessel" seeks to include within the meaning of the said definition, a foreign fishing vessel which is chartered or subchartered, leased or subleased by a Malaysian citizen or other body established in Malaysia. The proposed amendment to the definition of "pollutant" seeks to include any substance which is liable to pollute not only maritime waters but riverine waters as well. The proposed introduction of a new definition, namely of "traditional fishing appliance" is necessary in view of the proposed amendment to section 31.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 8 to make it an offence for any person not issued with a licence under the Act to undertake any fishing activity by whatever method, including in particular, the collection of any species of fish.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 11 to provide a presumption that any part of a fishing appliance is to be construed as a complete fishing appliance.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection (4) of section 13 by extending the period of appeal to the Minister to thirty days.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 14, to provide for the period of validity of a licence to be twelve calendar months and a new paragraph 14 (4) (aa) is inserted to allow licences to be transferred with the approval of the Director-General.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend the Act by introducing a new section 14A to provide that a person, not being a Malaysian citizen, found on board any local fishing vessel in Malaysian fisheries waters is presumed to be a crew member of such vessel.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection (1) of section 15 to add a further situation where a foreign fishing vessel may be allowed to conduct any techno-economic research or survey of any fishery, namely where there is an agreement for the same between a Malaysian citizen and owner of such vessel.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection (1) of section 16 to provide that passage in Malaysian fisheries waters by foreign fishing vessels shall be in accordance with rules of international law of the sea relevant to that part of the waters which is being navigated. Subsection

(3) of section 16 is amended to expand the methods of communication under that section to include also communication by telex and facsimile, and to prescribe the languages to be used', namely English and Malay.

<!-- page 16 -->

10. Clause 1O seeks to amend section 24 by inserting a new subsection (3) for a presumption that the onus of proving that a fishing vessel is not a foreign fishing vessel lies with the person charged with the offence in relation to which the issue arises.

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 25 by reducing the maximum fine to be imposed for any offence relating to local fishing vessel to twenty thousand ringgit.

12. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 31 to allow compound for certain offences provided that the offence committed is the first, second or third offence only and to allow a minimum compound of one hundred ringgit for an offence committed under subsection (1) of section 43 of the Act. The amendment to section 31 makes provision to compound any offence relating to a traditional fishing appliance. A provision is also made for the forfeiture of proceeds of sale of fish or article of a perishable nature.

13. Clause 13 seeks to introduce a new subsection (2) to section 35 to provide that a certificate issued by the Minister responsible for survey and mapping or the Minister responsible for defence stating that a map, plan or chart has been approved by the Government, shall be conclusive proof of the fact so stated.

14. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 40 to provide for the control on the transportation of live fish between the Federal Territory of Labuan and the States of Sabah and Sarawak, in addition to the existing provisions which only provide for the control on the transportation of live fish between West Malaysia and the Federal Territory of Labuan, the States of Sabah and Sarawak.

15. Clause 15 seeks to introduce sections 4lA and 4lB into the Act. Section 41 A seeks to establish the National Advisory Council for Marine Park and Marine Reserve to be appointed by the Minister. Section 41B seeks to provide the functions of the Council.

16. Clause 16 seeks to introduce new sections 47A, 47B, 47c and 47D into the Act. Section 47A seeks to empower any fisheries officer to investigate the commission of any offence under the Act. Sections 47B and 47c seek to provide for the powers of such officer to require the attendance of witnesses and to examine witnesses for the purpose of such investigation. Section 47D seeks to provide for the procedure where investigation carried out by a fisheries officer on any person arrested and detained in custody cannot be completed within twenty four hours.

17. Clause 17 seeks to amend subsection (2) of section 50 which relates to the computation of security to be furnished for the temporary return of any fishing vessel seized under the Act. Under the existing provisions, the maximum fine which would be imposable in respect of

<!-- page 17 -->

the offence committed and the expenses incurred in the seizure and repatriation of the crew shall be taken into account in determining the amount of security to be furnished for the release of both local and foreign fishing vessel. The proposed amendment seeks to provide that the security to be furnished for the temporary return of vessel is the aggregate value of vessel including fishing appliance, equipment, etc.

18. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 52 by specifying offences whereby the vessel and fishing appliance used in the commission of the offence shall be forfeited by the Court upon a conviction.

19. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 53 by reducing the maximum fine to be imposed for any offence under that section to twenty thousand ringgit.

20. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 56 of the Act consequent upon the amendments made to section 16.

21. Clause 21 seeks to amend section 58 by allowing the Director- General to exempt any vessel or person from all or any of the provisions of the Act for the purpose of survey on economic feasibility of any fishing activity.

22. Clause 22 seeks to introduce new paragraphs (aj) and (ak) into section 61 to empower the Minister to make regulations for the registration of any person carried or employed on board any local fishing vessel and the issue of an identification card to such person and to regulate the registration of any local fishing vessel.
