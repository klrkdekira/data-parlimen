## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code.

1. Clause 2 seeks to amend the definition of "Government Medical Officer" or "Medical Officer" in section 2 of the Criminal Procedure Code to include any medical practitioner who has been registered under the Medical Act 1971 and has also been given an
