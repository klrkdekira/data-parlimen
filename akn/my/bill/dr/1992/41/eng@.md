## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Medical Act 1971 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to introduce a new paragraph (c) to subsection

(13) of section 3 of the Act to provide an additional ground for disqualification for membership of the Medical Council. Clause 2 also seeks to introduce a new subsection (13A) to section 3 of the Act to disqualify a medical practitioner whose name has been struck off from the Register or who has been reprimanded from being nominated or elected to be a member of the Medical Council for a period of six years from the expiration of such suspension or from the date of such reprimand. Clause 2 further seeks to introduce new subsections (15), (16) and (17) to section 3 of the Act to provide that a member of the Medical Council in respect of whom a preliminary investigation has been commenced or a disciplinary inquiry has been instituted shall be suspended from being such member from the date of commencement of the investigation or the institution of the inquiry. However if the Council finds that no case has been made out against the member, he shall be reinstated.

3. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection (2) of section 13 of the Act to provide for the new period in which a provisionally registered person has to undergo employment in a resident surgical post, resident medical post and resident obstetrical and gynaecological post. Clause 4 also seeks to delete subsection (3) of section 13 of the Act. With the deletion of the said subsection a provisionally registered person who is the holder of the unscheduled qualification is no longer required to undergo the additional two years experience in a post in the public service as at present.

4. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new paragraph (c) to subsection

(1) of section 19 of the Act to empower the Council to refuse the registration of any person who is found to be unfit to perform his professional duties by reason of his mental or physical condition.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend subsection (1) of section 24 of the Act to provide for cessation of registration of a registered medical practitioner.

<!-- page 12 -->

## B I LL

6. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 34c which will enable the Director General to authorise registered medical practitioners who are not public officers to undertake the investigation, examination, treatment or management of any patient or to perform an autopsy or post-mortem investigation of any deceased person. The new section 34c further provides that the officers so authorised are deemed to be Government Medical Officers for the purposes of the Criminal Procedure Code or other relevant laws. This will render post-mortem reports or medical reports by such medical practitioners admissible as evidence in the court of law during their absence provided that all other procedural and legal requirements have been satisfied.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection (1) of section 35 of the Act lo increase the general penalty provided in that subsection.

8. Clause 10 seeks to provide that notwithstanding

<!-- marginal: their -->

amendments, certain provisions of sections 12 and 13 of the principal Act shall continue to have effect in relation to the persons specified in the said clause as if those amendments had not been made.

9. Clause 11 seeks to repeal the existing Part VII of the Act and substitute a new Part VII for national purposes. The new Part VII instead upon its commencement will continue to be in force until Parliament, by resolution, provides otherwise.

10. Clause 12 seeks to provide that the existing Part VII of the Act shall be deemed to have continued to be in force since 1st October 1986, since Part V II had ceased to be in force on 30th September 1986. In consequence, clause 12 seeks to validate any act or thing done or any notice issued by the Director General pursuant to the existing Part VII. However, penalties for offences are excluded from the retrospective operation.

11. Other amendments which are not specifically mentioned are of a consequential or minor in nature.
