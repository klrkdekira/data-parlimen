## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Securities Industry Act 1983, hereinafter referred to as the "Act", and references in this Statement to a "section", "subsection" or "paragraph" is a reference to a section, subsection or paragraph, respectively, in that Act.

2. Clause 1 deals with the commencement date of the Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 2(1) which contains definitions of words and expressions used in the Act. The deletion of words and expressions and the introduction of various new words and expression are consequential to the establishment of the Securities Commission under the Securities Commission Act 1992.

<!-- page 12 -->

4. Clause 3 seeks to increase the penalty for the offence under section 7.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 8 to empower the Minister to appoint the Executive Chairman of the committee of the stock exchange.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 9 to transfer the power of the Minister under that section to the Commission.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 10 to confer upon the Licensing Officer and the Investigating Officer of the Commission the powers to perform their functions and duties. This clause also seeks to increase the penalty for the offence under section 10.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 11. The proposed amendment seeks to empower the stock exchange to take action against any person for failure to comply with the rules or the listing requirements.

9. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 15A which empowers the Minister to appoint a Licensing Officer who will be responsible for matters relating to licensing under the Act. These matters are currently the responsibility of the Registrar of Companies.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend sections 16, 17, 18. 19, 20 and 21. The amendments are consequential to the introduction of the new section 15A.

11. Clause 10 seeks to increase the penalty for the offence under section 22.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend sections 23, 25, 26, 27, 28 and 31. The amendments are consequential to the introduction of the new section 15A.

13. Clauses 12, 13 and J4 seek to amend sections 33, 34 and 35 to give the Commission certain powers under the Act.

14. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 37. The amendment is consequential to the introduction of the new section 15A.

15. Clauses 16. 17 and 18 seek to amend sections 39, 40 and 41. The amendments seek to increase the penalties under those sections.

16. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 43 to replace the word "Registrar" with the word "Commission".

17. Clause 20 seeks to introduce a new subsection ( 1 A) into section 44. This subsection provides that any cheque issued but not

<!-- page 13 -->

collected within five bank trading days after the date of such issue shall be credited into the trust account. It also seeks to increase the penalty for an offence committed under that section.

18. Clauses 21, 22, 23. 24, 25, 26 and 27 seek to amend sections 49, 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56 and 57. The amendments are consequential to the establishment of the Commission whereby the powers of the Minister under the various sections are transferred to the Commission. The amendments are also consequential to the introduction of section 15A.

19. Clause 28 seeks to increase the penalty for an offence under section 58.

20. Clauses 29, 30 and 31 seek to amend sections 68, 69 and 72. The amendments are consequential to the establishment of the Commission whereby the powers of the Minister and Registrar under those sections are transferred to the Commission.

21. Clauses 32, 33, 34, 35, 36 and 37 seek to increase the penalty for offences under sections 91, 92, 96, 97. 98 and 100.

22. Clause 38 seeks to delete the whole of Division 2 of Part X. With the establishment of the Commission, the various powers of the Minister under that Division are no longer necessary.

23. Clause 39 seeks to increase the penalty for an offence under section 123.

24. Clause 40 seeks to introduce a new section 124 to empower the Licensing Officer to compound certain offences against the Act.

25. Clause 41 seeks to introduce a new section 126. relating to the conduct of prosecution.

26. Clause 42 contains savings provisions relating to the amendment of Part IV of the Act.
