## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Local Government Act 1976 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act'4)-

2. Clause I enables the Minister to appoint the commencement date of this Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by deleting the words "septic tank" in the definition of "building" and introduces a new definition of "sewage".

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new subsection (2) to section 72 to provide that effluent does not include sewage.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce a new subsection (2) to section 73 to provide that a local authority shall not make, amend or revoke any by-laws relating to sewage.

6. Clause 5 seeks to substitute section 128 with a new section 128 to revoke the power conferred on a local authority to impose sewerage rate.

7. Clauses 6 and 7 seek to delete certain provisions in the Act as they are now considered unnecessary.

8. Clause 8 contains a savings provision.
