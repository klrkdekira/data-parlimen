### intituled

An Act to amend the Town and Country Planning Act 1976. [ ]

WHEREAS it is expedient for the purpose of ensuring uniformity of law and policy to amend the Town and Country Planning Act 1976:

Now, THEREFORE, pursuant to the provisions of Clause

(4) of Article 76, BE IT ENACTED by the Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## BILL

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Town and Country Planning Act 1976 (hereinafter referred to as "the Act").

2. Clause 1 enables the Minister to appoint the commencement date of this Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the Act by inserting a new paragraph

(ba) as a consequence of the enactment of the Sewerage Services Act 1993.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1770.]
