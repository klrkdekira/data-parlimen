## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Public Health Ordinance of Sabah in force in the Federal Territory of Labuan as modified by the Federal Territory of Labuan (Modification of Public Health Ordinance) Order

1985.

2. Clause 1 enables the Minister to appoint the commencement date of this Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to define terms used in the Ordinance.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 111 of the Ordinance as regards the duty of a local authority. It provides that a local authority is no longer responsible for sewerage services in its area.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 112 of the Ordinance as regards sewers. Therefore, a local authority is no longer responsible to provide and maintain sewers.

6. Clauses 5,6 and 7 seek to amend sections 116,117 and 118 of the Ordinance by deleting the words "sewers and", "public and private sewers and" and "sewerage" as they are now considered unnecessary.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 119 of the Ordinance by deleting the words "cesspool or septic tank", "sewerage or" and "sewer or" as they are now considered unnecessary.

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 121 of the Ordinance by deleting the words "sewers or" as they are now considered unnecessary.

9. Clause 10 seeks to introduce a new provision. The new section 12lA provides that buildings shall not be erected over sewers without obtaining the consent of the Director General of Sewerage Services.

10. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 122 of the Ordinance by deleting the words "private sewer or" and paragraph (c) as they are now considered unnecessary.

11. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 123 of the Ordinance to provide that any works carried out to abate nuisance shall comply with the requirements of the Sewerage Services Act 1993.

12. Clause l3 seeks to delete sections 125 and 128 of the Ordinance.

13. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 130 of the Ordinance by deleting the words "cesspools, septic tanks, private sewers", etc. as

<!-- page 6 -->

they are now considered unnecessary. Therefore, a local authority is no longer responsible to provide the service of cleansing septic tanks or sewers.

14. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 131 of the Ordinance to provide that a local authority can no longer impose the duty of cleansing septic tanks or sewers on occupiers.

15. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 132 of the Ordinance as regards the power to make by-laws. A local authority no longer has the power to make any by-laws relating to sewerage services.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1764.]
