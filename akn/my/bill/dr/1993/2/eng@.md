## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Penang Commission Act 1955.

1. Clause 1 enables the Minister to appoint the commencement date of this Bill.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act and introduces a new definition of a "licensed operator".

3. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new section 14A to provide for the establishment of a port security force.

4. Clause 4 seeks to substitute section 24(5) with a new section 24(5) to enable the Commission to give various loans to its officers and servants for such purpose and on such terms and conditions as the Commission thinks fit.

5. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 25(3A) to enable the Commission to dispose of any capital asset or any asset kept in the store which is no longer required by the Commission.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 31(2) to define the "water authority" as the Penang Water Authority.

7. Clause 7 seeks to delete paragraph (f) of section 32(1) on the rationale that it should be correctly placed after section 36.

8. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 36A to replace and restate the provisions of paragraph (f) of section 32(1) which have been deleted by clause 7,

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 37(1) by deleting the words ", and of the auditor appointed by the Minister," as they are now considered unnecessary.

10. Clause 10 seeks to introduce a new section 38A to allow the Commission, with the approval of the Minister, to write off any bad debts.

11. Clause 11 seeks to delete section 49 as it is now considered unnecessary. Therefore, the provisions of the Statutory Bodies (Accounts and Annual Reports) Act 1980 will apply automatically to the Commission.

12. Clause 12 seeks to introduce a new section 58A to provide for the additional duties of the Commission. The Commission may, with the approval of the Minister, operate any port-related activities. In the exercise of such duties the Commission may expend such moneys as are necessary.

13. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 60(5) to increase the fine from a sum not exceeding five hundred dollars to a sum not exceeding one thousand ringgit.

14. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 64 whereby the privilege given to the vessels belonging to the British Government, of going alongside any landing area under the control of the Commission, is to be withdrawn.

15. Clause 15 seeks to introduce two new sections, namely, sections 70CA and 70cB. Section 70cA enables the Commission to authorise the Pilotage Committee to license any employee of a licensed operator to pilot vessels in a pilotage district subject to such terms and conditions as the Commission thinks fit. Section 70cB prohibits any vessel to be piloted in a pilotage district other than by the Commission pilot or a person licensed under section 70cA(l).

18. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 70j to allow the Pilotage Committee to submit to the Commission its findings after due inquiry on any person licensed under section 70cA(l). The Commission is then empowered to suspend or cancel the licence of such pilot or to impose such other punishment as it thinks fit.

19. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 70M(1) to extend its protection to the Pilotage Committee against any liability that may arise from any act or omission of any pilot licensed under section 70CA(1).

20. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 70o(2) to increase the fine from a sum not exceeding two thousand dollars to a sum not exceeding five thousand ringgit.

22. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 83(2) and (4) to increase the fine from a sum not exceeding five hundred dollars to a sum not exceeding two thousand ringgit.

23. Clause 23 seeks to introduce a new section 86A. It makes it an offence for any person to hinder or obstruct any member, officer or servant of the Commission in the performance or execution of his duty.

24. Clause 24 seeks to amend section 87(2) to increase the fine from a sum not exceeding one thousand dollars to a sum not exceeding two thousand ringgit.

25. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 88 to increase the fine from a sum not exceeding one thousand dollars to a sum not exceeding two thousand ringgit.

26. Clause 26 seeks to introduce a new section 88A. It makes it an offence for any person to make any statement which is false in any material particular in any document requested or authorised to be made under this Act. The penalty shall be a fine not exceeding five thousand ringgit.

27. Clause 27 seeks to introduce two new sections, namely, sections 89A and 89B. Section 89A enables the Commission to recover all expenses incurred for making good any loss or damage caused by any person who had not complied with any direction lawfully given under the Act. The expenses shall be recoverable as a civil debt. Section 89B provides for offences committed by a body corporate including its director, secretary or manager.

28. Clause 28 seeks to introduce new sections 90A and 90B. Section 90A empowers the General Manager or any person authorised by him to board any vessel within the port whenever he suspects that an offence has been or is about to be committed. Section 90B empowers the General Manager or any person authorised by him to give orders for scuttling the vessel in the event of any fire breaking out.

31. Clause 31 seeks to amend section 92c to widen its scope in order to bring within its ambit any offence committed by any Commission pilot or any person licensed under section 70cA(l).

32. Clause 32 seeks to amend section 92D in order to bring within its ambit any person licensed under section 70CA(l). It introduces

It further provides for the Court to make an order that upon being found guilty of the offence, the master, owner or agent of a vessel pays the pilotage dues and rates that are payable to the Commission.
