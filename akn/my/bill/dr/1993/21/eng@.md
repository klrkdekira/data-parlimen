A writing is a document.

Words printed, lithographed or photographed are documents.

A map, plan, graph or sketch is a document.

An inscription on wood, metal, stone or any other substance, material or thing is a document.

A drawing, painting, picture or caricature is a document.

A photograph or a negative is a document.

A tape recording of a telephonic communication, including a recording of such communication transmitted over distance, is a document.

A photographic or other visual recording, including a recording of a photographic or other visual transmission over a distance, is a document.

A matter recorded, stored, processed, retrieved or produced by a computer is a document.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Penal Code (F.M.S. Cap. 45).

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 24 to provide that irrespective of whether there is actual wrongful loss or gain, a person who does anything with the intention of causing wrongful gain or loss is said to do that "dishonestly".

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 29 to provide a definition of "document" that is consistent with an amendment effected to the definition of the same word in the Evidence Act 1950.

<!-- page 10 -->

4. Clause 4 and clause 5 seek to provide for the definition of the words "unlawful", "agent", "company", "director" and "officer" in the context of various provisions of the Penal Code.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend the scope of the offence of criminal misappropriation in section 403 to provide that a person could commit criminal misappropriation if he dishonestly causes any other person to dispose of property of another person. The amendment to section 403 also seeks to enhance the penalty for criminal misappropriation.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 404 to enhance the penalty for criminal misappropriation of property belonging to a deceased person and to effect the amendment consequential to the amendment made to section 403.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 405 to provide that where a person who is entrusted with property together with another, and who dishonestly misappropriates or dishonestly uses or dispose of that property, either of the two persons has committed criminal breach of trust.

8. Clauses 9, 10 and II seek to enhance the penalty in sections 406, 407 and 408.

9. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 409 to provide the punishment for criminal breach of trust committed by an agent as well as to provide a minimum sentence of imprisonment of two years,

10. Clause 13 seeks to provide for the new sections 409A and 409B. The new section 409A provides that it is not a defence for any offence of criminal misappropriation or criminal breach of trust to show that the appropriation was done openly or openly recorded and entered into books. Section 409e provides that where in any proceedings for the offence of criminal misappropriation or criminal breach of trust it is proved that a person had misappropriated any property, or used or disposed of the property in violation of a trust, or had suffered any person so to do, there is a presumption that he had acted dishonestly until the contrary is proved. This presumption is also applicable to offences of abetment and attempt in relation to criminal misappropriation or criminal breach of trust.

11. Clause 14 seeks to improve the definition of "cheating" in section 415 to provide that cheating is committed whether or not deception was the sole or main inducement.

12. Clause 15 seeks to enhance the penalty for cheating.
