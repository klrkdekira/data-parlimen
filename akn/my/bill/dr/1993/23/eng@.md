## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1993 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1993, the Supplementary Supply (1992 and 1993) Act 1993 and the Supplementary Supply (1993) Act 1993.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1786.]
