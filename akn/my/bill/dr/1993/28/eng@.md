## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 1994 and to appropriate that sum for the service of that year. [ ]

BE IT ENACTED by the Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

1. This Act may be cited as the Supply Act 1994.

2. The issue of a sum not exceeding twenty-two billion seven hundred and sixty-two million three hundred and fifty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty ringgit (RM22,762,353,730) from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 1994 is hereby authorised, and the said sum is appropriated for the purposes specified in . the Schedule.
