2. Clause 4 seeks to amend the definition of "approved scheme" to include the Employees Provident Fund. This amendment will be effective from the 1st June 1991.

3. Clau.se 5 seeks to increase the rebate allowed to an individual from RM60.00 to RM90.00; the rebate allowed in respect of a wife is increased from RM30.00 to RM50.00. This amendment will be effective from the year of assessment 1994.

4. Clause 6(a) seeks to increase contributions by an employer in respect of an employee's remuneration from 15 per cent to 16 per cent. Clause 6(b) seeks to introduce a new paragraph (g) into subsection 34(6) to allow deductions for expenditure incurred on the provision of public library facilities and in respect of contributions to libraries in schools and institutions of higher education. The amount that may be deducted is limited to RM 100,000.00. This amendment will be effective from the year of assessment 1994.

5. Clause 8 seeks to grant double deduction in respect of donations made during the period 1st November 1993 to 31si January 1994 to the Government low-cost housing fund managed by the Central Bank. This clause also seeks to include in the definition of "organisation" an organisation established for the conservation of the environment.

6. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 48 to provide for an increase in relief in respect of a child receiving education at a university or institution of higher education in Malaysia, to a maximum of four times the normal relief and in respect of a child receiving education in similar institutions outside Malaysia, to a maximum of twice ihe normal deduction. This clause also provides that in respect of a child who commenced such education outside Malaysia in the basis year for the year of assessment 1994 or in the basis year for any prior year of assessment, the maximum deduction of four times the normal deduction is continued. In respect of a resident of East Malaysia the maximum relief of four times the normal deduction in respect of a child who commenced schooling in West Malaysia in the basis year for the year of assessment 1994 or in the basis year for any prior year of assessment is continued. This amendment is effective from the year of assessment 1994.

8. Clause 12 seeks to amend the definition of "venture capital company" in section 60D to permit such a company to invest not more than 30 per cent of its funds in investments other than investments in a venture company. This amendment will be effective from the 1st January 1994.

This amendment will be effective from the year of assessment 1994.

## CHAPTER III

## AMENDMENTS TO THE PETROLEUM (INCOME TAX) ACT 1967

18. Clause 31 seeks to include the Malaysia-Thailand Joint Authority as a chargeable person.

19. Clause 34(b) seeks to reduce the rate of tax from 45 per cent to 40 per cent and sets out the rates of taxation in respect of income from the Joint Development Area.

20. Clause 35 seeks to enable the Minister to grant an exemption from tax to give effect to agreements relating to overlapping areas.

21. All other amendments are minor or consequential.

## CHAPTER

IV

## AMENDMENTS TO THE STAMP ACT 1949

22. Clause 39 seeks to amend paragraph 9(1)(c) by increasing the existing two dates of payment of compounded duties to four dates of payment. The period for payment without incurring further liabilities has been extended to fourteen days.

23. Clause 40 seeks to authorise the Minister to reduce or remit the further amount due as a result of default or delay in payment of compounded duties.

24. Clause 41 seeks to abolish stamp duty payable on a share certificate.

25. All other amendments are minor or consequential.
