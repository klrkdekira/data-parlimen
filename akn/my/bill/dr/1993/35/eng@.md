## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Excise Act 1976 ("the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 2(1) of the Act to include definitions for words used in the Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend the proviso to section 48 of the Act to enable the Director General to permit the use of forms submitted through an electronic data interchange.
