## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Widows' and Orphans' Pensions Ordinance of Sabah (the "Ordinance").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Ordinance to repeal the definition of "actuary" as it will no longer be necessary in view of the proposed amendment to section 7.

Clause 2 also seeks to provide definitions of "local pensions authority" and "Minister" which are used in the Ordinance.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of the Ordinance to specify the membership of the Board of Management of the Pund established under the Ordinance.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 7 to empower the Minister to appoint an actuary to carry out the valuation of the Fund under that section.

5. Clause 5 seeks to enable the Minister to revise the rates of pensions payable under the Ordinance, from time to time, on the recommendation of the Board.
