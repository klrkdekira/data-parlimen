## EXLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1994 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1994, the Supplementary Supply (1993 and 1994) Act 1994 and the Supplementary Supply (1994) Act 1994.

## IMPLICATIONS
