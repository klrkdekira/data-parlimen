## Schedule.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for preliminary matters and the date for the coming into force of the Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to introduce certain new definitions for words used in the Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 of the Act by deleting the proviso to subsection (4).

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 13 of the Act for purposes of authentication of the Board's common seal.

6. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new paragraph 24(2)(b) of the Act to enable moneys belonging to the Fund to be expended for the purpose of investment under this Act. It also seeks to amend paragraph 24(2)(d) to enable moneys belonging to the Fund to be expended for payment for services of any persons employed or appointed by the Board. This clause further allows for other expenses, cost or expenditure properly incurred or accepted by the Board in the execution of its powers and the carrying out of its duties.

<!-- page 20 -->

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section 26 to define the investment powers of the Board and the form of investments allowed.

8. Clause 7 seeks to introduce two new sections 26A and 26e giving power to the Board to invest in approved companies and in securities issued by the Government of Malaysia respectively.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 27 of the Act requiring the Board to seek the approval of the Minister in declaring dividends.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 30(2) of the Act to provide and clarify the position of the Chairman of the Board when no General Manager is appointed by the Minister. This clause also abolishes the post of Assistant General Managers.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 31 of the Act to increase the number of the members of the Disciplinary Committee and to empower the respective Committees to determine the conduct of its proceedings. This clause also seeks to provide for the period for appeal to the Board by any officer or servant against the decision of the Committee.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 33 of the Act to increase the number of the members of the Promotion Committee and to empower the Committee to determine the conduct of its proceedings. This clause also seeks to provide for the period for appeal to the Board by any officer or servant against the decision of the Committee.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend subsection 34(1) of the Act to empower the Board to appoint such number of Inspectors from any officer or servant of the Board who is appointed under subparagraph 30(3)(b).

14. Clause 13 and clause 14 seek to widen the jurisdiction and the definition of Inspector under the Act respectively.

15. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 37 of the Act by deleting the words "in the prescribed form" so as not to restrict the scope and form of statements made by persons examined under the A c t . to provide a

method to assess any amount of contribution based on information available in cases where the employer fails to keep or refuse to submit the necessary documents for assessment.

17. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 43 of the Act to clarify that each employer and employee shall be liable to pay monthly contributions based on the amount of wages due to the employee in respect of the month. It also seeks to introduce a new subsection 43(8) to enable any person not being an employer and not being an employee within the definition of the Act to contribute voluntarily.

<!-- page 21 -->

18. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 44 of the Act to safeguard the interest of any employee in the event an employer intends to remit to the Board any moneys being retirement benefits of the employee.

19. Clause 19 seeks to amend subsection 45(3) to facilitate the calculation of dividend in the event the dividend for a particular year has not been declared by the Board. It also seeks to introduce a new subsection 45(4) to provide a penalty against an employer who is convicted of an offence under subsection 45(3).

20. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 46 of the Act to further clarify that dividend on any contribution is a joint and several liability of director.

21. Clause 21 seeks to amend section 47 of the Act to provide that employers cannot deduct or recover from the wages or remuneration of the employee, the employer's contributions.

22. Clause 22 seeks to delete subsection 48(6) to be in line with the provisions of the Companies Act 1965 and the Bankruptcy Act

1967.

23. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 50 of the Act to facilitate the calculation of dividend to be based on the last dividend declared by the Board.

24. Clause 24 seeks to introduce a new section 50A to empower the Board to pay additional dividend to the member of the Fund who has made a withdrawal under subsection 54(1) of the Act.

25. Clause 25 seeks to delete the proviso to section 51.

26. Clause 26 seeks to introduce new subsections 54(1), (2), (3) and (4) on the authority for withdrawals from the Fund and new approvals for withdrawal from the Fund.

27. Clause 27 seeks to introduce a new section 55 to provide flexibility by enabling members of the Fund who has attained the age of fifty-five and above to make withdrawal on the amount standing to his credit in periodical payment or on the dividend only in lieu of a lump sum payment.

28. Clause 28 seeks to amend section 58 by introducing a new subsection (1A) to give the Board discretionary power to determine whether to pay or not to pay additional amount in the event of a member of the Fund dying and leaving no survivor.

<!-- page 22 -->

29. Clause 29 seeks to amend section 63 of the Act by introducing a new section providing for the courts' power to order an employer found guilty for certain offences under the Act to pay the amount of contribution and dividend thereon either in one single payment or in not more than six monthly instalments. It also provides for the courts' power to issue a warrant to levy the amount owed to the Board.

30. Clause 30 seeks to amend section 65 of the Act which provides that notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, all contributions payable are recoverable by the Board as a civil debt.

31. Clause 31 seeks to amend section 66 of the Act to enable the Board to recover arrears of contributions and dividend accrued for any period in any execution proceeding or sale in cases of bankruptcy or winding up of a company.

32. Clause 32 seeks to amend section 72 of the Act by inserting a new paragraph (c) to empower the Minister to declare any persons as an employer and an employee.

33. Clause 33 seeks to amend subparagraph 73(m)(iv) empowering the Board to make rules giving an opportunity for making representation under certain circumstances. It also seeks to introduce paragraph 73(n) giving the Board the power to make rules to provide for the procedure relating to financial and accounts of the Fund.

34. Clause 34 seeks to delete the Fourth Schedule.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
