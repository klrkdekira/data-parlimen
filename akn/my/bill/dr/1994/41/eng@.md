## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (P.M.S. Cap. 6) ("the Code") to provide for the abolition of preliminary inquiries in the Magistrate's Court for the purpose of committing an accused person for trial in the High Court, and trials with jury or trials with the aid of assessors.

2. Clause 2 seeks to delete section 8 of the Code whereby hearing in camera in a prelirainary inquiry is abolished in view of the fact that preliminary inquiries have also been abolished by this Bill.

3. Clause 5 seeks to delete Chapters XVII and XVIIA of the Code whereby provisions relating to preliminary inquiries are abolished. With the abolition of these provisions there is no necessity for a preliminary inquiry to be held in a Magistrate's Court for the purpose of committing an accused person to be tried in the High Court for offences triable in the High Court.

<!-- page 7 -->

4. Clause 8 provides for the transmission of cases to the High Court, without holding a preliminary inquiry under Chapter XVII of the Code, to be tried by such Court.

5. Clause 9 seeks to amend the heading of Chapter XX of the Code to delete the words "WITHOUT ASSESSORS". In view of the abolition of trials by jury or trials with the aid of assessors, there is no such special procedure applicable to the High Court.

6. Clause 11 seeks to delete Chapters XXI, XXII and XXIII of the Code whereby provisions relating to trials by jury or trials with aid of assessors in the High Court are abolished. With this amendment the trial before a High Court is the same as in a Magistrate's Court or Sessions Court.

7. Clau.se 21 seeks to amend section 281 of the Code in view of the creation of the Court of Appeal to provide that the report by the judge passing the sentence of death is to be forwarded to the Federal Court only after the Court of Appeal has determined the appeal.

8. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 288 of the Code to provide that for offences under the Penal Code relating to criminal misappropriation, criminal breach of trust and cheating, whipping shall be inflicted with a light cane as used for juvenile offenders.

9. Clause 38 seeks to delete Forms XXXII and XXXIX in the Second Schedule to the Code in view of the abolition of trials by jury or trial with the aid of assessors and preliminary inquiries.

10. Clause 39 seeks to provide that any preliminary inquiry, trial by jury or trial with the aid of assessors that has been commenced before or on the commencement of this Act shall be continued or concluded in all respects as if this Act had not been passed. Any preliminary inquiry or trial has been commenced when the accused has appeared before or has been brought before the Magistrate's Court or the High Court, as the case may be, and evidence has begun to be adduced in the inquiry or trial.

11. Other amendments to the Code are minor or consequential in nature.
