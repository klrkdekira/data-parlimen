## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Merchant Shipping Ordinance 1952 (the "Ordinance").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend certain definitions in section 2 of the Ordinance.

3. Clause4 seeks to introduce new sections 58A, 58B and 58c. Anew form of registry is introduced by the new section 58A, that is, a terminable certificate of registry for small ships. The new sections 58B and 58c provide for matters relating to the ownership of a ship and using any ship which has been granted a terminable certificate of registry as security for a loan or for other valuable consideration.

4. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 65K of the Ordinance to allow an appeal to be made against the decision of any person or body of persons delegated with the powers and duties of the Domestic Shipping Licensing Board.

5. Clause 7 seeks to delete subsection 65KA(4) since it is no longer considered necessary in view of the introduction of the new section

65T.

6. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 65u to empower the Minister to exempt any ship from the provisions of Part 11B of the Ordinance.

7. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 474 of the Ordinance to extend its requirements on the licensing of boats.

8. Clause 15 seeks to introduce a new section 483c to extend the provisions on manning in the Ordinance to a boat licensed under Part

## XIII.

9. Clause 16 seeks to introduce a new Part XIIIA, The new section 491B of the new Part requires any ship which is engaged or intends to engage in any of the activities stipulated therein in Malaysian waters to notify the Director of Marine of such activity. The Director of Marine may impose the fees or terms and conditions as he thinks fit on the activity. The new section 491c deals with the powers of the Director of Marine to carry out investigation of an offence under this new Part. The new section 49ID seeks to allow the Government to recover as a civil debt the cost of holding any ship or thing in its custody in the event of any person being found guilty of any offence under this new Part.

10. Clause 18 seeks to introduce new section 512A to empower the Director of Marine to compound any offence under the Ordinance.

11. Clauses 5,9,10,11,12,13 and 17 seek to make amendments to the Ordinance which are consequential or minor in nature.
