## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Price Control Act 1946 ("the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to include definitions for words used in the Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of the Act to empower the Minister to appoint the Price Controller, the Deputy Price Controller, Assistant Price Controllers and such other officers as may be necessary for the purposes of the Act.

4. Clause 4 seeks to re-enact the existing subsection 3(4) on the National Advisory Council for Consumers' Protection as a new section 3A.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 12(2) to delete the reference to the Chief Price Inspector. Similar amendments are made in clauses 6(a) and 8 whilst in clause 7 both the terms "Chief Price Inspector" as well as "Price Inspector" are deleted.

6. Clause 6(b) seeks to amend subsection 14(2) of the Act to enable the Price Controller to dispose off goods in cases where no proceedings have been instituted against any person and where the owner of such goods cannot be found, the matter shall be referred to a Magistrate for a decision as to the disposal of the goods. Where proceedings are in fact instituted, the question as to whether the goods are to be forfeited or released will be determined by the court before which the prosecution is held.

<!-- page 7 -->

7. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new section 1 7A to empower the Price Controller and the Deputy Price Controller to compound certain offences under the Act and the regulations.

8. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 23 of the Act to confer upon a Magistrate of the First Class the jurisdiction to try offences under the Act and the regulations and to impose the full punishment for such offences.

9. Clause 11 seeks to amend subsection 26(1) of the Act in relation lo those powers, duties and functions of the Price Controller under thc Act which cannot be delegated by him.

10. Clause 12 seeks to amend subsection 29(2) to enable the Minister to prescribe the offences that may be compounded, the amount of compound and the procedure to be followed in compounding offences.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
