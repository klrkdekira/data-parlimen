## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Tabung Angkatan Tentera Act 1973 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with the commencement of this Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to make general amendments to the Act. With these amendments, the Act will now be entitled Tabung Angkatan Tentera and Tabung Bekas Perajurit Act 1973.

4. Clause 3 seeks to correct errors in the national language text of the Act.

5. Clause 4 clearly provides that the change of name of the Act will not affect the reference to the Tabung Angkatan Tentera in any written law or in any instrument or document relating to it nor will it affect the rights, privileges, liabilities, duties or obligations of the Lembaga.

<!-- page 6 -->

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to introduce new definitions for purposes of clarity in relation to the proposed establishment of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit.

7. Clause 7 seeks to introduce new section 3A into the Act to provide for the establishment of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit. The objectives of this proposed new Tabung is to promote the socio- economic development and welfare of, and to provide other benefits for, the retiring personnel or retired personnel of the regular forces of Malaysia.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 4 of the Act. The amendment to subsection (1) is necessitated by the introduction of the new section

3A.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 5 of the Act consequent upon the proposed amendment made to subsection 4(1) by clause 8.

10. Clause 10 seeks to amend subsections 6(1) and 15(1) of the Act to provide for matters pertaining to the investment of the assets of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit.

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 22 of the Act. New paragraphs (aa) and (ga) seek to empower the Lembaga to specify the terms and conditions to be met in order for any retiring personnel or retired personnel to qualify for any benefits under the Act and to set the terms and conditions for granting any loan out of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit.

12. Other proposed amendments to the Act not specifically referred to are consequential or minor in nature.
