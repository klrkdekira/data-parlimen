## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to create a body corporate to be known as the "Small and Medium Industries Development Corporation" which will be charged with the responsibility of promoting the development of Malaysia's small and medium industries.

2. Part I deals with preliminary matters.

Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

Clause 2 seeks to define several terms used in this Bill.

3. Part II contains provisions relating to the Small and Medium Industries Development Corporation.

Clause 3 provides for the establishment of the Corporation.

<!-- page 21 -->

## DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION

Clause 4 seeks to provide power to the Minister to appoint members of the Corporation.

Clause 11 empowers the Minister to determine such allowances payable to members and alternate members of the Corporation.

4. Part III deals with functions and powers of the Corporation.

Clauses 12 and 13 list out respectively, the functions and powers of the Corporation.

To ensure that these powers and functions could be performed effectively clauses 14 to 17 accord the Corporation powers to borrow, delegate its functions and powers, engage and pay agents and technical advisers, and appoint committees.

5. Part IV contains financial provisions.

Clause 18 provides for the establishment of the Small and Medium Industries Development Fund.

Clause 19 specifies the expenses to be charged on the Fund.

Clause 20 imposes upon the Corporation the duty of conserving the Fund.

Clause 21 empowers the Corporation to invest.

Clause 22 relates to submission of accounts, audit and annual reports of the Corporation.

Clause 24 deals with the power of the Corporation to impose a surcharge on any past or present officer or servant of the Corporation.

6. Part V relates to administrative matters.

Clause 25 provides for the appointment of the Chief Executive Officer.

Clause 26 empowers the Minister to determine the remuneration payable to the Chief Executive Officer.

Clause 2 7 sets out the duties and responsibilities of the Chief Executive Officer.

Clause 28 contains provision for the appointment of such number of other officers and servants of the Corporation as the Corporation thinks desirable and necessary.

<!-- page 22 -->

Clause 29 seeks to establish a Disciplinary Board, prescribes its composition and sets out its jurisdiction and powers, including the power to delegate its disciplinary functions and powers to any committee of officers or servants of the Corporation to be known as a Disciplinary Committee.

7. Part VI contains general provisions.

Clause 32 seeks to empower the Minister to make regulations for the proper and effective carrying out of the purposes and provisions of this proposed Act.

Clause 33 requires the Corporation to furnish returns, reports, accounts and information to the Minister.

Clause 34 seeks to provide power to the Minister to give directions.
