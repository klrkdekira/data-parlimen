## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Control of Rent Act 1966 ("the Act").

1. Clause 1 contains the short title and seeks to empower the Minister to appoint the date of coming into force of the Act.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 1 of the Act to empower the State Authority to exempt any area within any local authority area from all or any of the provisions of the Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to introduce new sections 1A and 1B. Section 1A seeks to provide that the Act, unless sooner repealed, shall cease to be in operation at the expiration of five years after the coming into force of this section. The Act is preserved within the stated period to serve as an advanced notice of its repeal and to enable tenants of controlled premises to obtain alternative accomodation before the repeal. However, an earlier repeal is permissible in any State which is ready for the cessation of the Act. Hence by section 1B the Minister is

<!-- page 7 -->

empowered, after consultation with the State Authority, to appoint different dates for the cessation of this Act in any State or in such part or parts of the State within the five-year period.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 4 to provide an additional category of premises which is to be exempted from the operation of the Act.

5. Clause 5 seeks to substitute the mechanism for determining the fair rent of controlled premises in paragraph 5(2)(b) with a new mechanism which is based on the expected market price of the premises.

6. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section 8A to enable the landlord of a controlled premises to vary the fair rent of the premises.

7. Clause 7 seeks to introduce new subsections 12(2A) and (3A) and amend subsection (4). Subsection (2A) seeks to empower the Ruler or the Yang di-Pertua Negeri to appoint any of the persons specified in subsection (2) to perform the functions of the Chairman of the Rent Tribunal during the period in which the Chairman is unable to perform his functions. By subsection (3A), read with the amendment to subsection (4), the Ruler or the Yang di-Pertua Negeri is empowered to appoint a member of the Rent Tribunal from amongst the valuation officers in the public service or in the employment of any local authority.

8. Clause 8 seeks to enlarge the powers of the Rent Tribunal under section 13 of the Act. By an amendment to paragraph (l)(a), the Rent Tribunal is empowered to hear any application for the varying of fair rent under the new section 8A; and by the new paragraph ( l )( g ), the Rent Tribunal is empowered to hear and determine any application under section 20 or 24 of the Act.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 16 by substituting paragraph 16(1)( f ) of the Act. The new paragraph (1)(f) is essentially the existing paragraph (1)(f) but with a deletion of subparagraph (1)(f)(i). The paragraph deals with the recovery of possession of a controlled premises by an owner who requires the same for occupation by himself or any member of his family.

10. Clause 10 seeks to amend paragraph 18 (1)(c). By substituting for the word "three" the word "six" appearing therein, a landlord, in his written undertaking accompanying an application for recovery of possession of a controlled premises for purposes of development, is given a realistic time period from the date of possession of the controlled premises to commence work for the purpose of putting into effect the development.

<!-- page 8 -->

11. Clause 11 is a minor amendment to remove a typographical error in subsection 19(11) of the Act.

12. Clause 12 seeks to introduce a new section 24A to provide for the procedure in the service of notices under the Act.

13. Clause 13 deals with savings provision.

14. Other amendments which have not been specifically referred to are amendments which are minor and consequential in nature.
