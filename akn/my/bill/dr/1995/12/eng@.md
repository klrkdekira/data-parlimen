## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to create a body corporate to be known as the "Perbadanan Putrajaya" which will be charged with the responsibility of promoting and stimulating the development of Putrajaya, particularly as a Federal Government administrative centre.

2. Part I contains preliminary matters.

Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

Clause 2 contains the definitions of several terms used in this Bill.

3. Part II contains provisions relating to the Perbadanan Putrajaya.

Clause 3 provides for the establishment and objective of the Perbadanan, while clause 4 deals with its functions and powers which include powers to privatise its activities.

Clause 5 contains provisions on the membership of the Perbadanan.

Clause 9 seeks to empower the Minister to give directions to the Perbadanan.

Clause 10 contains a description of the area within which the Perbadanan will perform its functions.

<!-- page 17 -->

Clause 11 contains provisions which enable the Federal or State Government to assign to the Perbadanan other additional functions to be performed by the Perbadanan.

Clause 12 requires the Perbadanan to furnish returns, reports, accounts and information to the Minister and such other public authority as directed by the Minister.

4. Part III of the Bill contains provisions on the President, Chief Executive and officers and servants of the Perbadanan.

Clause 13 deals with the duties of the President of the Perbadanan.

Clause 14 contains provisions in respect of the chief executive officer of the Perbadanan.

Clause 15 seeks to empower the Perbadanan to appoint its officers and servants.

Clauses 16 and 17 seek to empower the Perbadanan, with the approval of the Minister, to make regulations on the conditions of service and the discipline of its officers and servants.

5. Part IV contains financial provisions.

Clause 20 provides for the establishment of a Fund and clause 21 seeks to impose upon the Perbadanan the duty of conserving such Fund.

Clause 22 specifies the expenditure which may be charged on the Fund.

6. Part V deals with the other powers and functions of the Perbadanan.

Clause 25 allows the Perbadanan to delegate certain functions, powers and duties to the Chief Executive.

Clause 26 provides for the borrowing of money by the Perbadanan and clause 27 seeks to enable the Perbadanan to invest its assets.

The Perbadanan is also allowed to establish corporations under clause 29.

7. Part VI contains general provisions.

Clause 31 seeks to exempt the Perbadanan from the payment of stamp duties.

Clause 32 prohibits a member, officer or servant of the Perbadanan from disclosing information obtained in the course of his duties.

<!-- page 18 -->

Clause 33 deals with the power of the Perbadanan to make regulations.

Clause 35 contains saving provision relating to structure plans prepared and approved by the State Planning Committee.
