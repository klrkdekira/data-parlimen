## TABUNG BEKAS PERAJURIT (AMENDMENT)

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Tabung Angkatan Tentera and Tabung Bekas Perajurit Act 1973 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title of this Bill.

3. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by inserting a definition of the word "Tabung". This is appropriate as the Tabung Bekas Perajurit has been wound up and a single fund now exists.

4. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 3 of the Act by substituting for subsection 3(1) a new subsection (1) and introducing new subsections (1A), (3) and (4).

The new subsection 3(1) provides for the establishment of a single fund known as the "Tabung Angkatan Tentera" (hereinafter referred to as the "Tabung") which is to replace the "Tabung Angkatan Tentera and Tabung Bekas Perajurit". This single fund has primary purposes and ancillary purposes. The primary purposes of the Tabung preserve the existing purposes of the Tabung Angkatan Tentera which is the provision of superannuation and other benefits to members of the regular forces who are contributors to the Tabung. The ancillary purposes are for the promotion of the socio-economic development and welfare of retiring and retired personnel of the regular forces of Malaysia.

The new subsection (1A) provides for the sources of funding of the Tabung. It includes a new source, which is an annual grant from the Federal Consolidated Fund. This source of funding is intended to be applied towards all activities related to the ancillary purposes of the Tabung including, for defraying administrative and other expenses incurred by the Lembaga or any other body charged with the responsibility of undertaking the activities for the ancillary purposes of the Tabung.

The new subsection (4) seeks to provide that the Lembaga may only utilise the other monies from the Tabung towards the ancillary purposes of the Tabung after it has first applied the monies from the annual grant, and where there are sufficient funds for the Lembaga to discharge its obligations in respect of its primary purposes, including a declaration of an annual rate of profit for the superannuation fund.

5. Clause 8 seeks to delete section 3A of the Act. Section 3A of the Act which established the Tabung Bekas Perajurit is no longer necessary since its purposes are now being transferred to the Tabung Angkatan Tentera.

<!-- page 8 -->

6. Clause 13 provides for transfer of property rights and liabilities, saving and transitional provisions occasioned by the winding-up of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit.

7. Other proposed amendments to the Act not specifically referred to are consequential or minor in nature and are necessitated by the winding-up of the Tabung Bekas Perajurit.
