## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Port Authorities Act 1963 ("the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsections 16A(1) and (3) of the Act to reflect the actual citation of the Ports (Privatisation) Act. It also seeks to introduce a new subsection 2A to section 16A to allow an avenue for appeal with regard to structure of charges.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 47 of the Act to insert a new subsection (1A) to enable the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to amend the name of any port in the First Schedule to the Act.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1874.]
