## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka Act 1959 ("the Act") and to extend the operation of the Act to the States of Sabah and Sarawak and the Federal Territory of Labuan.

2. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 1(2) of the Act to provide for the application of the Act throughout Malaysia. With this, paragraph

(d) of clause 5 seeks to delete the definition of "West Malaysia" in section 2 of the Act.

3. Clause 4 seeks to make general amendment in the national language text of the Act by deleting the word "Barat" in subsection 3(1) and by substituting the words "penjawat" and "penjawat-penjawat" with the words "pengkhidmat" and "pengkhidmat-pengkhidmat" respectively.

4. Clause 5 seeks to introduce the definitions of "Director General" and "disciplinary committee". It also seeks to substitute the word "pelajaran" with the word "pendidikan" in the definition of "Menteri".

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 4 of the Act relating to the constitution of the Board. It seeks to provide for new appointment of members and appointment of representatives to certain members.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 5 of the Act. It seeks, among other things, to introduce two new objects of the Board namely, to encourage the proper usage of the national language and to promote its wider use.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 6 of the Act by deleting subsection (2).

8. Clause 9 seeks to introduce new sections 6A and 6fi. The new section 6A seeks to provide that the Board shall be the sole co- ordinating authority pertaining to composing, devising and standardising of terminologies in the national language. This new role is necessary in view of the need for uniformity in the development of the national language. The new section 65 empowers the Board to delegate, in writing, any of its duties or powers except the powers under sections 18A, 18B, 19 and 27 to the Director General or the officers or servants of the Board.

<!-- page 16 -->

9. Clause 10 seeks to substitute section 7 of the Act. The new section 7 seeks to set out the powers of the Board.

10. Clause 11 seeks to introduce new section 7A which empowers the Board to transfer or dispose of any of its activity or duty in relation to the carrying out of its object under paragraph 5(iii) of the Act to any person or body subject to such terms and conditions as the Board may impose.

11. Clause 12 seeks to substitute section 11 of the Act. The new section 11 makes provisions for the Board to appoint one or more committees to assist it in the performance of its duties and the exercise of its powers. It also sets out the functions of the committees.

12. Clause 13 seeks to introduce new sections 1 1A and 11B. The new section 1 1A seeks to make provisions for the committee to appoint one or more sub-committees to assist it in the performance of its duties and the exercise of its powers. It also sets out the functions of the sub-committee. The new section 1 1B seeks to provide for the payment of remuneration or allowance to any person other than a member, officer or servant of the Board who is invited to attend any meeting of the Board, committee or sub-committee.

13. Clause 14 seeks to add a new heading to Part III of the Act pertaining to discipline.

14. Clause 15 seeks to introduce a new section 16A which seeks to set out the duties of the Director General.

15. Clause 16 seeks to delete matters relating to discipline from the scope of section 17 of the Act as such matters are dealt with in the proposed new sections 1 8A and 18B.

16. Clause 17 seeks to introduce new sections 1 8A and 18B. The new section ISA establishes disciplinary committees of the Board in respect of the Director General and its officers and servants and sets out their duties, powers and functions. The new section 18B empowers the Board to make disciplinary regulations.

17. Clause 18 seeks to add a new paragraph (e) to section 22 of the Act to enable the Board to make loans to its officers and servants for any purpose it may approve.

18. Clause 19 seeks to introduce new sections 24A, 24B, 24c and 24D which relate to the imposition of surcharge on any person who is or was in the employment of the Board.

19. Clause 20 seeks to introduce a new section 25A which seeks to make the members, the Director General, officers and servants of the Board public servants within the meaning of the Penal Code.
