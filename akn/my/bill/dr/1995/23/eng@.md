## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The proposed Town Planners Act 1995 ("the Act") seeks to establish a new body corporate by the name of "Board of Town Planners" to be responsible for the registration of Town Planners and related matters.

<!-- page 35 -->

## PART I

2. Part I contains preliminary matters. Clause 1 deals with the short title and commencement of the Act. Clause 2 defines certain words and expressions used in the Act.

## PART II

3. Part II contains provisions relating to the Board of Town Planners ("the Board"). Clause 3 establishes the Board. Clauses 4 to 7 provide for matters relating to the membership of the Board, termination and revocation of appointment and filling of vacancy. Clause 8 spells out the functions of the Board. Clause 9 empowers the Board to borrow money. The Board is allowed to appoint committees to assist it in clause 10.

## PART III

4. Part III contains provisions pertaining to the registration of Town Planners. Clause 11 deals with the keeping and maintenance of a Register of registered Town Planners and registered Graduate Town Planners. Clause 12 provides for the appointment of a Registrar of Town Planners and his duties. Clause 13 sets out the entitlement of a registered Town Planner. Restrictions on unregistered Town Planners and registered Graduate Town Planners are spelt out in clause 14. Clause 15 prescribes the qualification for registration as a registered Graduate Town Planner and registered Town Planner. Clauses 16 and 17 provide for the manner of application for registration and the form of the certificate of registration. Clause 18 deals with the temporary registration of foreign Town Planners. A duty is imposed on a registered Town Planner, registered Graduate Town Planner and foreign Town Planner to notify the Registrar of any change of address in clause 19.

## Part IV

5. Part IV contains provisions dealing with town planning practice by bodies corporate. Clause 20 describes the composition of the board of directors and shareholders of a body corporate which may practice as a Town Planner. Clause 21 among other things, allows a body corporate practising as a Town Planner to use the title "registered Town Planner". Clause 22 deals with the manner of application for permit. Clause 23 provides for the form of the permit. Under clause 24 the body corporate is to notify the Board of any change in the composition of its board of directors or shareholders and change of its business address. The Board may cancel a permit under clause 25 but only after a hearing of the grounds of complaint has been conducted and right to be heard has been accorded as required under clause 26. Clause 27 requires a body corporate to cease its practice as a Town

<!-- page 36 -->

Planner once the Board cancels its permit. Clause 28 pertains to the surrender of the permit upon its cancellation. The Board is empowered to issue fresh permits under clause 29. Matters under clause 30 are unappealable.

## PART V

6. Part V contains provisions pertaining to the cancellation of registration, suspension from practice, removal from and reinstatement into the Register.

Clause 31 provides for the appointment of an Investigation Committee and a Disciplinary Committee. Clause 32 relates to suspension of registration pending investigation. Clause 33 provides for orders that may be made by the Disciplinary Committee under the circumstances stated in clause 34. Clause 35 spells out the circumstances under which the registration of a registered Town Planner or registered Graduate Town Planner is null and void. Matters pertaining to the removal from the Register and the return of the certificate of registration to the Board upon removal are contained in clauses 36 and 37. Clause 38 on the other hand provides for reinstatement into the Register.

## PART VI

7. Part VI contains provisions on appeal.

Clause 39 establishes the Appeal Board. The tenure of office and allowance of members of the Appeal Board are regulated under clause 40. Clause 41 seeks to enable the Appeal Board to regulate its own procedure. Clauses 42 and 43 relate to appealable matters and procedure of appeal.

## PART VII

8. Part VII contains provisions on offences.

Clause 44 provides for certain offences under the Act and its penalty while clause 45 provides for the general penalty. Clause 46 deals with offences committed by a body corporate. Clauses 47 and 48 deal with conduct of prosecution and civil proceedings respectively.

## PART VIII

9. Part VIII contains general provisions.

Clause 49 seeks to protect acts or omission done in good faith. Clause 50 empowers the Board to make rules with the approval of the Minister. Clause 51 seeks to provide that every registered Town

<!-- page 37 -->

Planner, registered Graduate Town Planner, foreign Town Planner and body corporate practising as a Town Planner shall be subject to this Act. Clause 52 is a savings provision for the protection of the Federal Government or the Government of a State.
