## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967, the Real Property Gains Tax Act 1976, the Petroleum (Income Tax) Act 1967, the Stamp Act 1949, the Labuan Offshore Business Activity Tax Act 1990 and the Goods Vehicle Levy Act 1983.

## CHAPTER II

## AMENDMENTS TO THE INCOME TAX ACT 1967

2. Clause 4 relates to a foreign fund management company. A foreign fund management company is a company incorporated in Malaysia and

<!-- page 23 -->

licensed under the Securities Industry Act 1983. A wholly foreign owned fund management company will manage funds on behalf of foreign investors while a fund management company, which equity is in majority foreign owned, will manage funds on behalf of foreign and local investors. This clause seeks to introduce a new paragraph

13. Clause 18 seeks to introduce a new Schedule 7B to provide investment allowance to an approved service project. An approved service project is defined as a project in the service sector in relation to transportation, communications, utilities or any other sub-sector as approved by the Minister. The investment allowance is provided in respect of capital expenditure incurred within five years from the date from which the approval is granted.
