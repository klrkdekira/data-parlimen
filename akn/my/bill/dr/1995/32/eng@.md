## A BILL

### intituled

An Act to amend the Strata Titles Act 1985. [ ]

WHEREAS it is expedient for the purpose only of ensuring uniformity of law and policy to amend the Strata Titles Act 1985:

NOW, THEREFORE, pursuant to Clause (4) of Article 76 of the Federal Constitution, BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Strata Titles Act 1985.

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title of the Bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to insert the definition of "special resolution" and to amend the definition of "unanimous resolution" in section 4 in order to facilitate the management of the subdivided building.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 6(1) to allow a building having only one storey in the same strata scheme to be subdivided into parcels to be held under strata titles or into accessory parcels.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 7 by inserting two new subsections, namely subsections (2) and (3), to allow the proprietor of abuilding on any alienated land which has been duly surveyed held under qualified title or where the certificate of fitness for occupation has yet to be issued to submit an application to the Land Office for subdivision thereof into parcels.

<!-- page 6 -->

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 9(1) by inserting the new paragraphs (k) and (l) to provide for the conditions for the approval of an application for subdivision of any building on a land held under qualified title or where the certificate of fitness for occupation has not been issued.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend subparagraph I0(1)(b)(i) to include any approved amendments to the original building plans as part of the building plans. The new paragraph (cb) seeks to require the proprietor of a land held under qualified title to submit three copies of the certified plan of the land for the application for subdivision of the building. Subsection 10(8) has been amended to outline the necessary actions to be taken by a Land Administrator on receiving an application for subdivision of building. It also seeks to introduce a further proviso to subsection 10(10) to outline the subsequent actions to be carried out by the Land Administrator in respect of any building on a land held under qualified title or where the certificate of fitness for occupation has not been issued.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend subsection 10A(1) to introduce a proviso to the effect that a building having only one storey shall not be included in an application for a provisional strata title or titles for the provisional block or blocks.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection 13(2) by inserting a new paragraph

(c) to require that a certified strata plan shall contain a schedule showing the approved share units of each parcel and the total share units of all the parcels in the certified strata plan approved by the Director of Survey. Clause 8 further seeks to introduce a new subsection (5) to provide for the matters to be contained in a certified plan in respect of a provisional block.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend Form 1 in the First Schedule by inserting subparagraph (ea) which is necessitated by the introduction of paragraph 10(l)(cb).

11. Clause 10 seeks to delete paragraph 18 in the Second Schedule as the definition of "special resolution" is now proposed to be introduced in section 4 of the Act.
