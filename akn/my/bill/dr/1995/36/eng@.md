## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Securities Industry (Central Depositories) Act 1991 (the "Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with preliminary matters, including the Minister's power to appoint the date for the coming into force of this Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 2(1) of the Act to amend certain definitions and to introduce certain new definition of certain words used in the Act, as follows:

(a) paragraph (a) provides a definition of "Central Bank of Malaysia" for ease of reference to the Central Bank of Malaysia established under the Central Bank of Malaysia Act 1958;

(b) paragraph (b) provides a definition of "Commission" for ease of reference to the Securities Commission established under the Securities Commission Act 1993;

(c) paragraph (c) provides a new definition of "Investigating Officer" for ease of reference to Investigating Officers of the Securities Commission appointed under the Securities Commission Act 1993;

(d) paragraph (d) deletes the definition of "Registrar" consequential upon the transfer of the powers of regulation of the central depository system from the Registrar of Companies to the Commission;

(e) paragraph (e) amends the definition of "rules" to expand and clarify the definition thereof; and

(f) paragraph (f) amends the definition of "user" to allow for the rules of the central depository to specify other categories of users of the central depository system.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 by prohibiting any person from providing the services of a central depository unless that person is a central depository, the Central Bank of Malaysia or is exempted by the Minister under section 62A.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 to clarify that a central depository shall be incorporated as a company under the Companies Act 1965.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend subparagraph 6(2)(c)(i) of the Act, This is to substitute the Commission for the Capital Issues Committee, which no longer exists.

7. Clause 6 seeks to replace the existing section 7 with a new section that outlines the procedure to amend the rules of a central depository.

<!-- page 19 -->

## (AMENDMENT)

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 10 by substituting the Registrar with the Commission. This amendment, also found in other sections is to effect a rationalised structure for the Commission to be the single supervisory authority over the central depository,

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 13 to enable the central depository to prescribe in its rules the purposes of the appointment of authorised depository agents. The clause also substitutes for the Registrar the Commission.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 14 to provide for the power of the Commission to exempt the stock exchange from having to publish prescription notices in three daily national newspapers in appropriate circumstances. This is to reduce the costs of prescribing securities into the central depository system where further notification is superfluous and wasteful. For example, in the case of new listing of securities which are prescribed into the central depository, the listing rules of the stock exchange and the Commission's Guidelines For The New Issue Of Securities And The Valuation Of Public Limited Companies would have required the issuer to publish the fact of the prescription in its prospectus and in the newspapers. In such an event, the shareholders of the issuer would also be notified at the company's general meeting convened for the purposes of listing of the securities of the company on the stock exchange. Therefore to require further notification by the stock exchange would only increase the cost of prescribing the securities into the central depository system.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 15. This is consequential to the amendment of section 14.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 17 to enable a prescribed security that is deposited with the central depository to be capable of being utilised to settle a transaction on the stock market of a stock exchange if the issuer of the security registers the transfer of the security in favour of the central depository and delivers the appropriate certificate to the central depository.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 23, consequential to the amendment of section 17.

14. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 24 to substitute for the Minister the Commission.

15. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 26 to place the duty to issue statements which is an operational matter, within the ambit of the rules of the central depository.

16. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 28 by substituting for the Registrar the Commission.

<!-- page 20 -->

17. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 34 by substituting for the Registrar the Commission.

18. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 35 which deals with the rights and obligations of a depository subject to a lien over unpaid securities in favour of authorised depository agents. Subsection (3) states that any dealings over unpaid securities shall be governed by the rules of the stock exchange.

19. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 41 as a consequence to the amendment of section 35. The Minister is given the power to determine the rights and obligations arising from securities in suspense.

20. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 45 by substituting for the Minister and the Registrar, the Commission.

21. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 46 to enable the central depository to regulate access to its computer system, which is an operational matter, through the rules of the central depository. However, the Minister may regulate the extent of the access to the system under paragraph 46(3)(a) through regulations made under paragraph 63(2)(r).

22. Clause 21 seeks to amend section 49 by increasing the penalty for furnishing false and misleading statement from three years' imprisonment to ten years' imprisonment. This is to streamline the penalty in securities laws for false and misleading statement.

23. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 51 by increasing the penalty in the general penalty provision.

24. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 52 by substituting for the Registrar the Commission.

25. Clause 24 seeks to amend section 53 by substituting for the Registrar the Commission. This section also increases the penalty for the contravention of this section.

30. Clause 29 seeks to amend section 58 by substituting for the Registrar the Commission. This section also increases the penalty for the contravention of this section.

Clause 30 seeks to amend section 59 by limiting the type of records that a central depository is required to keep to only records and communications in relation to a depositor's account which is sufficient to explain the transactions and operations of the central depository and authorised depository agents. The amendment is to reduce the costs of storing the records by the central depository.
