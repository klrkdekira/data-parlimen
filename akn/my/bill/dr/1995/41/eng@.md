## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Passports Act 1966 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with preliminary matters, including the Minister's power to appoint the date for the coming into force of this Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 1A of the Act by deleting certain definitions and introducing certain new definitions.

<!-- page 11 -->

4. Clauses 3 and 5 seek to amend sections 2(1) and 9 of the Act respectively by substituting for the words "an alien" the words "a non-citizen".

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 6(3) of the Act to enhance the penalty for the failure of the master of a vessel to comply with the Director General's order under subsection 5(1) of the Act to remove a person entering Malaysia contrary to the Act from Malaysia.

6. Clause 6 seeks to substitute section 10 of the Act to clarify the powers of arrest and investigation under the Act.

7. Clause 7 seeks to introduce new sections 10A, 10B, 10C, 10D, 10E, 10F and 10G to strengthen the powers of enforcement under the Act.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection 12(1) of the Act to clarify the offences under the Act.

9. Clause 9 seeks to introduce new sections 12A, 12B, 12C and 12D. Section 12A deals with offences relating to the procurement of passports, internal travel documents, or endorsements or visas on passports, for other persons. Section 12B deals with the offence of making a false document to be used as a passport, whether by the person who made it or by any other person. Section 12C empowers the Director General of Immigration to reward persons for services rendered in connection with the detection and prosecution of offences under the Act. Section 12D provides that the Minister may give the Director General of Immigration directions of a general or specific nature not inconsistent with the Act and that the Director General shall give effect to all such directions.

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1894.]
