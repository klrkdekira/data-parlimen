## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1995 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1995 and the Supplementary Supply (1994 and 1995) Act 1995.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1892.]
