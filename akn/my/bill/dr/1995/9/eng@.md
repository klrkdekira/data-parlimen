## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Co-operative Societies Act 1993.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 3(2) in consequence of the introduction of the new sections 7lA, 71B and 71c under clause 5.

3. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new section 18A to provide additional powers to the Registrar-General to direct a registered society to convene a general meeting, within a period to be specified by him which is not less than three months from the date of the notification given by him, for the purpose of amending any of its by-laws which is ambiguous or doubtful in interpretation, and is likely to mislead or conflicts with or is inconsistent with the Act or any regulations made thereunder or if the amendment is necessary or desirable in the interest of the registered society. In the event that the registered society fails to comply with such directive any amendment made to the by-laws by the Registrar-General shall be deemed to be a registered amendment under section 18.

4. Clause 4 seeks to substitute for the words "saham perdagangan" the words "stok perniagaan" in paragraph 59(4)(d) of the national language text.

5. Clause 5 seeks to introduce three new sections namely, sections 7lA, 71B and71c. Section 7lA makes provision whereby a registered society may be dissolved with the view to its re-organisation as a company. This is available only in certain specific circumstances.

<!-- page 11 -->

In those circumstances as provided for under subsection 7lA(l), the Registrar-General shall notify or cause to be notified all members of the registered society of the proposal to dissolve and re-organise the registered society. The notification shall specify the matters stipulated in subsection 71A(6). Upon receipt of all the reply forms returned by the members who have been duly notified by ordinary post or by publication in the local newspapers, as the case may be, the Registrar-General shall, on being satisfied that three-fourths of the members have assented to the dissolution and re-organisation of the registered society, make an order for the revocation of the registration of the society. The order shall be conditional upon the incorporation of the successor company or, in the case of an existing company, the completion of any act necessary to prepare it to undertake the activities under paragraph 75(1)(1), and all the approvals, consents or endorsements referred to in paragraph 71A(6)(h) having been obtained.

The new section 71B provides for the distribution of the assets of the registered society to any member who has not assented to the dissolution and re-organisation of the registered society as a company.

The new section 71c is to facilitate the transfer of the assets of the registered society to the successor company by operation of law.

6. Clauses 6 and 7 seek to make minor consequential amendments to sections 73 and 74 necessitated by the introduction of the new section 7lA.

7. Clause 8 seeks to make a consequential amendment to section 81 to allow an aggrieved person to appeal to the Minister in respect of an order of the Registrar-General made under the new section 71B.

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 86(2) by introducing a new paragraph (q) to provide for matters relating to the new sections 7lA, 7IB and 71c.

9. Clause 10 seeks to amend paragraph 91(l)(g) of the principal Act by substituting it with a new paragraph to make it an offence if a registered society or any person does or causes to be done any act or thing prohibited by, or fails to comply with, the requirements of the Act.
