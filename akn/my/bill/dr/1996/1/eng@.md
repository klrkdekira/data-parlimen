## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to create a council to be known as the "National Council on Higher Education" and to provide for its functions relating to higher education in Malaysia.

2. Part I deals with preliminary matters.

Clause 1 contains the short title and provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

Clause 2 defines certain words and expressions used in this Bill.

3. Part II contains provisions relating to the Nationai Council on Higher Education ("the Council").

Clause 3 establishes the Council.

Clause 4 seeks to provide for the membership of the Council.

Clause 5 requires the Secretary General of the Ministry of Education to chair the meeting of the Council in the absence of the Chairman.

Clause 6 deals with the appointment and duration of appointment of the members of the Council under paragraphs 4(g), (h) and (i).

<!-- page 10 -->

Clause 7 spells out the circumstances under which a person is disqualified from being a member of the Council.

In clause 8, a member appointed under paragraph 4( g ), (h) or

(i) shall cease to be a member if he fails to attend three consecutive meetings of the Council without leave of the Chairman of the Council or if his appointment is revoked.

Clause 9 seeks to empower the Minister to appoint another person in the event of a vacancy among the members of the Council appointed under paragraph 4( g ), (h) or (i).

A member of the Council appointed under paragraph 4(i) may resign as a member of the Council by giving a month's notice in writing to the Minister under clause 10.

Clause 11 deals with the termination of appointment of a member of Ihe Council appointed under paragraph 4(g), (h) or (i).

Clause 12 seeks to provide for the functions of the Council. The primary role of the Council is to plan, formulate and determine national policies and strategies and set guidelines on matters pertaining to higher education in Malaysia as enumerated in paragraphs (l)(a) to (k).

4. Part III contains general provisions.

Clause 13 seeks to empower the Council to establish committees to assist it in discharging its functions under the proposed Act.

Clause 14 seeks to provide that the Council shall determine its own procedure.

Clause 15 deals with the meetings of the Council.

Clause 16 seeks to require a member who has any beneficial interest in any matter under consideration by the Council to disclose such interest to the Chairman of the Council.

Clause 17 seeks to require the Council to cause minutes of all its meetings to be maintained and kept in a proper form.

Clause 18 provides for the payment of allowances to the members of the Council or their representatives for attending meetings of the Council.

Clause 19 seeks to provide grants-in-aid to the Council to enable it to carry out its functions under the proposed Act.

<!-- page 11 -->

Clause 20 seeks to establish a Secretariat to assist the Council in carrying out its functions.
