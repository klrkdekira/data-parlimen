## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Federal Constitution.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend Article 46 of the Federal Constitution to provide for an increase in the membership of the House of Representatives from the State of Sarawak.

3. Clause 3 provides that Article 46 of the Federal Constitution, as amended, shall not affect the composition of the House of Representatives or any election to that House until the dissolution of Parliament occurring on or after the date of the coming into force of the Order made under section 12 of the Thirteenth Schedule to the Federal Constitution following the review undertaken pursuant to the said Clause (2) of Article 113 and the said Thirteenth Schedule.
