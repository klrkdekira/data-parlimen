21. The principal Act is amended by inserting, after the

## Schedule.

Sixth Schedule, the following Schedule:

## SEVENTH SCHEDULE

(Section 26C)

## THE PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO A CORPORATION

## ESTABLISHED BY THE BOARD

1. Duty of the Board to make regulations in respect of a corporation.

The Board shall, on or before the date on which a corporation is established, make regulations in respect of the corporation defining—

(a) the purposes and objects for which the corporation is established;

(b) the rights, powers, duties and functions of the corporation;

(c) the system of management of the corporation; and

(d) the relationship between the corporation and the Board and the right of control over the corporation.

2. Scope of corporation.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed to authorize the Board to establish any corporation for any purpose or object more extensive in scope than the purposes or objects for which the Board was constituted or as conferring on any corporation any

<!-- page 9 -->

right, duty, power or function which is not within the rights, duties, powers or functions of the Board under this Act.

3. Binding effect of such regulations.

Subject to the provisions of this Act, and of any regulations made under section 71, regulations made under paragraph 1 shall be binding on the corporation in respect of which they were made and shall have effect for all purposes under this Act.

4. Register of corporation.

The Board shall keep a register in the prescribed form of all corporations and such register together with copies of all regulations made under paragraph 1 shall be open to public inspection at such places and at such times as the Board may determine.

5. Winding-up.

(1) The Board may by order published in the Gazette direct that any corporation be wound up and dissolved.

(2) Upon the dissolution of any corporation under this paragraph, the assets of the corporation, after payment of all liabilities, shall be transferred to and vested in the Board.

6. Legal character of a corporation.

Every corporation shall be a body corporate by such name as the Board shall give to such corporation and shall have perpetual succession and a common seal and may sue and be sued in such name, and for the purpose of effecting the purposes and objects for which it was established, may enter into contracts and hold and deal in or with any movable or immovable property and may do all other matters and things incidental or appertaining to a body corporate not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act and subject to such restrictions or limitations as may be prescribed by the Board.

7. Common seal of a corporation.

(1) Every corporation shall have a common seal, which shall bear such device as the corporation, with the approval of the Board, may approve, and the seal may from time to time be broken, changed, altered and made anew by the corporation, with approval of the Board.

<!-- page 10 -->

(2) Until a seal is provided by the corporation under this paragraph, the common seal of the Employees Provident Fund Board may be used as the common seal of the corporation.

(3) The common seal shall be kept in the custody of, and shall be authenticated by, any person as the corporation may direct.

(4) All deeds, documents and other instruments purporting to be sealed with the common seal of the corporation and authenticated by the person in whose custody of the common seal is kept shall, until the contrary is proved, be deemed to have been validly executed.".

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title and the date of commencement of the Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the Act by substituting the words "General Manager" with the words "chief executive officer". It also seeks to delete the word "Deputy General Manager".

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by providing a definition of the words "chief executive officer".

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend paragraph 26A(2)(c) to empower the Minister to vary any limitation imposed on the total amount of the borrowings of the approved company under the said paragraph.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 26B(3) by deleting paragraph

(d). With this amendment, bonds having a maturity period of not less than two years would no longer be regarded as securities issued by the Government.

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section 26c to empower the Board to establish a corporation or a company with the special approval of the Minister.

8. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new Part IIIA in order to allow members of the Fund to invest the amount standing to their credit in fund management institutions approved by the Minister.

<!-- page 11 -->

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 30 to provide flexibility to the Board to determine the designation of the chief executive officer and to allow the Board to appoint such number of other oficers or servants as it deems appropriate.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 31 to establish a Disciplinary Committee in respect of officers and servants of the Board.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 33 to establish a Promotion Committee in respect of officers and servants of the Board.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend the proviso to subsection 45(3) to provide that the rate of last dividend declared shall apply for the purpose of calculating dividend for that year where the rate of dividend has not been declared by the Board.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 50 to clarify that dividend shall only be payable on any amount standing to the credit of a member and to delete the proviso to subsection (2) and subsection

(5).

14. Clause 13 seeks to amend subsection 54(2) to limit the number of elections by a member who has made full withdrawal to recontribute and to make applicable the provisions of section 54B in determining the date of withdrawal of payments.

15. Clause 14 seeks to introduce new sections 54A and 54B. Section 54A seeks to provide that the payment of dividend shall be in such manner as determined by the Board from time to time. Section 54B seeks to clarify the determination of the date of withdrawal for payment made by a warrant and for payment made in a manner other than by a warrant.

16. Clause 15 seeks to introduce a new section 59A to provide that the court shall order such person who is found guilty of an offence under subsection 59 (b) to produce any declaration or document required by the Act to be furnished or produced.

17. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 71 to empower the Minister to prescribe fees or charges which shall be paid to the Board in respect of services provided by the Board and to make regulations in respect of investment under Part IIIA.

18. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 73 to empower the Board to make rules in respect of investment under Part IIIA.

19. Clause 18 seeks to amend paragraph (5) of the First Schedule so as to exclude persons detained in drug rehabilitation centres from the definition of employees under the Act and, to amend paragraphs (6), (7) and (8) of the First Schedule as references to the Republic of Singapore are no longer necessary.

<!-- page 12 -->

20. Clause 19 seeks to amend the Fifth Schedule to provide that the amount invested under the new Part IIIA of the Act shall not be included as part of the credit of a member for the purpose of calculating additional amount in the event of death.

21. Clause 20 seeks to amend the Sixth Schedule to provide that the amount invested under the new Part IIIA of the Act shall not be included as part of the credit of a member for the purpose of calculating additional amount in the event of incapacity.

22. Clause 21 seeks to insert a new Seventh Schedule into the Act which specifies the provisions which are applicable only to a corporation established by the Board.
