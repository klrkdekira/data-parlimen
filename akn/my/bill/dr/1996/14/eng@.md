## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Malaysian Rubber Exchange (Incorporation) Act 1962 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with preliminary matters, including the Minister's power to appoint the date for the coming into force of this Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to substitute certain definitions and to introduce certain new definitions for expressions used in the Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 3(4) of the Act to substitute for the word "Director" the word "person".

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 of the Act to provide that the income of the Corporation is to accrue to the Board.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 7 of the Act to introduce a new category of membership of the Corporation and to remove the restriction on membership.

7. Clause 7 seeks to delete section 8 of the Act to remove the exclusive rights of Ordinary Members.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 12 of the Act to provide for the delegation of the management powers of the Board to the Management Committee established under section 17 of the Malaysian Rubber Board (Incorporation) Act 1996, the power to appoint, with the approval of the Minister, a Chairman of the Corporation, and the power to appoint a Deputy Chairman.

<!-- page 6 -->

9. Clauses 10 and 13 seek to delete section 15 of the Act and the Second Schedule respectively to allow the Corporation to make any rules it thinks necessary for the Malaysian Rubber Exchange under section 14.

10. Other proposed amendments to the Act which have not been specifically referred to are consequential or minor in nature.

## IMPLICATIONS
