## A BILL

intituled

An Act to repeal the Malaysian Rubber Research and Development Fund Act 1958, the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia Act 1966, the Malaysian Rubber Exchange and Licensing Board Act 1972, the Rubber Shipping and Packing Control Act 1949 and the Rubber Export Registration Act 1966; to dissolve the Malaysian Rubber Research and Development Board, the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia and the Board of the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia and the Malaysian Rubber Exchange and Licensing Board; to establish the Malaysian Rubber Board and to provide for matters connected therewith and to make better provision in respect of the rubber industry of Malaysia, [ ]

BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

synthetic rubber in liquid, solid or blended form derived from petrochemical products and includes all elastomers derived from natural or synthetic rubber or both;

Functions

4. (1) The functions of the Board shall be— and powers of

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to repeal the Malaysian Rubber Research and Development Fund Act 1958, the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia Act 1966, the Malaysian Rubber Exchange and Licensing Board Act 1972, the Rubber Shipping and Packing Control Act 1949 and the Rubber Export Registration Act 1966; to dissolve the Malaysian Rubber Research and Development Board, the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia and the Board of the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia and the Malaysian Rubber Exchange and Licensing Board; to establish the Malaysian Rubber Board and to provide for matters connected therewith and to make better provision in respect of the rubber industry of Malaysia.

2. Part I contains preliminary matters.

Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

Clause 2 contains the definitions of several expressions used in this Bill.

3. Part II contains provisions relating to the Malaysian Rubber Board.

Clause 3 provides for the establishment and objectives of the Board while clause 4 deals with its functions and powers.

Clause 5 contains provisions on the membership of the Board.

<!-- page 46 -->

Clause 14 seeks to empower the Minister to give directions to the Board.

Clause 15 requires the Board to furnish returns, reports, accounts and information to the Minister and such other public authority as directed by the Minister.

Clause 16 seeks to provide for the delegation of the Board's functions, powers and duties.

Clause 17 seeks to empower the Board to establish committees to assist it in the performance of its functions under the proposed Act, including a Management Committee to manage the Malaysian Rubber Exchange.

4. Part III contains provisions on the Director-General, officers and servants of the Board.

Clause 18 contains provisions in respect of the Director-General.

Clause 19 seeks to empower the Board to appoint its officers and servants. Subclause 19(3) seeks to empower the Board, with the approval of the Minister, to make regulations with respect to the conditions of service of its officers and servants.

Clause 20 seeks to empower the Board to grant loans and make advances to its officers and servants.

Clause 21 seeks to provide for the delegation of the Director- General's duties.

Clause 22 seeks to confer on the Board disciplinary control over its officers and servants and for this purpose the Board may establish disciplinary committees.

Clause 23 deals with the question of appeal against the decision of a disciplinary committee.

Clause 24 seeks to empower the Board, with the approval of the Minister, to make disciplinary regulations and provides that such regulations may create disciplinary offences and provide for disciplinary punishments.

Clause 25 seeks to allow the Board to impose a surcharge in respect of its officers and servants and sets out when the Board may do so.

Clause 26 requires the Board to inform the person to be surcharged whilst clauses 27 and 28 deal with the withdrawal of surcharge and the recovery of surcharge respectively.

<!-- page 47 -->

Clause 29 deals with the composition of the Board where the person to be surcharged is the Director-General.

5. Part IV contains provisions relating to research and development.

Clause 30 provides for the establishment of research centres. Subclause 30(2) seeks to provide for the reconstitution of the Rubber Research Institute of Malaysia as a research centre in accordance with the provisions of the proposed Act after its dissolution to enable the goodwill connected with the dissolved Institute to be enjoyed by the research centre.

Clause 31 deals with the functions and powers of research centres.

Clause 32 deals with access to research centres and their facilities and to their research findings.

Clause 33 prohibits reproduction and publication of research findings except with the prior written consent of the Director- General and in accordance with the terms and conditions determined by the Director-General.

6. Part V contains financial provisions.

Clause 34 provides for the establishment of a Fund and clause 36 seeks to impose upon the Board the duty of conserving such Fund.

Clause 35 specifies the expenditure which may be charged on the Fund.

Clause 37 empowers the Minister after consultation with the Minister of Finance to impose and collect a cess on rubber and rubber products produced in or exported from Malaysia.

Clause 38 empowers the Board to impose and collect fees and other charges in respect of technical advisory services or other services provided by the Board.

Clause 39 deals with the expenditure and preparation of estimates whilst clause 40 provides that the Statutory Bodies (Accounts and Annual Reports) Act 1980 applies to the Board and to a corporation established under the proposed Act.

7. Part VI deals with the other powers and functions of the Board.

Clause 41 provides for the borrowing of money by the Board and clause 42 seeks to enable the Board to invest its assets.

<!-- page 48 -->

Clause 43 empowers the Board to employ and pay agents and technical advisors, including advocates and solicitors, bankers and stockbrokers.

Clause 44 empowers the Board, with the approval of the Minister after consultation with the Minister of Finance, to grant loans.

The Board is also allowed to establish corporations under clause

45.

8. Part VII contains provisions relating to enforcement, investigation, evidence and offences.

9. Part VIII deals with general matters such as the regulation of the rubber industry", compounding of offences, institution and conduct of prosecution under the proposed Act, jurisdiction to try offences under the proposed Act, protection of officers, the power to make regulations and the validation of acts done in anticipation of the proposed Act.

10. Part IX contains repeal, savings and transitional provisions.
