## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Armed Forces Act 1972 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with preliminary matters including the commencement of this Act.

<!-- page 20 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to introduce certain new definitions for terms used in the Act, namely the definition of "appointed date", "Perintah Majlis Angkatan Tentera" and "Service Chief.

4. Clause 4 seeks to introduce four new sections. Section 5A provides that the Armed Forces Council may require an officer or serviceman to serve outside Malaysia. Section 5B empowers the Armed Forces Council to attach temporarily any officer or serviceman to any foreign force in or outside Malaysia. Section 5c empowers the Armed Forces Council to second any officer or serviceman to any department of the Federal Government, a State, local authority, statutory authority or organisation, in or outside Malaysia. Section 5D seeks to provide that sections 5A, 5B and 5c shall not apply to the operational use of the officer or servicemen. This is in conformity with Article 137(1) of the Federal Constitution.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 6 of the Act to empower the Armed Forces Council to recognise the reckonable service, if any, of an officer of the volunteer forces who is commissioned into the regular forces. Clause 11 seeks to do the same in respect of a serviceman.

6. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 10A to permit an officer cadet, other than a serviceman who has been appointed as an officer cadet, to purchase his discharge with the consent of the Armed Forces Council within three months of his appointment. This is intended to give newly appointed officer cadets an opportunity to leave the armed forces where he finds it unsuitable for him.

7. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 13 of the Act to extend the application of that section to the transfer of an officer from one corps or branch to another corps or branch in the same Service.

8. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 15 of the Act to allow regulations made by the Armed Forces Council to provide for matters which may be issued by Perintah Majlis Angkatan Tentera. It also seeks to widen the scope of matters which may be governed by regulations. Clause 16 seeks to do the same in respect of regulations governing servicemen.

9. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 21 of the Act for the purposes of clarity as well as to extend the application of the section to the Navy and the Air Force.

10. Clause 13 seeks to introduce two new sections, Section 24A provides that sections 24, 25 and 26 shall not apply to a person enlisting in the regular forces on or after the appointed date. Section 24B seeks to provide for the terms of enlistment of servicemen enlisting on or after the appointed date. Where the person enlisting has attained the age of eighteen years, the initial enlistment is for twelve years.

11. Clause 14 seeks to introduce a new section 25 A to enable the service of servicemen enlisting on or after the appointed date to be extended from twelve to fifteen years of full-time service.

12. Clause 15 seeks to introduce three new sections. Section 26A provides for the re-engagement of servicemen enlisting on or after the appointed date. Section 26B disallows the service of any servicemen to be extended or re-engaged beyond the age of fifty-five years. Section 26c introduces the requirement for servicemen who are still serving in the regular forces or the reserve on or after the appointed date to, upon discharge from full-time service or completion of service in the reserve, serve in the reserve for five years or until the age of fifty years, whichever is earlier. The Armed Forces Council has the discretion to exempt any person or category of persons from such service in the reserve.

13. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 51 of the Act to enable the 'respective Service Chiefs of each Service or any officer authorised by his Service Chief to make standing orders or other routine orders.

14. Clause 21 seeks to introduce four new sections. Sections 94A, 94B, 94c and 94D are intended to provide for matters including the appointment of the Chief Provost Marshal, Provost Marshals, provost officers and investigating officers.

15. Clause 33 seeks to amend subsection 144(1) of the Act to extend the offences exempted from its application to sections 61,17 and 88. Except for those offences, the three-year limitation period for a trial by court-martial runs from the date of the commission of the offence to the date the convening order is issued and served on the accused person. Further, the clause seeks to provide that in reckoning this limitation period, the period during which judicial review proceedings are taken is to be disregarded.

16. Clauses 35, 37, 40, 44 and 48 seek to introduce similar amendments to sections 155, 157, 187, 194 and 206 respectively. The amendments are intended to allow regulations made by the Armed Forces Council to provide for matters which may be issued by Perintah Majlis Angkatan Tentera.

17. Clause 41 seeks to introduce two new sections. Section 187A authorises the collection of contributions for the welfare and benefit of the armed forces and their families. Section 187B validates the collection made prior to the coming into force of this Act and the use thereof.

18. Clause 42 seeks to amend section 188 of the Act to introduce the new policy on the creation of a reserve of officers which shall apply to all officers who on or after the appointed date, are still serving in the regular forces.

19. Clause 43 seeks to introduce a new section 189A to provide that an officer or serviceman serving in the Regular Forces Reserve shall hold the last substantive rank held by him whilst in the regular service. For this purpose, subsection 189A(2) provides that the short service commission officer serving in the regular forces shall be deemed to be a commissioned officer.

20. Clause 46 seeks to amend section 201 of the Act to provide that every member of a volunteer force who has completed the requisite training may be required to continue to serve in the volunteer forces for a minimum period as prescribed by regulations made under section 207 of the Act. Clause 49 in turn amends subsection 207(2) of the Act to provide for this prescription of a minimum period.

21. Clause 47 seeks to introduce a new section 201A to permit the Armed Forces Council to apply, mutatis mutandis, certain provisions of Parts II, III and IV of the Act to the members of the volunteer forces where it is not inconsistent with the provisions of Part IX of the Act.

22. Clause 50 seeks to amend section 213 of the Act to enable the Regular Forces Reserve to use the existing structure and organisation of the regular forces for the purpose of service offences and punishment.

23. Clause 51 seeks to repeal the Armed Forces (Amendment) Act

1984.

24. Other proposed amendments to the Act not specifically referred to are consequential in nature necessitated by the change in the structure of ranks in the Armed Forces as well as the nomenclature of some ranks.
