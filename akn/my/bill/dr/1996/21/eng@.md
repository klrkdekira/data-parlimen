## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Road Transport Act 1987 (the "Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 to amend the definition of "driving licence" and to introduce the definitions of "learner's driving licence", "licensed registrar" and "probationary driving licence".

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 8 to provide for the imposition of a duty upon a licensed registrar to keep and maintain the register of all motor vehicles registered under the Act after the privatisation of the activity in respect of registration and licensing of motor vehicles. Various amendments have also been made under clauses 4, 5, 6(a)(i), 8, 9. 10, 11, 12(a), 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 34, 35(a), 36, 37 and 39 as a result of privatisation.

4. Subclause 6(a)(iii) seeks to amend section 11 by deleting the proviso to subsection (1) as the proviso will no longer be applicable after the introduction of new subsections (1A) and (1B) into subclause 6(b) which allow, inter alia, an owner of a motor vehicle to assign his registration number to another motor vehicle owned by another person.

5. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 1 1A to allow any person to propose his own registration number.

6. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 27 to extend the types of offences where the penalty is a disqualification from obtaining a driving licence for a period of twelve months.

7. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 29 to introduce a new category of driving licence, namely a probationary driving licence. Under this provision, a driver who has just passed the prescribed tests will be granted a probationary driving licence for a period of two years before a competent driving licence is granted to him. Clause 19 also seeks to provide that the driving licence of a person will not be renewed if the person has an outstanding matter or case.

8. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 35 to empower the Director General of the Road Transport Department to revoke a driving licence under the demerit point system.

<!-- page 17 -->

9. Clause 21 seeks to introduce a new section 35A to empower the Director General of the Road Transport Department to revoke a probationary driving licence.

10. Clauses 22 and 23 seek to amend sections 37 and 38 to extend the power of the Director General of the Road Transport Department to revoke a driving licence of a person as a consequence of the introduction of section 35A.

11. Clause 24 seeks to introduce a new subsection (3A) into section 41 to empower the court to revoke a probationary driving licence of a person. Similar amendments are also proposed in clauses 25, 26, 27, 28, 29 and 31(b) to sections 42, 43, 44, 45, 45A and 45B.

12. Clauses 30 and 31(a) seek to amend sections 45B and 45c to enable the same penalty as in the existing section 45A to be imposed on any person who refuses to undergo a breath test.

13. Clause 32 seeks to amend subsection 45G(l) to amend the definition of "breath test".

14. Clause 33 seeks to amend section 54 to extend the provision of that section to offences committed under sections 45A to 45c.

15. Clause 38 seeks to introduce a new section 117A to enable a record of previous conviction to be admissible as evidence and to constitute prima facie proof of any fact stated therein.

16. Clause 40 seeks to amend section 126 by introducing a new subsection (8) to empower the Minister to direct the Director General or a Director to exercise any function of a road transport officer that has been assigned to any person licensed under that section.
