## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Rubber Industry Smallholders Development Authority Act 1972 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for preliminary matters.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by inserting a new subsection (1).

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce new sections 11B, 11C, 11D, 11E, 1 1F, 1 1G, 11H. 11I, 11J and 1 1K. The proposed new sections 11B and 11C seek to provide for the establishment of a Personnel Committee and a Personnel Appeal Committee respectively. The proposed new section 11D seeks to provide for the establishment of disciplinary committees for the different categories of officers and servants of the Authority. The proposed new section 11E seeks to allow an appeal to be made to the Minister or Disciplinary Appeal Committee, as the case may be, against the decision of the disciplinary committee. The proposed new section 11F seeks to empower the Authority,

<!-- page 10 -->

with the approval of the Minister, to make regulations to provide for disciplinary offences and punishments. The proposed new sections 11G, 11H, 11I and 11J relate to the imposition of surcharge on any person who is or was in the employment of the Authority. The proposed new section 1 1K seeks to provide that in any action for surcharge against the Director General, the composition of the Authority for the purpose of sections 11G and 11J shall not include the Director General.
