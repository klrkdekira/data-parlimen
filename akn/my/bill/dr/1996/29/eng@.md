## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Lembaga Kemajuan Wilayah Jengka Act 1983 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for preliminary matters.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by inserting a definition for the word "accounting officer".

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce new sections 17A, 17B, 17c, 17D, 17E, 17F, 17G and 17H. The proposed new section 1 7A seeks to provide for the establishment of disciplinary committees for different categories of officers and servants of the Lembaga. The proposed new section 17B seeks to allow an appeal to be made to the Minister or the Disciplinary Appeal Committee, as the case may be, against the decision of the disciplinary committee. The proposed new section 17c seeks to empower the Lembaga, with the approval of the Minister, to make regulations to provide for disciplinary offences and punishments. The new sections 17D, 17E, 17F and 17G relate to the imposition of surcharge on any person who is or was in the employment of the Lembaga. The proposed new section 1 7H seeks to provide that in any action for surcharge against the General Manager, the composition of the Lembaga for the purpose of sections 17D and 17G shall not include the General Manager.
