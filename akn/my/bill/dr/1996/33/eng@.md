## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Institut Teknologi MARA Act 1976 (the "Act").

3. Clause 3 seeks to make general amendments to the Act, The words "Director" has been substituted for the word "Rector", "Majlis" for the words "Board of Directors" and "Principal" for the word "Provost".

4. Clause 4 seeks to give effect to the change of executive body of the Institut from the Majlis to the Board of Directors and to provide that all references to the old executive body are to be construed as references to the new executive body.

Subclauses4(2) and (3) relate to the rights, privileges, liabilities, duties or obligations of the Majlis, and legal proceedings by or against it, which are not affected by the change of executive body.

5. Clause 5 seeks to provide that all existing liabilities incurred by the Majlis may be enforced against the Board of Directors.

6. Clause 6 seeks to provide that all land, property and assets vested in the Majlis will, after the date of coming into force of the Act, vest in the Board of Directors.

7. Clause 7 seeks to provide that any reference to the Director or Principal will, after the date of coming into force of the Act, be construed as references to the Rector or Provost respectively.

<!-- page 46 -->

Subclauses 7(2) and (3) relate to the rights, privileges, liabilities, duties or obligations of the Director or the Principal, and legal proceedings by or against them, which are not affected by the change of designation.

8. Clause 8 seeks to provide that all existing liabilities incurred by the Director or Principal may be enforced against the Rector or Provost respectively.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to introduce certain new definitions and to amend certain definitions used in the Act, the more significant being as follows:

(a) the definition of "Board" to refer to the Board of Director of the Institut as the new executive body of the Institut in place of the Majlis;

(b) the definition of "degree" as the Institut is empowered to confer degrees;

(c) the definition of "Rector" as the chief executive, admini- strative and academic officer of the Institut who may also be styled as the "President" of the Institut;

(d) the definition of "School or Centre" to include Faculty, College, Department and Academy of the Institut;

(e) the definition of "staff to specifically reflect the appoint- ment of lecturers and to include senior professors, professors, etc.; and

(f) the definitions of "student" and "Students' Representative Committee" ("SRC"), all for the purposes of the provisions of the Act relating to students of the Institut.

10. Clause 10 seeks to make general amendments to the national language text of the Act by substituting for the words "Meteri", "meteri" and "dimeterikan" the words "Meterai", "meterai" and "dimeteraikan" respectively and for the word "pelajaran" the word "pendidikan".

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 4 of the Act to empower the Institut, amongst others, to confer degrees, honorary degrees or awards and to establish Branches outside Malaysia. It also seeks to accord the status of degrees at Bachelor level, with or without honours, as the case may be, the Diplomas and Advanced Diplomas which were conferred by the Institut and have been recognised by the Government to be equivalent to degrees, from the dates the Government gave its recognition.

12. Clause 12 seeks to amend the Act to introduce a new section 4A which confers additional powers on the Institut. Amongst others, it includes power to borrow money, to enter into business joint-ventures, to establish companies and corporations, and to acquire and hold for investment shares, etc. Where a corporation is established by the Institut, it shall adhere to the provisions of the new First Schedule to the Act.

<!-- page 47 -->

13. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 5 of the Act to enable a principal officer of the Institut or a representative of the Rector, as the circumstances may require, to execute the sealing of, and to sign, instruments, degrees, ere., in place of the Rector.

14. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 6 of the Act to specify that the main campus of the Institut is at Shah Alam, in the State of Selangor.

15. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 7 of the Act to allow the Institut to establish and maintain its Branch or Branches abroad.

16. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 8 of the Act to delete the requirement, on the part of the Board of Directors, to obtain prior concurrence of the Minister in determining the number of Schools and Centres of the Institut.

17. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 9 of the Act to enable the Institut to conduct any course of study jointly, or in association, affiliation or collaboration with any University or University College or higher educational institutions, etc. The conduct of any such course of study can either be wholly or partly within or outside Malaysia.

18. Clause 18 seeks to substitute section 13 of the Act to establish the Board of Directors which shall be the executive body of the Institut, in line with the objective of restructuring the administration of the Institut.

19. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 16 of the Act to allow the Board of Directors to appoint any committees as it thinks necessary or desirable. Consequent to this, sections 17,18 and 19 are deleted by clause 21.

20. Clause 20 seeks to amend the Act to introduce a new section 16A which creates the Senate of the Institut. Although the Senate merely replaces the existing Academic Affairs Committee of the Majlis appointed under section 16 of the Act, the role of the Senate is enhanced by the fact that it is the body that advises the Board of Directors on all academic matters of the Institut and no resolution affecting any such matter can be passed by the Board unless the Senate has first been given the opportunity of recording and transmitting to the Board its opinion thereon.

21. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 21 of the Act to clarify that the Registrar is the principal Registry officer of the Institut. This clause and clauses 24 and 26 also seek to provide that the terms and conditions of service of the Registrar, Bursar and other staff of the Institut shall be approved by the Board of Directors instead of the Minister.

22. Clause 25 seeks to amend the Act to introduce a new section 22A to provide for the appointment of other principal officers of the Institut.

23. Clause 27 seeks to amend the Act to introduce new section 23A relating to the discipline of the staff of the Institut.

<!-- page 48 -->

At present, the disciplinary authority in respect of the staff is the Disciplinary Committee of the Institut constituted under subsection 4( 1), or any member of the staff of the Institut or any board of members of the staff of the Institut to whom disciplinary functions have been delegated under subsection 4(3), of the Educational Institutions (Discipline) Act 1976 ("Act 174"). The new section 23A provides for a change of the disciplinary authority. It empowers the Board of Directors to appoint different disciplinary committees in respect of different categories of staff.

24. Clause 28 seeks to provide for transitional provisions relating to disciplinary proceedings which are pending before, and appeals against the decision of, the Disciplinary Committee of the Institut or any member of the staffer any board of members of the staff of the Institut as explained in paragraph 23. These provisions are necessitated by the change of disciplinary authority over the staff.

25. Clause 29 contains transitional provisions relating to the existing disciplinary rules made under section 35 of the Act.

26. Act 174 also contains provisions for matters relating to students of the Institut. With the view to enable the Institut to specifically deal with such matters under the Act, clause 30 seeks to create a new Part IVA containing 11 new sections, namely, sections 23B to 23L which substantially incorporate the provisions of Parts II, III and IV of Act 174, and certain provisions of Act 30, as may be necessary for the purposes of this Act.

Necessary amendments will have to be made to the First Schedule to Act 174 simultaneously upon the coming into force of the new section 23A and the new Part IVA.

27. Clause 31 seeks to amend section 24 of the Act to add "subventions" and "leases" to the Institut's Fund whilst the proposed amendment to section 28 of the Act by clause 32 would enable the Board of Directors to accept "subventions" and "legacies" in aid of the Institut's finances.

28. Clause 33 seeks to amend the Act to introduce a new section 28A which relates to the imposition of surcharge on past or present staff of the Institut with regard to any improper payment, deficiency in or destruction of property, failure to keep proper accounts or records, failure to make or delay in making payment, where no satisfactory explanation for such action has been given. The amount of such surcharge shall be a debt due to the Institut. .

29. Clause 34 seeks to amend section 29 of the Act to require the Minister, in exercising his power to give directions, to act in accordance with the national policies, strategies and guidelines on higher education formulated or determined by the National Council on Higher Education.

<!-- page 49 -->

30. Clause 35 seeks to amend the Act to introduce new section 29A. New section 29A seeks to empower the Minister to delegate to the Board of Directors any of his powers, duties or functions under the Act.

31. Clause 36 seeks to amend the Act to introduce new sections 34A and

34B. The new section 34A seeks to protect any actor omission of the Board of Directors, member of the Board, the Rector, the Deputy Rector, the Senate, member of the Senate, staff of the Institut or any person authorised by the Institut, done in good faith. The new section 34B contains provisions relating to service of document.

32. Clause 37 seeks to amend section 35 of the Act to empower the Board of Directors to make rules instead of the Minister. It includes the power to make rules pertaining to the award of degrees, the discipline of the staff and students of the Institut and any matters relating to the SRC and other student bodies.

33. Clause 38 seeks to introduce new First, Second and Third Schedules to the Act.

The First Schedule contains provisions relating to corporations established by the Institut.

The Second Schedule contains provisions regulating the Board, the committees and the Senate.

The Third Schedule contains provisions relating to the SRC and other student bodies.

34. Other amendments not specifically referred to are minor or consequential in nature.
