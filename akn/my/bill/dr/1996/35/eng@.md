## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Broadcasting Act 1988 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title and commencement of this Bill.

3. Clauses 2 and 10 seek to amend the definition of certain terms in the Act and introduce new definitions into the Act to, amongst others, cater for new technologies in broadcasting and facilitate enforcement of the Act.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 4(1) of the Act to require the licensing of both the activity of broadcasting any broadcast matter in or from Malaysia and specifying the broadcasting services, as listed out in a new Schedule sought to be introduced through clause 24, through which broadcast matter may be broadcast. Power is given to the Minister to amend the Schedule, by order published in the Gazette, in a new subsection (5) introduced into section 4 of the Act.

5. Section 5 of the Act is amended in clause 5 to make the contravention of the conditions of a licence to broadcast an offence.

6. The maximum validity period of 12 months of a licence to broadcast contained in section 7 of the Act is substituted in clause 6 with a provision empowering the Minister to determine the term of the licence.

7. Clause 7 seeks to provide that a licensee to whom a notice of intention to suspend or revoke his licence to broadcast is issued under subsection 9(1) of the Act, shall show cause within thirty days from receipt of such notice why the suspension or revocation should not be done.

8. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new section 1 1A in a new Part IIIA to require that a licence is to be obtained before a person may receive in Malaysia any foreign broadcast matter from any satellite or transmit from Malaysia any broadcast matter to any satellite.

9. The words "memasang" and "dipasang"' in the national language text of the Act are replaced with the words "menempatkan" and "ditempatkan" respectively in clauses 10, 11, 12 and 23 to reflect the equivalent meaning of the word "install" within the context of the Act.

10. Subsection 14(2) of the Act is amended in clause 12 to increase the maximum penalty for offences relating to radio broadcast receivers and television broadcast receivers from three to ten thousand ringgit.

<!-- page 23 -->

11. Clause 15 seeks to increase the general penalty for offences under Part IV in section 18 from one thousand five hundred ringgit to three thousand ringgit.

12. Clause 16 seeks to amend the heading of Part V of the Act from "MISCELLANEOUS" to "GENERAL POWERS".

13. Clause 18 seeks to introduce new subsections (3) and (4) into section 21 of the Act to change the present administrative requirement on the prior approval of the Minister before the airing of any advertisements into a legal requirement, failure of which shall be an offence, subject to a penalty of ten thousand ringgit or one year imprisonment or both.

14. New sections 2lA to 21c are introduced into the Act in clause

19. Section 2lA empowers the Minister to grant licences to distribute broadcast matter. Section 21s is on the power of the Minister to revoke or suspend a licence and the right of the licensee to show cause why his licence should not be revoked or suspended. The Minister is given the power to exempt any person or class of persons from the application of sections 4, I IA, 14 and 2lA of the Act in section 21C.

15. Clause 20 deals with a new Part VI on enforcement. Section 21D is on the power to enter, search and seize. The power to search and seize with and without warrant is provided in section 2lE. The requirement to put up a list of things seized is provided in section 2 IF. The provision on the forfeiture of all things seized is in section

21G. The power to seal is dealt with in section 2lH. The power to require production of books, registers and other documents is dealt with in section 21I. The power to require information and examine persons is provided in section 21J. The power to prosecute is contained in section 2lK while the power to compound is contained in section 2lL.

16. Clause 21 seeks to introduce a new Part VII on miscellaneous provisions. Offences by bodies corporate are dealt with in section 2lM. Section 2lN relates to protection against legal proceedings afforded to an authorized officer. Section 21O seeks to indemnify the Government.

17. Clause 22 seeks to correct a spelling error of the words "authorized" and "licensed" in section 22 of the Act.

18. Clause 23 seeks to enable the Minister to prescribe regulations arising from the additional powers given to the Minister in this Bill.
