## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend sections 3, 4 and 6 of the Members of Parliament (Remuneration) Act 1980 to enable adjustments to be made to the salaries or allowances, as the case may be, of Members of Parliament, the President and Deputy President of the Senate, the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives, Members of the Administration, the Leader and Deputy Leader of the House of Representatives and the Leader of the Opposition.
