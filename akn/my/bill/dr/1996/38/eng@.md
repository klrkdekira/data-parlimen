## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employees' Social Security Act 1969 ("the Act").

2. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new proviso to section 31 of the Act. The new proviso allows insured persons who sustain employment injury due to motor vehicle accidents or their dependants to claim compensation or damages from the insured persons' employer or a servant of the insured persons' employer who is required to be insured against Third Party risks under Part IV of the Road Transport Act 1987.

3. Clause 4 seeks to delete section 42 of the Act. With this deletion any person who is entitled to receive benefits under this Act will be able to receive any similar benefits admissible under the provisions of any other written law.

4. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 57A. The new section 57A enables the Organisation to provide for education benefit in the form of loans or scholarships to a dependant child of an insured person.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 59 of the Act by adding a new subsection (2A). The new subsection (2A) empowers the Minister to appoint one of the Deputy Directors General to perform the duties of the Director General in the absence of the Director General.

Employees' Social Security (Amendment) 7

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 59B of the Act to specifically name the Board as the Social Security Organisation Board and to enlarge the composition of the Board by introducing as members one officer from the Ministry of Human Resources, one officer from the Ministry of Health and three persons with experience in social security matters to be appointed by the Minister. The Director General will no longer be the Deputy Chairman. Clause 7 also seeks to introduce new subsections (8), (9) and (10). Subsection (8) provides for the appointment of alternate members for members who represent employers and insured persons. Subsection (9) provides that when attending meetings of the Board, an alternate member shall be deemed to be a member of the Board. Subsection (10) provides that an alternate member, unless he sooner resigns membership or his appointment is sooner revoked, ceases to be an alternate member when the member in respect of whom he is an alternate member ceases to be a member of the Board.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 59c of the Act. With the proposed amendment the Minister is required to appoint any person to carry out the duties of the Chairman in the absence of the Chairman.

8. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 71 of the Act by adding a new paragraph (viiia). The new paragraph (viiia) enables the Fund to be used for the purpose of granting loans or scholarships to a dependant child of an insured person as specified under the new section 57A.

9. Clause 12 seeks to delete section 75B of the Act as it is no longer applicable in view of the proposed amendments to sections 59B and 59c.

10. Clauses 13 and 14 seek to amend sections 93 and 94 of the Act to increase the penalty provided in those sections.

11. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 105 of the Act by adding a new paragraph (xxxia). The new paragraph (xxxia) enables regulations to be made for the imposition of a fee or a charge for providing any information or document which is in the custody of the Social Security Organisation.

12. Other amendments which are not specifically mentioned are consequential or minor in nature.
