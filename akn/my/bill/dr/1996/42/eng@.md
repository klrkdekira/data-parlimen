## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967, the Petroleum (Income Tax) Act 1967, the Real Property Gains Tax Act 1976, the Labuan Offshore Business Activity Tax Act 1990, the Service Tax Act 1975, the Sales Tax Act 1972, the Customs Act 1967 and the Free Zones Act 1990, and to repeal the Cinematograph Film-Hire Duty Act 1965.
