## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Dental Act 1971 ("the Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to substitute for the words "doktor gigi" wherever they appear in the national language text of the Act the words "doktor pergigian".

<!-- page 6 -->

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of the Act to enlarge the composition of the Malaysian Dental Council ("Council") by introducing as members six dental surgeons from among the staff of the dental faculties of the higher educational institutions in Malaysia and six dental surgeons to be appointed by the Minister. Clause 3 also seeks to introduce new subsections (13A), (15), (16), (17), (18) and (19). Subsection (13A) seeks to disqualify a practitioner whose name has been struck off from the Register or who has been reprimanded from being nominated or elected to be a member of the Council for a period of six years from the expiration of such suspension or from the date of such reprimand, as the case may be. Subsection (16) provides that a member of the Council in respect of whom a preliminary investigation has been commenced or a disciplinary inquiry has been instituted shall be suspended from being such a member from the date of commencement of the investigation or the institution of the inquiry. However subsection

(18) provides that if the Council finds that no case has been made against a member, he shall be reinstated. Subsection (19) provides that the member shall hold office for the unexpired term of his office if the reinstatement is before the expiration of his term of office.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 17(1) of the Act by introducing a new restriction on registration under the Act. The Council may direct the Registrar not to register any person who is found unfit to perform his professional duties by reason of his mental or physical condition.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 23(1) of the Act to provide for mental or physical condition as another ground for the cessation of registration under the Act.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend subsection 42(1) of the Act to increase the general penalty provided in that subsection.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend subparagraph 2(3) of the First Schedule to the Act by increasing the quorum for a meeting of the Council from seven to ten members.

8. Other amendments which are not specifically mentioned are consequential or minor in nature.
