## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (the "Code") as a consequence of the decision of the Federal Court in July 1996 in the case of Arulpragasan a/I Sandaraju dan Pendakwa Raya ("Arulpragasan's case").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 173 which deals with summary trials in the subordinate courts.

<!-- page 6 -->

6 Bill

Amendments are proposed to paragraph (f) of section 173 of the Code to make it clear that at the close of the prosecution's case, what the Court has to consider is whether or not the prosecution has made out a prima facie case against the accused person. This approach is consistent with the approach taken by the courts in several other Commonwealth countries. In Malaysia, particularly since the decision of the Privy Council in the case of Haw Tua Tau v. Public Prosecutor in 1981, this has also been the approach taken by the Supreme Court and the Federal Court (except in the case of Khoo Hi Chiang v. Public Prosecutor in 1993) prior to Arulpragasan's case.

The amendments proposed to paragraph (m) of section 173 of the Code provides that it is only at the conclusion of the whole case that the Court must consider all the evidence adduced before it (including the evidence adduced by the accused in his defence) and determine whether the prosecution has proved its case beyond reasonable doubt.

3. Clause 4 of the Bill seeks to amend section 180 of the Code to achieve the same purpose as that sought to be achieved by amendments to paragraph (f) of section 173 of the Code.

4. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section I82A into the Code to provide for the same matters as are in the proposed amendments to paragraph (m) of section 173 of the Code.

5. Other amendments proposed in this Bill are—

(a) amendments for the purpose of reducing the differences between the trial procedure in the High Court and the trial procedure in the subordinate courts;

(b) consequential amendments necessitated by the amendments proposed to paragraphs (f) and (m) of section 173 and to section 180 and the proposed introduction of section 182A; and

(c) drafting amendments and other amendments which are minor in nature.
