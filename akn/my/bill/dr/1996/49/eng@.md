## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for offences against the precepts of the religion of Islam by persons professing the religion in the Federal Territories, Several of the offences included in this Bill are presently provided for in Part IX of the Administration of Muslim Law Enactment 1952 of the State of Selangor as modified and extended to the Federal Territories (hereinafter referred to as the "1952 Enactment"). However, such provisions have been revised and improved, including imposing heavier penalties to reflect the gravity of the offences.

2. Part I deals with preliminary matters. Subclause 1(2) clearly provides that the Act proposed by this Bill applies only to persons professing the religion of Islam.

3. Part II contains offences relating to 'aqidah. Clause 3 makes it an offence for any person to worship nature or does any act which shows worship or reverence of any person, animal, place or thing in any manner contrary to Islamic Law. Clause 4 is an offence on deviationist teachings and practices. Other offences provided under this Part are the offences of propagating religious doctrines other than that of the religion of Islam among persons professing the Islamic faith (clause 5) and the offence of declaring onself or another person a prophet, Imam Mahadi or wall (clause 6).

4. Part III contains offences relating to the sanctity of the religion of Islam. Among the offences provided for in this Part which are not provided for in the 1952 Enactment are the offence of insulting or bringing into contempt the religion of Islam (clause 7), the offence of defying a Court order (clause 10) and the offence of gambling (clause 18). Offences provided for in the 1952 Enactment and which provisions have been improved upon are the offence of deriding Quranic verses or Hadith (clause 8), the offence of acting in contempt or defiance of religious authorities (clause 9), the offence of teaching religion without tauliah (clause 11), the offence of giving, propagating or disseminating opinion contrary to fatwa (clause 12), the offence of printing, producing, etc. religious publication contrary to Islamic Law (clause 13), the offence of failing to perform Friday prayers (clause 14), the offence of being disrespectful of Ramadhan (clause 15), the offence of non-payment of zakat or fitrah (clause 16), the offence of instigating neglect of religious duty (clause 17) and the offence of consuming intoxicating drinks (clause 19).

5. Part IV contains offences relating to decency. Apart from the offences of sexual intercourse out of wedlock (clause 23) and khalwat (clause 27), all other offences under this Part are not provided for under the 1952 Enactment. Such offences are the offence of incest (clause 20), the offence of doing an act preparatory to sexual intercourse out of wedlock (clause 24), the offence of liwat (clause

<!-- page 24 -->

(Federal Territories)

25), the offence of musahaqah (clause 26), the offence of a male person posing as a woman (clause 28) and the offence of acting or behaving in an indecent manner in public (clause 29).

6. Part V contains miscellaneous offences. The offences provided for are the offence of giving false evidence, information or statement for use in judicial proceedings (clause 30), the offence of regarding a Muslim as non-Muslim (clause 31), the offence of destroying or defiling mosques, etc. (clause 32), the offence of encouraging vice (clause 35), the offence of enticing a married woman (clause 36), the offence of preventing a married couple from cohabiting (clause 37), the offence of encouraging divorce or neglect of responsibilities (clause 38), the offence of enticing a female person (clause 39), the offence of selling or giving away a child to a non-Muslim (clause 40), the offence of qazaf (clause 41) and the offence of abusing halal signs (clause 42). The offences of collecting zakat or fitrah without authority (clause 33) and illegally paying zakat or fitrah (clause 34) are existing provisions in the 1952 Enactment.

7. Part VI contains provisions on abetment and attempt which are more detailed than those provided for in the 1952 Enactment.

8. Part VII contains general exceptions. Among the general exceptions to the offences provided under the proposed Act are acts of a Judge when acting judicially (clause 48), acts done pursuant to a judgment or order of a Court (clause 49), acts done by a person justified by law (clause 50), acts of a child who is not baligh (clause 51), acts of person of unsound mind (clause 52) and acts which a person is compelled to do by threat (clause 53).

9. Part VIII contains general matters. Clause 54 empowers the Majlis to appoint rehabilitation centres or approved homes. Clauses 55 and 56 empower the Court to commit persons convicted of certain offences to an approved rehabilitation centre or approved home. Clause 57 provides for the cessation of application of Part IX, except sections 164 and 165, of the 1952 Enactment to the Federal Territories. Clause 58 seeks to modify the provision of section 164 of the 1952 Enactment in its application to the Federal Territories.
