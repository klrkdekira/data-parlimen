## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Offshore Companies Act 1990 (the "Act").

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title and commencement of this Bill.

<!-- page 21 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act to substitute certain definitions and to introduce certain new definitions to facilitate the interpretation of the Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 6 of the Act to clarify that a resident who incorporates an offshore company in Labuan is a non-resident for the purposes of section 8 of the Labuan Trust Companies Act 1990.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 7(3) of the Act to stipulate when an offshore company can carry on business in Malaysian currency and with Malaysian residents and to allow petroleum activities to be conducted by offshore companies. Clause 4 also seeks to amend subsection 7(4) to clarify instances when an offshore company is deemed not to be dealing with residents.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 9(2) to provide for instances when documents could be extracted from or lodged with the Registrar without the need to go through a trust company.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 21 to require that certain labels form part of the name of an offshore company.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend subsection 29(2) to permit residents to subscribe for shares in an offshore company but cannot subscribe for debentures or lend to or deposit money with an offshore company.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 51 to allow redenomination of shares from one currency to another.

10. Clause 13 seeks to insert a new subsection (2A) into section 86 and a new paragraph (d) into subsection (4) in that section. Under the proposed provisions the company number must appear on the memorandum and articles of association of an offshore company and on other prescribed documents.

11. Clause 14 seeks to amend subsection 87(2) to allow a domestic company wholly owned by a trust company to be a director of an offshore company.

12. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 93 to insert a new subsection

(1A) and to amend subsection (2) to allow a corporation to be a secretary to an offshore company.

13. Clause 16 seeks to amend subsections 94(2) and 94(3) to set out the particulars of a director and secretary of any offshore company that must be entered in the register.

14. Clause 17 seeks to insert a new section 94A to set out the liability of corporate directors or secretaries for, and defences to offences against the Act.

<!-- page 22 -->

### 22

### Bill

15. Clause 21 seeks to introduce a new Part, namely Part VIIIA, into the Act to provide for the licensing, control, administration and liabilities of management companies.

16. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 147 to allow offshore companies owned by non-residents to hold securities in a domestic company subject to the limitation that it does not amount to a controlling interest in the domestic company.

17. Clause 24 seeks to amend section 150 to widen the scope of persons on whom the Minister may grant exemption from certain provisions of the Act.

18. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 151 and clause 26 seeks to insert new sections 15lA to 151c to provide for circumstances under which the names of offshore companies may be struck off the register and the consequences of such striking off.

19. Other amendments not specifically mentioned in this Statement are—

(a) amendments to several provisions to lengthen the period for doing certain acts under the Act from a week or two weeks to a month; and

(b) consequential and minor amendments.
