## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The Bill seeks to amend the Offshore Banking Act 1990 (the "Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by inserting several new definitions and amending several existing definitions. The definition of "Bank Negara Malaysia" is necessary because the definition of "Bank" now no longer refers to Bank Negara but to the Labuan Offshore Financial Services Authority. Definitions such as "building credit business", "credit token business", "dealing in securities", "derivative financial instrument", "derivative instrument", "development finance business", "factoring business", "home monetary authority", "Islamic banking business", "leasing business", "offshore bank", "offshore banking business", "offshore investment banking business" and "securities" have been inserted or amended due to new provisions being inserted to cover situations in which such terms are used.

3. At the moment section 4 of the Act prohibits any person from carrying on offshore banking business (which includes investment banking business) without a valid licence. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 to enable a licence to be granted for persons who are desirous of carrying on investment banking business only.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 of the Act to clarify—

(a) in paragraph (2)(ee) that an application for an offshore banking licence by a foreign offshore company needs only to be supported by an undertaking;

(b) in subparagraph (2)(ee)(ii) that a foreign offshore company who appplies for an offshore banking licence needs only to notify the Bank of any change in the percentage of participation of its participants.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 17—

(a) to require a licensed offshore bank to submit four copies of its audited annual financial reports instead of two as is the present requirement; and

<!-- page 11 -->

(b) to delete subsection (2) as the submission of annual financial reports approved by participants of a licensed offshore bank is not required by the Bank.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 18—

(a) in subsection (1), to provide for a greater flow of information, at such frequency specified by the Bank, from licensed offshore banks to the Bank; and

(b) in subsection (3), to allow the Bank to disclose any information which it obtains from a licensed offshore bank to the relevant home monetary authority of the licensed offshore bank.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 23 to allow the Bank to require the internal auditors or approved auditors of a licensed offshore bank which is a foreign offshore company to verify submissions made by the licensed offshore bank,

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 26 to extend the scope of indemnity provided in that section to the Director General and members of the Labuan Offshore Financial Services Authority.

9. Clause 10 seeks to introduce three new sections, namely, sections 33, 34 and 35.

Section 33 confers the status of exempt dealer on licensed offshore banks and provides that a representative of a licensed offshore bank dealing in securities need not be licensed as a dealer's representative.

Section 34 provides that the provisions of the Securities Industry Act 1983 shall not apply to transactions carried out by licensed offshore banks in certain circumstances.

Finally, section 36 provides for the internal audit and appointment of internal auditors of a licensed offshore bank.

10. Other amendments not specifically mentioned in this Statement are consequential and minor in nature.
