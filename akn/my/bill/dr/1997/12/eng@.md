## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Registration of Pharmacists Act 1951 ("Act").

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 3(1) of the Act to enlarge the composition of the Pharmacy Board ("Board"). Clause 2 also seeks to impose the condition that every member of the Board shall be a citizen of Malaysia. The term "citizen of Malaysia" shall not,

<!-- page 6 -->

by virtue of the proposed subsection 3(5), include any permanent resident of Malaysia.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 4(4) of the Act to increase the quorum for a meeting of the Board from five to nine members consequent upon the increase proposed in clause 2.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 13(7) of the Act to enable the various disciplinary punishments provided for in section 1 8A to be imposed on a body corporate. At present the only punishment provided for is the removal of its name from the register.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend paragraph 14(3)(b) of the Act to enable the various disciplinary punishments provided for in section 1 8A to be imposed on any executor, administrator or trustee of the deceased registered pharmacist or any person in the employment of the executor, administrator or trustee.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend subsection 17(1) of the Act to enable a distinction to be made between the removal of a person's name from the register as a form of disciplinary action and the removal of a person's name from the register due to his death, having permanently left Malaysia or unsoundness of mind or infirmity which renders him incapable of practising as a pharmacist.

7. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 18A. The new section 1 8A enables the Board at the conclusion of an inquiry to impose disciplinary punishment in the exercise of its diciplinary jurisdiction. The Board may at the conclusion of the inquiry order the person or body corporate whose misconduct is the subject of the inquiry, to pay costs to the Board or to the complainant where disciplinary punishment is imposed. The Board may also order the complainant to pay costs to the registered pharmacist or body corporate where no case has been made out against the registered pharmacist or body corporate. Such costs may be recovered as a civil debt.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 19 of the Act to allow those upon whom a punishment of removal, suspension or reprimand has been imposed, to appeal against such order. This amendment is consequential to the introduction of the proposed section 18A.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 22(1) of the Act by adding a new paragraph (ba). The new paragraph (ba) enables regulations to be made for the establishment of a Committee of Inquiry to inquire into any complaint or information which the Board is empowered to inquire into.

<!-- page 7 -->

10. Other amendments which are not specifically mentioned are consequential or minor.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 1902.]
