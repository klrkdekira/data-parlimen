<!-- marginal: Sabah -->

or he has been" the words "conferred the degree of Bachelor of Laws (Honours) or".

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend paragraph 4(l)(cc) of the Advocates Ordinance of Sabah to enable a person who has been conferred the degree of Bachelor of Laws (Honours) by the MARA Institute of Technology to be admitted as an advocate in Sabah.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure. [PN. (U2) 2010.]
