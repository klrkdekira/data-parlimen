## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Land Acquisition Act 1960 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for the short title to the Bill and its commencement and application.

<!-- page 28 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by introducing a few new definitions which are considered necessary, such as the definitions of "Committee", "development approval", "Economic Planning Unit" ("EPU"), "public utility", "sewerage system" and "valuer". The definition of "building" has been expanded to make its meaning more specific and clearer. The definition of "Commissioner" has been replaced by the definition of "State Director". The definition of "person interested" has been amended to exclude from its meaning a tenant at will.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of the Act. The existing section 3 is renumbered as subsection (I). Paragraph 3(l)(c) is amended to provide for an acquisition of land which is for recreational purposes or for a combination of any of the purposes provided therein. The amendment to section 3 also seeks to provide for the procedure in making an application for acquisition under paragraph 3(1)(b) or (c). Upon receipt of an application, the Land Administrator shall transmit it with the accompanying documents to the State EPU, or the Committee for the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur ("Committee for F.T.K.L."), in the case of an application made in the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur. The Land Administrator is empowered to reject any application which does not conform to the requirements provided in subsection (3). Under subsection (6), the Land Administrator shall also reject any application where in respect of the land to be acquired there is a development approval granted to the registered proprietor and the acquisition is not for the purpose of public utility. This is in view of the fact that the State Authority shall not consider any such application.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce the new sections 3A, 3B, 3C, 3D, 3E and 3F.

The proposed section 3A provides for the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L., as the case may be, to consider the application on the various aspects provided. Where there is a development approval granted in respect of the land and the acquisition is for the purpose of public utility, the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L. shall, if appropriate, give directions to the applicant to negotiate with the registered proprietor on the form of co-operation or commercial arrangement for the sharing of profits in the project. The applicant is then required to submit a report of the negotiations to the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L., as the case may be.

Where negotiations are successful then it shall not be necessary for the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L. to proceed further with the application for acquisition.

Where negotiations have been unsuccessful, the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L. may proceed to consider the application and make any recommendation as it deems fit.

<!-- page 29 -->

After the State EPU or the Committee for F.T.K.L. has considered each application under the section, it shall then transmit the application to the Committee, in the case of a State, or to the State Authority, in the case of the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur.

The proposed section 3B provides for the application of section 3A where there is no development approval in respect of the land to be acquired. Here, the power of the State EPU, or the Committee for F.T.K.L., to require the applicant to negotiate with the registered proprietor in the project is only discretionary in nature.

The proposed section 3C provides for the establishment of the Jawatankuasa Khas Pengambilan Tanah ("Committee").

The proposed section 3D provides for the evaluation of the application by the Committee, in the case of a State, after which it will be forwarded to the State Authority.

The proposed section 3E empowers the State Authority to approve or reject the application and for the applicant to be informed accordingly. It also provides that the State Authority shall not approve the application for the acquisition of land, for any purpose, larger in area than that needed for that purpose. Under subsection (3) the applicant is required to confirm his acceptance of the terms and conditions of the approval imposed by the State Authority within thirty days of the notification otherwise the applicant is deemed to have withdrawn the application.

The proposed section 3F enables the applicant to withdraw from the acquisition of any land before the publication of the declaration under section 8. It also covers the situation where the applicant is deemed to have withdrawn his application under subsection 3E(3). In all such cases, the Land Administrator shall conduct an enquiry to determine the amount of deposit to be forfeited for the payment of compensation to the persons interested.

6. Clause 5 seeks to substitute for the word "Commissioner" in subsection 5(1) the words "State Director".

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new subsection 8(5) to empower the Land Administrator to pay compensation to any person injured after the declaration under subsection (1) has lapsed on the expiry of two years after its publication in the Gazette.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend subsection 9(2) by deleting the words "In States other than the States of Penang and Malacca" as it is no longer necessary to differentiate the other States from Penang and Malacca in matters relating to the making of any note on any intended acquisition in the register.

<!-- page 30 -->

9. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 9A to the Act. This section is intended to enable the Land Administrator to obtain information from the State Director of Town and Country Planning or the Commissioner of the City of Kuala Lumpur, as the case may be, stating whether the scheduled land is within a local planning authority area and whether there is a development plan or land use indicated in the development plan for the scheduled land. The Director or the Commissioner, as the case may be, is required to submit the information within four weeks of the request being made by the Land Administrator. Accordingly, the local planning authority is given two weeks to provide the information to the Director. Subsection

(6) provides that non-compliance with the time period stipulated for the information to be obtained shall not invalidate the acquisition or the award. These amendments are related to the amendment to the First Schedule where a new subparagraph 1(2BA) will be introduced.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 12(1) by inserting a new proviso to provide that the Land Administrator may obtain a written opinion on the value of all scheduled lands from a valuer prior to making an award under section 14.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend subsection 14(5) to include valuation fees as part of the costs incurred for the proceedings before the Land Administrator.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 19 to empower the Land Administrator to issue a certificate of urgency in respect of an acquisition under paragraph 3(l)(b) where it is for public utility.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 20 to exclude the main access to a building on a scheduled land from being taken formal possession of by the Land Administrator under a certificate of urgency.

14. Clause 13 seeks to make an amendment to paragraph 23(a) in the same manner as is done in section 66 under clause 31 to provide for the vesting of a parcel of a subdivided building in the person or corporation on whose behalf the parcel has been acquired.

15. Clauses 14 and 15 seek to amend sections 24 and 27 to delete all references to a "deed" or "deeds evidencing title" since the registration of deeds system for Penang and Malacca is no longer applicable.

16. Clauses 16, 17 and 26 seek to amend subsection 29A(5) and sections 32 and 48 respectively to substitute for the word "interest" the words "late payment charges" wherever appearing.

17. Clause 18 seeks to introduce a new section 32A to provide for the meaning of "payment" for the purposes of sections 29A, 32 and 48.

<!-- page 31 -->

18. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 35 to provide that every withdrawal from acquisition shall be gazetted. It also introduces a new subsection (5) to allow the Land Administrator to forfeit from the deposit, where there is any withdrawal made under the section, an amount which is sufficient to defray the amount of costs and damages incurred by any person interested.

19. Clause 20 seeks to amend subsection 37(2) to preclude an objection being made to the Court where the total amount of compensation "awarded" does not exceed three thousand ringgit. The current provision provides that no objection may be made if the amount "claimed" does not exceed three thousand ringgit.

20. Clause 21 seeks to amend subsection 38(5) to extend the time limit from three months to six months for the Land Administrator to refer any objection to the Court.

21. Clause 22 seeks to amend subsection 39(1) to increase the amount of deposit required by the Land Administrator in a reference to the Court from a sum not exceeding one thousand five hundred ringgit or the amount of the award, whichever is the less, to a sum of three thousand ringgit or ten per cent of the amount claimed, whichever is the less.

22. Clause 23 seeks to introduce the new sections 40A, 40B, 40C and 40D.

The proposed section 40A provides for objection proceedings in regard to compensation to be heard by a Court consisting of the Judge and two assessors (one of whom shall be a valuation officer employed by the Government). The assessors shall be appointed by the Court from two lists, that is from the list of names of registered valuers and appraisers to be submitted annually to the Court by the President of the Board of Valuers, Appraisers and Estate Agents and from the list of names of valuers employed by the Government to be submitted annually to the Court by the Director General of the Valuation and Property Services Department.

The proposed section 40B provides for the legal duty of the two assessors to attend Court. An assessor who is not a valuation officer employed by the Government will be paid a fee not exceeding five hundred ringgit a day. If an assessor appointed from the list of names submitted under subsection 40A(4) fails to attend Court without written excuse by the Judge, the Court shall report the matter to the President of the Board of Valuers, Appraisers and Estate Agents for disciplinary proceedings to be taken against him. If an assessor who is a valuation officer employed by the Government fails to attend Court without written excuse by the Judge, the Court shall report the matter to the Director General of the Valuation and Property Services Department for disciplinary proceedings to the taken against the assessor under the appropriate disciplinary regulations applicable to such officer.

<!-- page 32 -->

The proposed section 40C provides that the opinion of each of the two assessors on the various heads of compensation shall be in writing and shall be recorded by the judge.

The proposed section 40D provides for the decision of the Court on compensation matters to be based on the amount decided by the two assessors. However, where the two assessors have each arrived at a different decision from each other then the Judge shall elect to concur with the decision of one of the assessors and the amount of compensation to be awarded shall be the amount decided upon by that assessor. Any decision made under this section is final and there shall be no further appeal to a higher Court on the matter.

23. Clause 24 seeks to amend section 45 by proposing to introduce a new subsection (1A) to provide for the provisions of the Third Schedule to apply to every proceeding under the Act. The new proposed subsection 45(1B) seeks to empower the Rules Committee to amend the provisions of the Third Schedule. The procedure in the Third Schedule is in fact the procedure being followed at present by virtue of Practice Direction 11 of 1987.

24. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 47 to provide that the decision of the Court shall also be signed by the two assessors in addition to the existing requirement that it should be signed by the Judge.

25. Clause 27 seeks to amend subsection 49(1) to substitute for the words "Supreme Court" the words "Court of Appeal and to the Federal Court" in view of the introduction of the Court of Appeal as an additional court for appeal. The amendment to the proviso to subsection (1) is a consequential amendment necessitated by the introduction of subsection 40D(3).

26. Clause 28 seeks to amend section 54 by introducing a new paragraph (c) to allow the Land Administrator to publish a notice under the Act in such local newspapers as he thinks fit.

27. Clause 29 seeks to amend section 63 to include a "Commanding Officer" as a "Chief Police Officer" to enable the Land Administrator to seek assistance from such person when taking possession of any land.

28. Clause 30 seeks to amend section 64 to insert a new paragraph

(a) and also to increase the penalty for obstruction from imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month or a fine not exceeding two hundred ringgit or both to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or a fine not exceeding five thousand ringgit or both.

30. Clause 33 seeks to amend the First Schedule.

Sub-subparagraph 1(1)(a) has been amended. The existing provision stipulates that if a notification under section 4 in respect of any scheduled land is followed within six months by a declaration under section 8, the date for the purpose of valuing the market value of the scheduled land is the date of the notification. The proposed amendment seeks to increase that period to twelve months so that it is consistent with the period specified in subsection 4(3),

A new subparagraph 1(1A) is proposed to be introduced to provide that in assessing the value of any scheduled land, the valuer may use any suitable method of valuation to arrive at the market value but regard may be had to the prices paid for the recent sales of land with similar characteristics situated within the same vicinity and that particular consideration must be given to the last transaction on the scheduled land within two years from the date with reference to which the scheduled land is to be assessed under subparagraph (1).

A new subparagraph 1(1B) is proposed to be introduced to provide that where only a part of the scheduled land is to be acquired, the market value shall be determined by reference to the whole land as shown in the document of title of the scheduled land and after having regard to the particular features of that part.

A new subparagraph l(lC) is proposed to be introduced to permit only the sales of land on or before the date of acquisition of any scheduled land to be taken into consideration in determining the market value of the acquired land. Any evidence of any sales transactions effected after the date of notification shall not be considered.

A new subparagraph 1(1D) is proposed to be introduced to provide that in assessing the market value of any scheduled

acquired which is held under a title for a period of years, regard may be had to the date of expiry of the lease as shown in the document of title, but regard may not be had to the likelihood of a subsequent alienation to the person or body who is the proprietor thereof immediately before the expiry of the lease.

Subparagraph 1(2A) is amended to provide that where a Malay reservation land, a Malay holding or customary land is to be acquired ("scheduled land"), the fact that it is such Malay reservation land, Malay holding or customary land shall not be taken into account in assessing the market value except where the scheduled land is to be devoted after the acquisition solely to a purpose for the benefit of persons who are eligible to hold such land under the relevant written law.

Subparagraph l(2B) is proposed to be deleted as it is now considered no longer necessary in view of the other amendments made to the Act.

A new Subparagraph l(2BA) is proposed to be introduced to provide that if the information submitted by the Slate Director of Town and Country Planning or the Commissioner of the City of Kuala Lumpur, as the case may be, indicates that the scheduled land is within a local planning authority area, then the land shall be assessed by having regard to the specific land use indicated for the land in the development plan.

A new Subparagraph l(2D) is proposed to be introduced to provide that in assessing the market value of any estate land, the market value shall not be affected by the fact that it can be sold to one person. This amendment is inserted in view of the decision made by the Supreme Court in the case of Kumpulan Sua Belong Sdn. Bhd. V. Ezan Sdn. A new Subparagraph 1(3A) is proposed to be introduced to provide that the value of any building on any land to be acquired shall be disregarded if that building is not permitted for that category of land use, or by an express or implied condition or restriction, to which the land is subject or deemed to be subject under the State land law.

Subparagraph 2(b) of the First Schedule is amended to insert the words ", which shall be deducted from the total compensation ," to make it consistent with the provisions of paragraph 4 7 ( 2 )( b) of the Act.

Subparagraph 2(d) of the First Schedule has also been amended to delete the words "or his actual earnings" since these words are unnecessary considering that the matter is already covered by the existing Subparagraph 2( e ).
