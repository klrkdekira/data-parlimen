## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1997 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1997 and the Supplementary Supply (1996 and 1997) Act 1997. [PN. (U2) 2023.]
