## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend section 4 of the Members of Parliament (Remuneration) Act 1980 to provide for an increment in the monthly allowances of the Deputy President of the Senate and the Deputy Speaker of the House of Representatives.

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which is estimated to be RM24,000 per annum. [PN. (U2) 2019.]
