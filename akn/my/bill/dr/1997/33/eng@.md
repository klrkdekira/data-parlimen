## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Central Bank of Malaysia Act 1958.

2. Clause 1 deals with the short title and commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clauses 2, 3, 4 and 5 seek to amend sections 16A, 30, 34 and 37 which are amendments consequential to the enactment of a separate Act on money-changing.
