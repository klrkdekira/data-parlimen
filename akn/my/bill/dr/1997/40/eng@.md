## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Judges' Remuneration Act 1971. [ ]

BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

<!-- marginal: Short title -->

1. This Act may be cited as the Judges' Remuneration

<!-- marginal: and commence- -->

(Amendment) Act 1997 and shall be deemed to have

<!-- marginal: ment. -->

come into force on 1 January 1995.

<!-- marginal: Amendment -->

2. The Judges' Remuneration Act 1971 is amended in

<!-- marginal: of section 7. -->

section 7 by substituting for the words "1/20 of his last

<!-- marginal: Act 45. -->

drawn salary for each completed month of his service" the words "seven and a half per centum of the amount arrived at by multiplying his total completed months of service as a Judge by his last drawn salary".

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend section 7 of the Judges' Remuneration Act 1971 so that "Pekeliling Perkhidmatan Bilangan 2 Tahun 1996" is also made applicable to judges.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained. [PN. (U2) 2004.]
