## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of thirty-two billion three hundred and ninety-two million and two hundred and sixty-eight thousand ringgit (RM32,392,268,000) for the service of the year 1998 and for that sum to be appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule. [PN. (U2) 2047.]
