## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The proposed Water Supply (Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur) Act 1997 ("the Act") seeks to provide for the law to be made applicable to the Federal Territory of Kuala Lumpur ("Federal Territory") on matters relating to the supply and distribution of water in the event that the water supply service is privatised both in the Federal Territory and in the State of Selangor.

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for the short title and commencement of the Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to provide that the law relating to the supply and distribution of water in the State of Selangor shall apply to the Federal Territory but with the necessary modifications.

4. Clause 3 seeks to provide that the authority responsible for the supply and distribution of water in the State of Selangor shall be the authority responsible, on behalf of the Government of the Federation, for such matters in the Federal Territory. However, this does not preclude the Government of the Federation from exercising on its own behalf, its powers and functions which have been conferred upon the authority in the State of Selangor by this Act.
