## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Securities Industry (Central Depositories) Act 1991 ("the Act").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for the short title and commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the definition of "security" in section 2 of the Act which is consequential to the amendment of section 84 of the Companies Act 1965 made by the Companies (Amendment) Act 1995.

This clause also seeks to insert a new definition of "unit trust scheme" into section 2 of the Act to define the expression which is used in the amended definition of "security".

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 14 of the Act to cater for the automatic prescription of additional issue of securities by an issuer.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 22 of the Act to reflect the fact that the withdrawal of securities are also governed by the rules of the central depository.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 34 of the Act to provide for the flexibility in the manner in which an issuer can request for the record of depositors from the central depository.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 35 of the Act. This amendment is consequential to the amendment of section 84 of the Companies Act 1965 made by the Companies (Amendment) Act 1995. After the amendment to section 84 of the Companies Act 1965, all unit trust schemes are regulated under the Securities Commission (Unit Trust Scheme) Regulations 1996.

<!-- page 7 -->

(Amendment)

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 38 of the Act to provide for situations involving an offer for sale of securities.

9. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 41A into the Act to ensure that the operations of the central depository are not affected by trust notices.

10. The amendment to clause 9 is necessary following the decision in the case of Repco Holdings Bhd. v. Public Prosecutor (1997).

11. Clause 10 seeks to introduce a new section 6 lA into the Act to enable the Commission to be represented in any civil proceedings by any officer of the Commission authorised by the Commission for such purpose.
