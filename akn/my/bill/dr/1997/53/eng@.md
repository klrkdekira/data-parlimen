## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Securities Industry Act 1983 (the "Act").

2. Clause I seeks to provide for the short title and commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the long title of the Act by deleting the word "stock brokers" as the term is now a defunct term.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by amending the definition of "exempt dealer" to enable the Minister to prescribe any person or class of persons as an exempt dealer. In addition, paragraph

(e) of the definition is amended consequential to the amendment of the definition of "fund manager".

Clause 3 also seeks to amend the definition of "fund manager" to rationalise the regulatory framework relating to fund management activities and consequential to the amendment of the definition of "futures fund management business" in the Futures Industry Act 1993 and "unit trust scheme" in the Securities Commission Act 1993.

The deletion of the definition of "Registrar" is proposed consequent to the rationalisation of the regulatory structure of the securities industry. Apart from this deletion, this Bill also seeks to remove all references to the Registrar in sections 2, 10, 33, 34, 35, 49, 50, 94A, 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100 and 126 of the Act.

Other amendments to section 2 of the Act proposed in clause 3 are—

(a) the amendment of the definition of "securities" to make it consistent with the definition of "securities" in the Securities Commission Act 1993;

(b) the deletion of the definition of "stock broker" is consequent to the removal of references to "stock brokers" from sections 2, 14, 39, 89 and 120 of the Act;

(c) the amendment of the definition of "stock market" to clarify the meaning of the term when used in the Act;

(d) the amendment of the definition of "unit trust scheme'' to make it consistent with the definition of "unit trust scheme" in the Securities Commision Act 1993; and

(e) the amendment of subparagraphs (2)(a)(i) and (iii) to include references to fund managers.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 14(2) of the Act to clarify that a fund manager does not require an investment adviser's licence when carrying on a business of giving investment advice.
