## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Rubber Industry (Replanting) Fund Ordinance 1952 ("the Ordinance").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 16 of the Ordinance to empower the Minister after consultation with the Minister of Finance to impose and collect a cess on rubber for replanting purposes.

4. Clause 6 seeks to introduce new Parts V and VI into the Ordinance.

## Part V contains provisions relating to enforcement, investigation,

evidence and offences.

## Part VI deals with general matters such as the compounding of

offences, institution of prosecution under the Act, jurisdiction to try offences under the Act and power to exempt.

5. Amendments which are not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.
