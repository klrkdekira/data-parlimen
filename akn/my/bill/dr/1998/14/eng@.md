## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 1998 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year. [ ]

BE IT ENACTED by the Seri Paduka Baginda Yang di-Pertuan Agong with the advice and consent of the Dewan Negara and Dewan Rakyat in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:

<!-- marginal: Short title. -->

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply (1998) Act 1998.

<!-- marginal: Further -->

2. (1) The issue is authorized of a sum not exceeding

<!-- marginal: expenditure -->

four hundred million ringgit (RM400,000,000) from the

<!-- marginal: for the year -->

<!-- marginal: 1998. -->

Consolidated Fund for expenditure on the services specified in the Schedule for the service of the year 1998, not

<!-- marginal: Act A1009. -->

provided for or not fully provided for by the Supply Act 1998.

(2) The said sum is appropriated for the purpose specified in the Schedule.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1998 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1998. [PN.(U2)2091.]
