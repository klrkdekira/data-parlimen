## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The proposed Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission Act 1998 ("the Act") seeks to establish a body corporate by the name of "Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission" ("the Commission") having power to supervise and regulate the communications and multimedia activities in Malaysia including to enforce certain legislations relating to communications and multimedia.
