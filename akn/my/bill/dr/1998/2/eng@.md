## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to repeal the Teachers' Superannuation Ordinance 1963 (Sabah) ("the Ordinance"), to dissolve the Teachers' Superannuation Fund and the Board of Management established under the Ordinance.

2. Clauses 3 and 4 provide for the dissolution of the Board and the Fund and for the devolution of powers, rights, privileges, duties, liabilities and obligations of the Board to the Government.

3. Clause 5 provides for the transfer of all immovable property and other assets of the Fund to the Government.

4. Clause 6 provides for the transfer of all moneys standing in, or due to be paid, to the Fund to the Government.

5. Clause 7 provides for the enforcement of all existing contracts by or against the Government in substitution of the Board.

6. Clause 8 provides that The State Treasurer of Sabah shall be responsible for the payment of pensions to the eligible persons.

7. Clause 9 provides for the payment of pension in a lump sum amount to a person who was, on 15 February 1983, an eligible person and the lump sum amount shall be paid out of the moneys referred to in section 6.

8. Clause 10 provides that any payment may be made under the proposed Act to the dependants of a deceased eligible person without probate or letters of administration.

9. Clause II provides that any payment which is made in good faith shall constitute a full discharge of all the liabilities of the Government under the Ordinance.

10. Clause 12 provides for the continuance of legal proceedings by or against the Government in substitution of the Board.

11. Clause 13 provides for the conferment of powers to the Minister to make orders for the purpose of removing any difficulties that may arise in connection with the implementation of the proposed Act.

(Sabah) (Dissolution) 12. Clause 14 provides for the cessation of all regulations made under the Ordinance which affect the Board and the Fund.
