## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Bank Pertanian Malaysia Act 1969.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Bank Pertanian Malaysia (Amendment) Act 1998.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

Amendment of section 10B

2. The Bank Pertanian Malaysia Act 1969 [Act 9], which in this Act is referred to as the "principal Act", is amended in section 10B—

(a) in subsection (3), by substituting for the words "Disciplinary Committee in respect of the General Manager" the words "disciplinary authority in respect of the General Manager shall be the Disciplinary Committee which";

(b) by substituting for subsections (8) and (9) the following subsections:

"(8) A decision of the Disciplinary Committee established under subsection (1) shall be appealable to the Disciplinary Appeal Board established under section 10D.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Bank Pertanian Malaysia Act 1969 ("Act 9").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for preliminary matters.

<!-- page 5 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 10B of Act 9 to provide for appeals to the Disciplinary Appeal Board or the Minister against the decision of the Disciplinary Committee.

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new section 10D into Act 9 to provide for the establishment of the Disciplinary Appeal Board. It also seeks to introduce a new section 1 OE to provide for the termination of service of an officer or employee of the Bank in the public interest.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 38A of Act 9 to provide that under certain circumstances the opportunity to make representations need not be given.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
