intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 1997 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year.

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply short title. (1997) Act 1998.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The object of this Bill is to give statutory authority for certain additional payments to be made for federal public services in the year 1997 in excess of the expenditure sanctioned by the Supply Act 1997, the Supplementary Supply (1996 and 1997) Act 1997, the Supplementary Supply (1997) Act 1997 and the Supplementary Supply (1997) (No. 2) Act 1997. [PN.(U2)2080.]
