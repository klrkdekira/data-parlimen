## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 ("the Act").

2. Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to amend section 2 of the Act by inserting a new definition of "licensed pharmacist" to mean a registered pharmacist who holds a Type A Poison Licence issued under the Poisons Act 1952. This definition is introduced for the purpose of the proposed amendment to subsection 16(2) of the Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 12 to increase the maximum fine for offences relating to the possession of dangerous drugs from twenty thousand ringgit to one hundred thousand ringgit. It also seeks to introduce a new subsection (4) which provides that public officers employed at any prison, rehabilitation centre, police lock- up or any place of detention who contravene the provisions of subsection 12(2) are also liable to whipping.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 16 of the Act by substituting for the words "person who is licensed to sell by retail poisons" the words "licensed pharmacist" which include manufacturing pharmacists who are usually not involved in any retail sale of poisons.

<!-- page 8 -->

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 37 to insert 14 types of Amphetamine Type Stimulants as dangerous drugs so that a person found in possession of any of such drugs in the specified amount will be presumed to be trafficking in the drug.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 38A to empower the Court to deal with a juvenile offender in accordance with the provisions of the Drug Dependants (Treatment and Rehabilitation) Act 1983.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 38B to require an offender found guilty under section 15 of the Act to undergo supervision by a Rehabilitation Officer or a police officer after having undergone the punishment imposed on him.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 39A to insert 14 types of Amphetamine Type Stimulants as dangerous drugs so that a person found to be in possession of any of such drugs in amounts which are less than those which can give rise to the presumption that he is trafficking in such drug will be subject to the increased penalty provided in that section.
