## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967, the Petroleum (Income Tax) Act J967 and the Real Property Gains Tax Act 1976.

## CHAPTER II

## AMENDMENTS TO THE INCOME TAX ACT 1967

2. Clause 4 seeks to delete section 123 of the Act to bring it in line with Clause 145(3) of the Federal Constitution.

3. Clause 6 seeks to amend Schedule 3 of the Act by deleting paragraphs 11 and HA. The rate of initial allowance applied to mining, timber and construction businesses will now be prescribed under paragraph 10 of the same Schedule. This amendment will come into force from the year of assessment 1998.
