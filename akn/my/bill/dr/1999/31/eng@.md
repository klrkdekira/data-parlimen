## PART I

## PRELIMINARY

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Income Tax (Amendment) (No. 2) Act 1999.

(2) This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2000 and, except as proyided in subsections (3), (4) and (5), shall have effect for the year of assessment 2001 and subsequent years of assessment.

(3) Subsection 107c(3) which is introduced by section 12 of this Act shall have effect for the year of assessment 2002 and subsequent years of assessment.

(4) Sections 13,14,15 and 16 shall have effect upon the coming into operation of this Act.

(5) Section 17 shall have effect only for the year of assessment 2001.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967 ("Act 53").

2. Clause / of the Bill contains the short title and provides for the commencement and effect of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to amend section 77 of Act 53. The new subsection 77(1A) provides that every company is required to furnish to the Director General a return in the prescribed form for each year of assessment within six months from the date the company closes its accounts. Where the accounts are not closed in any particular year and the company would not be able to comply with this requirement, the new subsectlon 77( 1 B) provides for the return to be furnished by the company together with the return for the year of assessment in which the accounts are closed and the period to do so would be within six months from the date of the close of the accounts. Following the change to the self-assessment system, the company is now required to show in the return a computation of its tax payable. In addition, the company must also provide in the return any information required of it. The new subsection 77(4) enables the Director General to allow a return to be furnished electronically. This amendment is effective from the year of assessment 2001.

4. Clause 3 of the Bill seeks to amend section 80 of Act 53. The amendment introduces a new subsection ( 1 A) to ensure that the Director General or an authorized officer who has entered or who proposes to enter any land, building or place in exercise of the powers under section 80 is provided with all reasonable facilities and assistance. This amendment is effective from the year of assessment

2001.

5. Clause 4 of the Bill seeks to amend section 90 of Act 53. The amendment introduces new subsections ( 1 A) and ( 1 B) to provide that the Director General is deemed to have made an assessment in respect of a company on the day the return is furnished to him. Subsection 90( 1B) deems the return to be a notice of assessment and also deems the notice of assessment to be served on the company on the day on which the Director General is deemed to have made the assessment. This amendment is effective from the year of assessment 2001.

6. Clause 9 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 103A into Act 53 to deal with the payment of tax by companies. Section 103A seeks to provide that a company is liable to pay the balance of tax payable (if any) by the due date, defined in this section as the last day of the sixth month trom the date following the close of the accounting period. The section also provides for an increase to the tax at the prescnbed rate in the event the original tax payable is not fully paid at the expiration of the prescribed period. This amendment is effective from the year of assessment 2001.

7. Clause 12 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 107c into Act 53 to provide that every company is required to furnish to the Director General an estimate of its tax payable in the prescribed form not later than thirty days before the beginning of its basis period. However, in the case of a company

<!-- page 13 -->

that has just commenced its operations in a particular year of assessment, the estimate of tax payable may be furnished within three months from the date of commencement of operations.

Section 107c also requires the company to pay the estimate of tax payable by the tenth day of every calendar month in equal monthly instalments beginning from the second month of the basis period for which the estimate is furnished. In the case of a new company, the payment shall be made by the tenth day of every calendar month in equal monthly instalments beginning from the sixth month of the basis period. The number of instalments that have to be paid by the company will be determined according to the number of months in the basis period for a year of assessment for the company.

Subsection 107c(3) provides that the estimate of tax payable shall not be less than the revised estimate or, if no revised estimate is furnished, the estimate of tax payable for the immediately preceding year of assessment. Subsection 107c(7) allows a company to furnish a revised estimate in the sixth month of the basis period.

Except for subsection 107c(3), this amendment is effective from the year of assessment 2001. Subsection 107c(3) is effective from the year of assessment 2002.

8. Clause 13 of the Bill seeks to amend section 112 of Act 53 to provide that the failure to furnish a return under the new subsection 77(1A) shall be an offence punishable under section 112. In addition, a new subsection 112(2A) is introduced to empower the court to further order a person convicted of an offence under subsection 112(1) to comply with the relevant provision of Act 53 that has been contravened. This amendment is effective upon the coming into operation of this Act.

9. Clause 14 of the Bill seeks to amend section 114 of Act 53. The amendment introduces a new subsection (1A) to provide that a person who assists in, or advises with respect to, the preparation of any return that results in an understatement of the liability for tax of another person shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine of not less than two thousand ringgit and not more than twenty thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or to both unless he satisfies the court that the assistance or advice was given with reasonable care. This amendment is effective upon the coming into operation of this Act.

10. Clause 15 of the Bill seeks to amend section 116 of Act 53. The amendment seeks to provide that the failure to provide reasonable facilities or assistance or both to the Director General or an authorized officer in the exercise of his powers under Act 53 shall be an offence punishable under section 116. The amendment also seeks to increase the fine that may be imposed under section 116 from a maximum fine of four thousand ringgit to a minimum fine of one thousand ringgit and a maximum fine of ten thousand ringgit. This amendment is effective upon the coming into operation of this Act.

<!-- page 14 -->

11. Clause 16 of the Bill seeks to amend section 120 of Act 53. The amendment seeks to provide that the failure to furnish an estimate under the new section 107c shall be an offence punishable under section 120. In addition, a new subsection 120(2) is introduced to empower the court to further order a person convicted of an offence under subsection 120(1) to comply with the relevant provision of Act 53 that has been contravened. This amendment is effective upon the coming into operation of this Act.

12. Clause 17 of the Bill is introduced to provide that for the year of assessment 2001 the estimate of tax payable furnished to the Director General under subsection 107c(2) of Act 53 shall not be less than the amount of tax payable for the year of assessment 1999. This amendment is effective only for the year of assessment 2001.

13. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.
