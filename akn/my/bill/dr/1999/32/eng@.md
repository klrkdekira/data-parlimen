## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Sales Tax Act 1972.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Sales Tax (Amendment) Act 1999.

(2) This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2000.

### Interpretation

2. For the purpose of this Act, "appointed date" means 1 January

2000.

### Amendment of section 2

3. The Sales Tax Act 1972 [Act 64), which in this Act is referred to as the "principal Act", is amended in section 2—

(a) by substituting for the definition of "manufacture" the following definition:

'manufacture" means—

(a) in relation to goods other than petroleum, the conversion by manual or mechanical means of organic or inorganic materials into a new product

(b) in relation to petroleum,

(b) by inserting after the definition of "person" the following definition:

' "petroleum" shall have the meaning assigned to it under section 2(1) of the Customs Act 1967;'.

### Substitution of section 16

4. The principal Act is amended by substituting for section 16 the following section:

16. Subject to this Act, sales tax shall be levied—

(a) on the sale value at the rate in force at the time at which the sales tax is due; or

(b) on the quantity of goods sold, used or disposed of, or imported, at the rate in force at the time at which the sales tax is due.".

### Amendment of section 17

5. Subsection 17(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting after the word "sold" the words "as well as the quantities of goods sold".

### Amendment of section 69

6. Subsection 69(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting after the words "licensed manufacturer" the words "or importer".

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

1. This Bill seeks to amend the Sales Tax Act 1972 ("Act 64").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for the short title and the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 defines "appointed date" for the purpose of the proposed Act to mean 1 January 2000.

<!-- page 5 -->

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 64 by extending the definition of "manufacture" to incorporate the manufacture of petroleum and to introduce a new definition for "petroleum".

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 64 to allow sales tax to be computed on the quantity of goods sold, used or disposed of, or imported, in addition to being computed on sale value.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 17(1) of Act 64 to require quantities of goods sold to be recorded in invoices to be issued.

7. Clauses 6 and 7 seek to amend subsections 69(1) and 70(1) of Act 64 respectively. The amendments seek to require liquidators and receivers of importers of goods including petroleum, for which sales tax are payable, to give notice to the Director General of Customs within fourteen days of their appointment, to set aside a sufficient sum out of any assets to pay sales tax before disposing of such assets and to pay the sales tax payable. Currently, only liquidators and receivers of licensed manufacturers are subject to these obligations.

8. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new Part XVIII to Act 64 to deal specially with petroleum. The new Part XVIII introduces new sections 91 to 95. Section 91 states that Act 64 applies to petroleum subject to sections 92 to 95. Section 92 empowers the Minister to make an order varying, in respect of petroleum, the period for sales tax to be due and payable and the taxable period as well as to attach conditions to such variations. Section 93 permits any person to obtain delivery of imported taxable petroleum from the control of Customs even if any part or all of the sales tax on such goods have not been paid. Section 94 does not permit drawback in respect of petroleum. Section 95 provides that for the purpose of petroleum the special provisions in Parts XIV and XVII relating to Labuan and Langkawi in Act 64 are to be of no effect, and petroleum in Labuan and Langkawi will be subject to the rest of that Act.

9. Clause 9 deal.s with the transitional provisions for petroleum that are subject to excise duties under the Excise Act 1976 when the proposed Act comes into operation on 1 January 2000.
