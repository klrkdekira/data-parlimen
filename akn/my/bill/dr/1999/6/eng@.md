## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Sales Tax Act 1972 ("Act 64").

2. Clause 1 seeks to provide for the short title and commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 64 to introduce the definition of "surcharge" into that Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 64 to require an officer of sales tax to produce on demand a badge or an authority card as he has been directed to carry under subsection 9(1) of the Customs Act 1967.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce a new subsection 7(3) into Act 64 to provide for the sale value of goods where the goods are manufactured on behalf of any supplier of taxable goods from taxable goods supplied by that supplier and where the goods so manufactured are returned to that supplier.

6. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 12A into Act 64 which provides that if the Director General is satisfied that any separation of business activities is artificial, he may direct that the persons named in the direction be treated as a single taxable person.

<!-- page 20 -->

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new subsection (4) into section 13A of Act 64 which provides that before the Director General revokes a licence under subsection 13A(2), he shall serve a notice of revocation of licence on the person to whom the licence was issued.

Clause 6 also seeks to introduce new subsections (5) and (6) into section 13A of Act 64 to require the Director General, before he revokes a licence under subsection 13A(3), to allow representation to be made by the person to whom the licence was issued. If after considering the representation the Director General is satisfied that the licence should be revoked, he shall serve upon that person a notice of revocation of the licence.

8. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 1 7A into Act 64 to provide for the production of invoices by computer.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection 23(1) of Act 64 so that any surcharge accruing under Act 64 may be recovered as a civil debt due to the Government. Clause 8 also seeks to introduce a new subsection 23(3) into Act 64 to provide that a penalty or surcharge imposed under Act 64 will not be subject to a limitation period of one year from the date on which the cause of action accrued.

This clause also seeks to introduce new subsections (4) and (5) into section

23. The new subsection (4) deals with the amount of sales tax that is recoverable from a taxable person who issued an invoice. The new subsection (5) enables the Director General to recover sales tax from persons who have issued an invoice charging an amount which purports to be sales tax.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend paragraph 24(b) of Act 64 to increase the rate of penalty payable from two per cent to ten per cent.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 26 of Act 64 to make the directors of a company, partners of a firm or members of an association of persons, together with the company, firm or association of persons liable to pay a penalty or surcharge, jointly and severally liable for the penalty or surcharge.

12. Clause 11 seeks to introduce a new section 27A into Act 64 to enable any sales tax, penalty, surcharge or other moneys payable under Act 64 to be recovered from any person who is believed to be about to leave the Federation before paying any sales tax, penalty, surcharge or other moneys payable by him.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend subsection 28(1) of Act 64 to provide that where any surcharge is due and payable by a taxable person, the Director General may by notice in writing require any person owing money to the taxable person to pay such moneys to him.

14. Clause 13 seeks to amend subsection 30(1) of Act 64 to provide for the recovery of penalties that are unpaid, short paid or erroneously refunded. This clause also seeks to amend subsection 30(lB) of Act 64 to provide for the imposition of surcharge if any one of the instalment payments authorized under this section is not paid on its due date.

<!-- page 21 -->

15. Clause 14 seeks to introduce a new section 31B into Act 64 to allow a licensed manufacturer to deduct sales tax from the amount payable in a tax return for which credit notes have been issued for taxable goods returned by the purchaser or discounts subsequently given to the purchaser.

16. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 32 of Act 64 to empower the Director General to reject an application for the refund of sales tax or penalty that has been overpaid or erroneously paid on the grounds that it would unjustly enrich the claimant.

17. Clause 16 seeks to introduce two new subsections, namely subsections (2A) and (2B), into section 36 of Act 64 to allow any senior officer of sales tax the right to be given access to any recorded information or computerized data, whether stored in a computer or otherwise, and to inspect and check the operation of any computer which is or has been in use in connection with that information or data.

18. Clause 17 seeks to amend paragraph 43(a) of Act 64 which provides that any person who fails or refuses to comply with section 12A or section 1 7A is guilty of an offence. This clause also seeks to insert a new paragraph (ea) into section 43 which provides that the contravention of subsection 63A(2) of Act 64 is an offence.

19. Clause 18 seeks to introduce a new section 43A into Act 64 which provides that any person who assists any other person to evade sales tax commits an offence.

20. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 44 of Act 64 to provide that any person who aids, abets, assists, incites or induces any other person to commit an offence under section 43 is guilty of an offence.

21. Clause 20 seeks to amend subsection 57(1) of Act 64 to increase the maximum amount which can be accepted for the compounding of offences from one thousand ringgit to five thousand ringgit.

22. Clause 21 seeks to introduce a new section 59A into Act 64 to empower the courts to order any person found guilty of an offence under section 43 or 43A to pay to the Director General the amount of any sales tax due and payable, or any penalty payable under Act 64.

23. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 61 of Act 64 to empower the Minister to make regulations with regard to the production of invoices by computer and credit notes.

24. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 62 of Act 64 to provide for the service of notices, directions or other documents on companies, firms and associations of persons, apart from individual persons.

25. Clause 25 seeks to introduce a new section 63A into Act 64. That section provides that other than the persons stated in section 63, no person can transact business on behalf of a taxable person except on matters with regard to refund,

<!-- page 22 -->

remission, exemption or any other matters as approved by the Director General. This clause also sets out certain conditions that need to be fulfilled by a person who transacts business on behalf of the taxable person.

26. Clause 26 seeks to amend section 69 of Act 64 to provide that a liquidator of a company commits an offence if he fails to set aside and pay the sales tax payable by the company.

27. Clause 27 seeks to amend section 70 of Act 64 to make a receiver of a licensed manufacturer guilty of an offence if he fails to set aside and pay the sales tax payable by the licensed manufacturer.

28. Other amendments not specifically referred to are minor or consequential in nature.

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
