## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Excise Act 1976.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows;

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Excise (Amendment) Act 1999.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette, and the Minister may appoint different dates for different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Excise Act 1976[Act 176], which in this Act is referred to as the "principal Act", is amended in subsection 2(1)—

(a) by inserting after the definition of "intoxicating liquor" the following definition:

' "licensed carrier" has the meaning assigned by section 2(1) of the Customs Act 1967;'; and

(b) by substituting for the definition of "petroleum" the following definition:

' "petroleum" has the meaning assigned by section 2(1) of the Customs Act 1967;'.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Excise Act 1976 ("Act 176").

2. Clause I seeks to provide for the short title and the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 2(1) of Act 176 to provide for the definition of "licensed carrier" and to amend the definition of "petroleum".

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 176 to require an officer of excise to produce, on demand, a badge or an authority card as he has been directed to carry under subsection 9 ( 1) of the Customs Act 1967.

5. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section 15A into Act 176 to enable any excise duty payable under the Act to be recovered from any person believed to be leaving Malaysia before paying the excise duty payable by him.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 23A of Act 176 to provide that the liquidator of a company commits an offence against the Act if he fails to set aside and pay the excise duty that is payable by the company.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 23B of Act 176 to provide that the receiver of the property of a company commits an offence against the Act if he fails to set aside and pay the excise duty that is payable by the company.

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 42 of Act 176 to provide for the service of notices or other documents on companies, firms and association of persons, apart from individual persons.

9. Other amendments not specifically referred to in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
