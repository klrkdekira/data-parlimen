## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Service Tax Act 1975.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Service Tax (Amendment) Act 2001.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Service Tax Act 1975 [Act 151], which in this Act is referred to as the "principal Act", is amended in section 2—

(a) by renumbering the existing section as subsection (1) of that section;

(b) in subsection (1)—

(i) in the definition of "person", by substituting for the words "an association of persons" the words "a society, an association"; and

(ii) by substituting for the definition of "exported taxable service" the following definition:

' "exported taxable service"—

(a) in relation to the provision of insurance policies for the coverage of the international transportation of goods, means where the

<!-- page 2 -->

insurance policies are provided to cover risks relating to the transportation of goods—

(i) from a place outside Malaysia to a place outside Malaysia;

(ii) from a place within Malaysia to a place outside Malaysia; or

(iii) from a place outside Malaysia to a place within Malaysia,

and includes the provision of insurance policies to cover risks relating to the transportation of goods within Malaysia that forms part of the transportation referred to in subparagraphs (ii) and (iii) where the coverage is provided by the same person;

(b) in relation to the provision of export credit insurance policies to local exporters, banks and investors, means where the export credit insurance policies are provided to cover risks outside Malaysia relating to the export of goods, services and investments; and

(c) in any other case, means any taxable service supplied for and to a person in a country other than Malaysia, provided that the service is not supplied in connection with goods or land situated in Malaysia and the person is not in Malaysia at the time the service is performed;'; and

(c) by inserting after subsection (1) the following subsection:

"(2) For the avoidance of doubt it is declared that notwithstanding subsections 1(1) and 1(1A) and without affecting section 2A, for the purposes of the definition of the expression "exported taxable service" Labuan, Langkawi, the Joint Development Area and the free zones shall be deemed to be places outside Malaysia.".

### New section 37A

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Service Tax Act 1975 ("Act 151").

2. Clause 1 of the Bill contains the short title.

3. Clause 2 of the Bill seeks to amend the definition of "exported taxable service" in subsection 2(1) of Act 151 to clarify the position in relation to the provision of insurance policies for the coverage of the international transportation

<!-- page 8 -->

of goods and the provision of export credit insurance policies. The clause also seeks to insert a new subsection 2(2) to clarify the position of Labuan, Langkawi, the Joint Development Area and the free zones for the purposes of that definition.

4. Clauses 3 and 12 of the Bill seek to amend section 10A and paragraph 4 1( c c) of Act 151 respectively to make provision for receipts produced by computer.

5. Clause 4 of the Bill seeks to amend subsection 15(3) of Act 151 to provide that a penalty or surcharge imposed under Act 151 shall not be subject to the one year period of limitation prescribed under the Limitation Ordinance of Sabah and the Limitation Ordinance of Sarawak respectively.

6. Clause 5 of the Bill seeks to amend section 17 of Act 151 to provide that if a company, a firm, a society, an association or other body of persons, as the case may be, is liable to pay service tax or penalty, then the directors of the company, the partners of the firm or the members of the society, association or other body of persons shall, together with the company, firm, society, association or other body of persons, be jointly and severally liable for the service tax or penalty payable. However, in the case of a company that is being wound up, the directors of such company shall only be so liable where the assets of the company are insufficient to meet the amount due, after paying any sums having priority under the Companies Act 1965 in relation to the application of the assets of the company in such winding up over the service tax or penalty.

7. Clause 6 of the Bill seeks to delete subsections 24(3) and 24(4) from Act 151 as access to recorded information and computerized data is to be dealt with under the new section 26A.

8. Clause 7 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 26A into Act 151 to enable a proper officer exercising his powers under sections 24, 25 and 26 of Act 151 to gain access to recorded information or computerized data.

9. Clause 8 of the Bill seeks to amend section 28 of Act 151 to provide for the release of a person arrested under Act 151 from custody on his depositing such reasonable sum of money as the proper officer may require or on his executing a bond, with such surety or sureties as the proper officer may require, or both.

10. Clause 10 of the Bill seeks to delete section 36 of Act 151 as the authorization for the conduct of prosecutions is to be dealt with under section 377 of the Criminal Procedure Code.

11. Clause 11 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 37A into Act 151 to impose a duty to keep the name and address of an informer and the substance of the information received from an informer secret. A person who contravenes this requirement shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding ten thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to both.

<!-- page 9 -->

12. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.
