## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Excise Act 1976. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Excise (Amendment) Act 2001.

### Amendment of section 42

2. The Excise Act 1976 [Act 176], which in this Act is referred to as the "principal Act", is amended in paragraph 42(2)(a) by substituting for the words "firm or association of persons" the words "a firm, a society, an association or other body of persons".

### New section 53A

3. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 53 the following section: " Access to 53A. (1) Any officer of excise exercising his powers recorded under sections 51, 52 and 53 shall be given access to any informa- tion or recorded information or computerised data, whether stored compute- in a computer or otherwise. rised data.

<!-- page 2 -->

(2) In addition, an officer of excise exercising his powers under sections 51, 52 and 53—

(a) may inspect and check the operation of any computer and any associated apparatus or material which he has reasonable cause to suspect is or has been used in connection with that information or data; and

(b) may require— (i) the person by whom or on whose behalf the officer of excise has reasonable cause to suspect the computer is or has been so used; or (ii) the person having charge of, or is otherwise concerned with, the operation of the computer, apparatus or material,

to provide him with such reasonable assistance as he may require for the purposes of this section.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), "access" includes being provided with the necessary password, encryption code, decryption code, software or hardware and any other means required to enable comprehension of recorded information or computerised data.".

### Amendment of section 55

4. Subsection 55(1) of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (b), by inserting after the words "67(4)" the words ", or in the event of such amount being forfeited under section 69, as the case may be"; and

(b) in paragraph (c), by inserting after the word "claim" the words ", or a forfeiture under section 69, as the case may be".

### Amendment of section 56

5. Section 56 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in the proviso to subsection (1), by substituting for the words "a female" the words "another female with strict regard to decency"; and

### New section 63A

8. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 63 the following section: " Obligation 63A. (1) Except as provided under section 64, the name of secrecy. and address of an informer and the substance of the information received from an informer shall be kept secret and shall not be disclosed by any proper officer or any person who in the ordinary course of his duties comes into possession of or has control of or access to such information to any person except the designated officer of excise authorised by the Director General.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Excise Act 1976 ("Act 176").

2. Clause 1 of the Bill contains the short title.

3. Clause 3 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 53A into Act 176 to enable an officer of excise exercising his powers under sections 51, 52 and 53 of Act 176 to gain access to recorded information or computerised data.

4. Clause 4 of the Bill seeks to amend subsection 55(1) of Act 176 to allow the amount secured or the proceeds of sale held under that section to be paid to the Director General if such amount or proceeds of sale are forfeited under section 69 of Act 176.

5. Clause 5 of the Bill seeks to amend section 56 of Act 176 to provide for the release of a person arrested under Act 176 from custody on his depositing such reasonable sum of money as the proper officer of excise may require or on his executing a bond, with such surety or sureties as the proper officer of excise may require, or both.

<!-- page 6 -->

6. Clause 6 of the Bill seeks to delete section 57 of Act 176 as the authorisation for the conduct of prosecutions is to be dealt with under section 377 of the Criminal Procedure Code.

7. Clause 7 of the Bill seeks to amend section 60 of Act 176 to reduce the percentage of goods required to be examined in a proportional examination from ten per centum to five per centum and to provide that if it is necessary to test any goods seized under Act 176, it shall be sufficient to test only a sample not exceeding five per centum of the goods so examined.

8. Clause 8 of the Bill seeks to introduce a new section 63A into Act 176 to impose a duty to keep the name and address of an informer and the substance of the information received from an informer secret. A person who fails to comply with this requirement shall be guilty of an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding ten thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

9. Clause 9 of the Bill seeks to amend section 69 of Act 176 to provide for the forfeiture of the amount secured under paragraph 55(1)(a) or 55(1 )( b) of Act 176 or of the amount realised by sale under paragraph 55(l)(c) of Act 176 when an offence is compounded.

10. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL

## IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
