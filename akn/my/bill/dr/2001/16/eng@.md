## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Registration of Businesses Act 1956 ("Act 197").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to introduce various definitions including the definition of Companies Commission of Malaysia and to state that the Registrar is the Chief Executive Officer of the Commission.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of Act 197. The new section 3 seeks to empower the Companies Commission of Malaysia to appoint Deputy Registrars, Assistant Registrars, inspectors and other officers for the office of the Registrar of Businesses.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 197 to provide for simpler provisions in relation to the requirements for the registration of business.

6. Clause 7 seeks to introduce new sections 5A, 5B, 5C, 5D, 5E and 5F into Act 197.

New section 5A seeks to provide for the renewal of the registration of a business.

New section 5B deals with changes in the particulars registered in respect of any associate of a business or in respect of the business.

New section 5c seeks to empower the Registrar to refuse to register any business and to revoke the registration of any business if the Registrar is satisfied that the business is likely to be used for unlawful purposes or any purpose prejudicial to or incompatible with the security of the Federation, public order or morality.

New section 5D deals with the termination of a business.

New section 5E deals with the removal of a registered business from the register.

New section 5F provides that a certificate of registration containing certain specific information is conclusive that the requirements of Act 197 have been complied with and that the business is duly registered.

7. Clauses 8 and 9 seek to amend sections 9 and 10 respectively of Act 197 to, inter alia, so that the powers presently exercisable under those sections by an inspector can also be exercised by the Registrar, Deputy Registrars and Assistant Registrars.

8. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 11 of Act 197 to enable aggrieved persons to appeal to the Minister against decisions of the Registrar under section 5, 5A, 5B, 5C, 5D or 5E.

9. Clause 11 seeks to introduce a new section 1 1A into Act 197 to require the display of the certificate of registration. Currently, this requirement is found in section 5, which contains provisions on registration.

10. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 12 of Act 197 to increase the penalties for offences in subsections 12(1) and (2). In subsection 12(1) of Act 197 the maximum term of imprisonment is increased to two years from the exisiting six months whilst subsection 12(2) imposes a term of imprisonment not exceeding six months. The fines are increased to fifty thousand ringgit and two thousand ringgit from the existing one thousand ringgit and two hundred ringgit, respectively.

11. Clause 13 seeks to introduce a new section 12A into Act 197. The new section provides for a general penalty for offences under the Act or rules made under the Act for which no penalty is expressly provided for.

12. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 13 of the Act to provide that prosecution for offences under the Act may only be instituted by or with the consent in writing of the Public Prosecutor.

13. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 14 of Act 197 which deals with the compounding of offences, so that the procedure is similar to that provided for in more recent laws.

14. Clause 17 seeks to introduce a new section 18A into Act 197. The new section deals with the service of notices, certificates or order documents required to be given or served under Act 197.

15. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 19 of Act 197 to allow any person to obtain a copy of a certificate of registration.

16. Clause 20 seeks to introduce a new section 22A into Act 197 to enable documents required under Act 197 to be electronically filed or lodged with the Registrar.

17. Clause 21 deals with transitional matters relating to certificates of registration and appeals from the decision of the Registrar.

18. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.
