## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 (''Act 452").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 26B of Act 452. This amendment seeks to widen the meaning of "securities issued by the Government of Malaysia" to include negotiable securities and instruments which are fully guaranteed by the Government of Malaysia and Bank Negara Malaysia and negotiable securities and instruments issued by a corporation which is fully owned by the Government of Malaysia or Bank Negara Malaysia.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 44 of Act 452. With this amendment, the Board may receive any funds from any retirement scheme or plan relating to an employee remitted by any other person or the employee's employer.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 54 of Act 452. Generally, this amendment seeks to enable a member of the Fund to withdraw a part of the amount standing to his credit in the Fund to purchase or build a second house, to finance his higher learning and to purchase a personal computer for himself. This amendment also seeks to empower the Minister, if he thinks it expedient and after consulting the Board, by order published in the Gazette, to add to, vary or modify the purposes for which a member of the Fund may make a withdrawal under subsection 54(6) of Act 452.

6. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section, namely section 54c, into Act

452. This amendment seeks to enable the Board to authorize the withdrawal of all or part of the amount of money standing to the credit of the member of the Fund who is a pensionable employee if the member has been required to retire or had elected to retire under sections 10, 11, 12 and 12A of the Pensions Act 1980 or sections 10, 11, 12 and 13 of the Statutory and Local Authorities Pensions Act 1980.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend Part VIA of Act 452 to correct the sequence of provisions in the Act.

8. Other amendments not specifically mentioned are minor or consequential in nature.
