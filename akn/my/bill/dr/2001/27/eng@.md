## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Code. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2001.

(2) Subject to subsection (3), this Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code ("Act 593").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

<!-- page 7 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to replace the title of "Medical Superintendent" with "Medical Director" in line with the creation of the post of Medical Director by the Mental Health Act 2001, and to provide that the expression "Medical Director" will include a "Deputy Medical Director". This amendment is consequential to the enactment of the Mental Health Act 2001.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 108A to delete the reference to section 108.

5. Clauses 4,6, 7,5,9 and 10 are amendments consequential to the abolition of preliminary inquiries and trials by jury.

6. Clauses 11,12,13,14,15,16,17,18,19and 21 are amendments consequential to the enactment of the Mental Health Act 2001.

1. Paragraphs 20(a), (b),(c) and(d) contain amendments consequential to the proposed amendments to the Penal Code which will incorporate into it offences currently existing in the Women and Girls Protection Act 1973 [Act 106] which will be repealed when the Child Act 2001 [Act 611 ] comes into force. Paragraphs 20(e) and (f) contain amendments consequential to the proposed amendments to the Penal Code which will create the offence of incest.
