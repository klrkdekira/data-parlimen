## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Banking and Financial Institutions Act 1989.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Banking and Financial Institutions (Amendment) Act 2001. (2) This Act shall come into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 43

2. The Banking and Financial Institutions Act 1989 [Act 372] is amended in subsection 43(2) by deleting paragraph (b).

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Banking and Financial Institutions Act 1989 ("Act 372"). The proposed amendment in this Bill is an amendment consequential to the amendments made to the Central Bank of Malaysia Act 1958.
