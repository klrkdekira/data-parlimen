## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 2002 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year.

### [

### ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply (2002) (No. 2) Act 2002.

### Further expenditure for the year 2002

2. The issue is hereby authorised of a sum not exceeding three hundred and fifteen million eight hundred and three thousand eight hundred and thirty ringgit (RM315,803,830) from the Consolidated Fund for expenditure on the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2002, not provided for or not fully provided for by the Supply Act 2002 [ActA1135], the Supplementary Supply (2001 and 2002) Act 2002 [Act A1147] and the Supplementary Supply (2002) Act 2002 [Act A1156].

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of three hundred and fifteen million eight hundred and three thousand eight hundred and thirty ringgit (RM315,803,830) for the services and purposes as specified in the Schedule for the year 2002.
