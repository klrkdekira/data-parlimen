## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 2003 and to appropriate that sum for the service of that year. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supply Act 2003.

### Issue and appropriation for the service of the year 2003

2. The issue of a sum not exceeding fifty-five billion three hundred and eighty-six million two hundred and forty-four thousand and eight hundred and fifty ringgit (RM55,386,244,850) from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 2003 is hereby authorised, and that sum is appropriated for purposes specified in the Schedule.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of fifty-five billion three hundred and eighty-six million two hundred and forty-four thousand and eight hundred and fifty ringgit (RM55,386,244,850) for the service of the year 2003 and for that sum to be appropriated for the purposes specified in the ScheduLe.
