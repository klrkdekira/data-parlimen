## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Labuan Trust Companies Act 1990 ("Act 442").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 442 by deleting the definition of "carry on business" and introducing the definitions of "director" and "trust company business".

4. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 442 to provide that only companies incorporated or registered as offshore companies under the Offshore Companies Act 1990 ("Act 441") may be registered as trust companies. The amendment to subsection (2) is to require a trust company to place security deposit with the Registrar instead of the Accountant-General and to allow, as an alternative, trust companies to obtain prbfessional indemnity insurance coverage. Under the amendment proposed to subsection (3) and the proposed new subsection (4) a trust company which is not a trust company managed by another trust company, must have a place of business in Labuan.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend subsection 5(2) of Act 442 to require all trust companies to pay the prescribed fees to the Registrar on or before 15 January in each year. The fees are prescribed by regulations made under Act 442.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 442 to require trust company to obtain consent from the Registrar if the trust company intends to open any office or acquire or establish any subsidiary elsewhere outside Labuan. This is contained iri the new prbposed subsection (4).

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 7 of Act 442 to require all trust companies to comply with the provisions of the'Offshore Companies Act-1990.

<!-- page 15 -->

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 8 of Act 442 to allow a trust company to provide services to residents but only for purposes permitted under the Offshore Companies Act 1990. At present, trust companies can only provide services to non-resident.

9. Clause 11 seeks to introduce new subsections (5), (6) and (7) into section 10 of Act 442 to provide for the cancellation of registration of a trust company upon the request of the trust company itself.

10. Clause 12 seeks to introduce a new section 1 1A into Act 442 to enable a trust company whose business is to be transferred to another trust company, and the trust company whom the business is to be transferred, to apply to the court for an order which will enable their agreement or arrangement to be given effect to.

11. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 23 of Act 442 to provide for new provisions relating to secrecy.

12. Clause 14 seeks to introduce new sections 28 and 29 into Act 442. The new section 28 confers on the Minister the power to exempt any person from any provision of Act 442. The new section 29 deals with the non-applicability of several existing provisions of the Offshore Companies Act 1990 to a trust company.

13. Clauses 15 and 16 contain savings and transitional provisions.

14. Other amendments not specifically referred to in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.
