## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967 ("Act 53"), the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2002 ("Act A1151"), the Stamp Act 1949 ("Act 378") and Labuan Offshore Business Activity Tax Act 1990 ("Act 445").

## AMENDMENTS TO THE INCOME TAX ACT 1967

## Chapter II of this Bill seeks to amend the Income Tax Act 1967.

2. Clause 4 seeks to delete paragraph 6(l)(g) of Act 53 to enable an approved operational headquarters company be exempt from tax.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

<!-- page 20 -->

20 Bill

3. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 7 of Act 53. Section 7 is a provision to determine residence status of an individual. Where an individual is in Malaysia in the basis year for a period of less than 182 days and that period is linked by or to a period of 182 or more consecutive days, that individual is a resident for that basis year. The proposed amendment allows the individual to be temporarily absent during the period that links the two periods.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2002 and subsequent years of assessment.

4. Clause 6 seeks to delete section 11 of Act 53. With the deletion of section 11, occupation of premises for non-business purposes do not constitute a source of income.

Clauses 9, 10 and 21 seek to delete sections 31, 37 and 128 of Act 53 respectively and clause 22 seeks to amend subsection 131(2) of Act 53. These amendments are consequential to the deletion of section 11 of Act 53.

These amendments shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

5. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 15A of Act 53. With this amendment, the derivation scope for services referred to under paragraphs15A(a) and (b) of Act 53 are limited only to those services which are performed in Malaysia.

This amendment shall have effect from 21 September 2002.

6. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 18 of Act 53. The proposed amendment seeks to introduce a new definition of "licensed Malaysian offshore bank" as a consequence of the amendment to section 60c of Act 53.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment

7. Clause 1] seeks to amend section 38 of Act 53 as a consequence of the amendment of section 37.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

8. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 60c of Act 53. The amendment seeks to exclude the gross income, adjusted income or adjusted loss and statutory income attributable to an offshore business activity of a licensed Malaysian offshore bank from the banking business in Malaysia being chargeable to tax under Act 53.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

<!-- page 21 -->

9. Clause 13 seeks to delete section 60E of Act 53. With the amendment, an approved operational headquarters company can now be exempt from tax by an exemption order made under section 127 of Act 53. .

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

10. Clauses 14 and 15 seek to amend section 75 of Act 53, and introduce a new section 75A into Act 53 respectively. The new section 75A is introduced in order to make any director and any other person who is concerned in the management of the company's business and, either on his own or with one or more associates within the meaning of subsection 139(7), is the owner of, or able directly or through the medium of other companies or by any other indirect means to control, more than fifty per cent of the ordinary share capital of the company, jointly and severally liable for the tax due and payable by the company.

This amendment shall have effect from the coming into force of this Act.

11. Clause 17 seeks to introduce a new section 82A. Prior to the proposed amendment, the duty to keep records is only applicable to a person who carries on a business. The new section requires every person to keep records for a period of seven years from the end of a year of assessment for ascertaining his chargeable income and tax payable.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment,

These amendments shall have effect from 21 September 2002.

13. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 108 of Act 53. A new subsection (1A) is introduced to determine the rate of tax deducted when distributing dividend in respect of a company which has a paid up capital of less than two million five hundred thousand ringgit.

This amendment shall have effect for the year of assessment 2003 and subsequent years of assessment.

A new subsection (16) is introduced to allow the Director General to remit the amount increased under subsection (7), (9) or (10) of section 108 of Act 53.
