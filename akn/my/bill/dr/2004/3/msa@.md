### "Setiausaha Tribunal Rayuun dan pegawai lain

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 ("Act 588").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend paragraph 4(2)(b) of Act 588 to clarify that the application of Act 588 does not cover prospective facilities or service providers.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend the definition of "network facilities provider" in section 6 of Act 588 to be in line with section 126 of the Act.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 588 so that regulations made by the Minister can provide for additional penalty if an offence is continued after conviction.

6. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 23A into Act 588 to facilitate the enforcement of decisions of the Appeal Tribunal.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 24 of Act 588 to remove the requirement of a determination by the Minister under Chapter 2 of Part II of the Act in respect of Appeal Tribunal procedures.

8. Clause 7 seeks to introduce a new section 24A into Act 588 which sets out the powers of the Appeal Tribunal in performing its functions under the Act.

9. Clause 8 seeks to introduce new sections 26A, 26B, 26C, 26D and 26E into Act 588. The proposed section 26A seeks to empower the Minister to designate a public officer as the Secretary of the Appeal Tribunal. He is also empowered to designate other public officers to assist the Secretary. The proposed section 26B seeks to impose an obligation of secrecy on the Chairman, members and officers of the Appeal Tribunal. The proposed section 26c deems the Chairman, members and officers of the Appeal Tribunal to be public servants and public officers for the purposes of the Penal Code and Criminal Procedure Code, respectively. The proposed section 26D extends the protection in the Public Authorities Protection Act 1948 to the Appeal Tribunal, the Chairman and members and officers of the Appeal Tribunal in respect of any action, suit, prosecution or proceedings against them for any act, neglect or default done or committed in carrying out their respective duties under Act 588. The proposed section 26E seeks to provide protection for the Appeal Tribunal, the Chairman, officers of the Appeal Tribunal and other persons authorized to act for and on behalf of the Appeal Tribunal against any action or suit for any act or omission done in good faith.

10. Clauses 9 and 10 seek to amend subsections 30(9) and 34(2) of Act 588 respectively so that the Minister's decision under those subsections will not have to go through the process of "determination" under Chapter 2 of Part II of the Act.

18. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 204 of Act 588 to enable any person, other than the licensees under the Act, to contribute to the Universal Service Provision Fund.
