## HURAIAN

Rang Undang-Undang ini bertujuan untuk meminda Akta Racun Makhluk Perosak 1974 ("Akta 149").

2. Fasal 1 mengandungi tajuk ringkas dan peruntukan bagi tarikh permulaan kuat kuasa Akta pindaan yang dicadangkan.

3. Fasal 2 bertujuan untuk meminda seksyen 2 Akta 149 untuk memasukkan takrif baru dan meminda takrif bagi istilah tertentu yang digunakan dalam Akta.

4. Fasal 3 bertujuan untuk meminda seksyen 3 Akta 149 dengan menambah keahlian Lembaga kepada empat belas orang ahli, dan meminda gelaran bagi jawatan yang dipcgang oleh ahli-ahli Lembaga yang sedia ada supaya menepati gelaran semasa bagi jawatan sedemikian.

5. Fasal 4 bertujuan untuk meminda subseksyen 6(4) Akta dengan menambah kuorum yang diperlukan bagi suatu mesyuarat Lembaga kepada tujuh orang ahli.

6. ## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Pesticides Act 1974 ("Act 149").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provisions on the date of commencement of the proposed amendment Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 149 to introduce new definitions and to amend definitions of certain terms used in the Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 3 of Act 149 by increasing the membership of the Board to fourteen members, and amending the designation of the posts held by the existing members of the Board to conform with the current designation for such posts.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 6(4) of the Act by increasing the quorum required for a meeting of the Board to seven members.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 7 of the Act to require a person who intends to register a pesticide to submit to the Board a sample of the pesticide, in addition to the requirements presently provided for under that section. This clause also requires an application to register a pesticide to be accompanied with the prescribed application fee.

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new subsection (3A) into section 8 of Act 149 to enable the Board to issue guidelines regarding the classes of pesticides under which pesticides are to be classified.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 9 of Act 149 to increase the period of registration of a pesticide to five years.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 10 of Act 149 to empower the Board to cancel the registration of a pesticide if the Board considers that the pesticide has adverse effects on animals, plants, fruits or property.

10. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new section 10A into Act 149 to require a person on whose application a pesticide was registered to inform the Board if he discovers any adverse effects of the pesticide on animals, plants, fruits or property.

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 13 of Act 149 to enhance the penalties for offences under that section. Similar amendments are proposed to sections 14, 20, 53 and 56 of the Act in clauses 12.. 16, 23 and 25, respectively.

12. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 14 of Act 1.49 to require a person who intends to import a pesticide as a registration sample or an analytical standard to apply for a permit.

13. Clause )3 seeks to introduce a new section 14A into Act 149 to require a person who intends to carry out an experiment or research outside the laboratory on an unregistered pesticide synthesized in Malaysia to apply for a permit.

<!-- page 32 -->

14. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 33 of Act 149 to provide that an authorized officer who is not in uniform must, on demand, declare his office and produce his certificate of identity to the person against whom he is acting.

3 5. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 35 of Act 149 to enable a part of a sample of the substance required to be analysed to be sent to the Analyst by courier service.

16. Clause 19 seeks to introduce a new section 35A into Act 149, providing for the time within which shall be commenced the analysis of a substance that has been purchased, taken or obtained from a person under the Act.

17. Clause 20 seeks to delete section 44 of the Act since the provisions as proposed in clause 19 are sufficient to ensure that the analysis of a substance is done within a reasonable time.

18. Clause 21 seeks to introduce two new sections into Act 149, namely sections 45A and 45B. Section 45A provides that where the label on a package containing a substance describes the substance as a pesticide, it is presumed that the label is evidence of that substance being a pesticide. Under the proposed section 45B, a pesticide found in any premises licensed for the sale or storage for sale of pesticides is presumed to be intended for sale or is being stored for sale.

19. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 49 of Act 149 to require a person convicted of an offence against the Act or the rules or regulations made under the Act relating to a pesticide to bear the cost of disposal of the pesticide.

20. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 50 of Act 149 to confer on a Court of a First Class Magistrate the jurisdiction to try any offence against the Act or the rules or regulations made under the Act, and to impose the full penalty for such offence.

21. Clause 25 seeks to introduce a new section 53A into Act 149. The proposed section prohibits the possession and use of unregistered pesticides and unapproved use of pesticides.

22. Clause 27 seeks to amend section 57 of Act 149 to enable the Minister to make rules or regulations regarding the export of pesticides.

23. Clause 28 seeks to amend the First Schedule of Act 149 to clarify that the substances listed in that schedule are active ingredients.

24. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are amendments which are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
