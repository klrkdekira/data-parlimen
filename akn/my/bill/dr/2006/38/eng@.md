### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act 2006.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### New sections 10A and 10B

9. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 10 the following sections:

### "Holding out as a co-operative society

10A. (1) No person shall hold himself out as a co-operative society, or represent that he or any other person is carrying on the business of a co-operative society in any form or manner unless he or such other person, as the case may be, is registered under this Act.

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding five million ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to both.

### Prohibition of the use of the word "koperasi", etc.

10B. (1) No person other than a co-operative society shall trade or carry on any business or dealing under any name or title of which the word "koperasi", "koperatif, "co-operative society", or "co-op", or any derivative of these words in any language, or any other words in any language capable of being construed as indicating the carrying on of such business or dealing, in relation to the business or dealing or any part of the business or dealing carried on by the person, or make any representation to such effect in any bill head, letter paper, notice, advertisement or in any other manner whatsoever.

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding five million ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to both and shall in addition, be liable to a daily fine of five thousand ringgit for each day during which the offence continues after conviction."

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Co-operative Societies Act 1993 ("Act 502") pursuant to the proposed Malaysia Co-operative Societies Commission Bill 2006 (MCSC Bill).

1. Clause 1 contains the short title and the power of the Minister to appoint the commencement date of the proposed Act.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend the long title of Act 502 to provide for the co- operative values of honesty, trustworthiness and transparency as the guiding principles of the co-operative society movement. The objective of the Act is also extended to include contributing towards achieving the socio-economic objectives of the nation.

3. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 502 to introduce new definitions and to amend definitions of certain terms used in the Act.

4. Clause 5 seeks to delete section 3 of Act 502 as the functions of the Registrar General and Registrar will be taken over by the Malaysia Co-operative Societies Commission established under the proposed MCSC Bill.

5. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 502 to reduce the minimum number of co-operative society members from one hundred to fifty.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 502 to prescribe different requirements for the registration of primary, secondary and tertiary society.

7. Clause 8 is a consequential amendment as the function of the Registrar is taken over by the Commission.

8. Clause 9 seeks to introduce new sections 10A and 10B into Act 502. The new section 10A makes it an offence for any person, unless registered, to hold himself out as a co-operative society or represent that he or any person is carrying on the business of a co-operative society in any form or manner.

The new section 10B replaces section 89 of Act 502 and makes it an offence for any person other than a co-operative society to use the words "koperasi", "koperatif, "co-operative society" or "co-op".

9. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 12 of Act 502 by substituting for the words "places of business" the word "branches" which carries wider meaning under the proposed Act.

10. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 13 of Act 502 to ensure closer supervision on co-operative society and to promote transparency in the management of co- operative society. It also makes it an offence for failure to comply with the provision of this section.

11. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 14 of Act 502 by deleting the requirement for the minutes of Board meetings to be lodged with the Commission to reduce the lodging of unnecessary documents with the Commission. These minutes are

<!-- page 54 -->

only to be submitted when required by the Commission. A penalty provision is inserted for the non-compliance of this requirement.

12. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 17 of Act 502. The new subsection (2) is inserted to empower the Commission, at any time, to require the by-laws of co- operative society to contain provisions on secrecy or permitted disclosures.

13. Clause 14 seeks to introduce a new section 17A into Act 502 for the purpose of streamlining all co-operative society by-laws with Act 502.

14. Clause 15 seeks to amend subsection 18(4) of Act 502, which provides that any appeal against the Commission's refusal to register any amendment of the by- laws may be made to the Minister within three month from the date of the refusal.

15. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 18A of Act 502 to empower the Commission to direct a co-operative society to convene a general meeting to amend any inconsistent by-laws upon receiving notification made by its Board under subsection 17A(3) of Act 502.

16. Clause 77 seeks to substitute section 19 of Act 502 with a new provision to monitor the formation of subsidiaries of co-operative society and the movement of funds in any form from the co-operative society to their subsidiaries. It also makes it an offence for failure to comply with the provision of this section.

17. Clause 18 seeks to introduce a new section 19A into Act 502 to require a co- operative society to obtain prior written approval of the Commission for opening of branches in or outside Malaysia. The proposed new section would enable the Commission to monitor the opening of branches of co-operative society. A penalty provision is inserted for non-compliance of this section.

18. Clause 19 seeks to delete section 20 of Act 502 to allow members to dispose off produce other than through co-operative society.

19. Clauses 20, 21 and 22 seek to amend sections 22, 23 and 24 of Act 502 to insert the word "return" in the provisions of these sections to cater for Islamic transactions carried out by any co-operative society.

20. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 25 of Act 502 by substituting for the word "minor" the word "child" to streamline Act 502 with the Child Act 2001 [Act 611].

21. Clause 24 seeks to delete section 29 of Act 502 to remove the current restriction on members to join more than one co-operative society that provide credit to its members.

22. Clause 25 seeks to amend the shoulder note of section 31 of Act 502 by substituting for the word "Representation" the word "Voting" as the latter word is more appropriate in explaining the contents of the provision.

<!-- page 55 -->

23. Clause 26 seeks to amend section 32 of Act 502 to emphasize the legality of a contract entered by a child who is a member of a co-operative society.

24. Clause 27 seeks to introduce a new section 34A into Act 502 to delimit the liability of a member of a co-operative society to the nominal value of his subscription of shares. Where in the by-laws of a co-operative society the amount of the liability of a member is expressed to be greater than the nominal value of any shares held or subscribed for by him, then the liability of the member shall extend to such greater amount.

25. Clause 28 seeks to amend section 35 of Act 502 to tie the liability of a past member of a co-operative society to the limitation provided in section 34A.

26. Clause 29 seeks to amend section 36 of Act 502 to empower the Commission to issue directives or guidelines in relation to the ultimate authority of a co-operative society.

27. Clause 30 seeks to amend section 37 of Act 502 to empower the Commission to issue directives if it wishes to exclude certain co-operative society from the requirements of this section.

28. Clause 31 seeks to substitute section 38 of Act 502 with a new provision to enable the inaugural general meeting of a co-operative society to have the powers similar as in an annual general meeting of a co-operative society.

30. Clause 34 seeks to substitute section 42 of Act 502 with a new provision for the purpose of deleting the requirement of appointing an Internal Audit Committee at the annual general meeting.
