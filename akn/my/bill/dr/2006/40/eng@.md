### Application for customs ruling

10A. (1) Any person may apply, in the prescribed form together with the prescribed fee, to the Director General for a customs ruling in respect of any one or more of the following matters:

(a) the classification of goods;

(b) the principles to be adopted for the purposes of determination of value of goods; or

(2) An application under subsection (1) may be made —

(a) in respect of imported goods —

(i) at any time before the goods, that are the subject matter of the application, are to be imported or intended to be imported into Malaysia; or

(b) in respect of manufactured goods —

(i) at any time before the goods that are the subject matter of the application, are to be manufactured; or

(ii) at any later time, if the Director General may in his discretion permit.

(3) An applicant may withdraw his application at any time before a customs ruling is made and any payment made relating to the application for the customs ruling shall be forfeited by the Director General.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Customs Act 1967 ("Act 235").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the power of the Minister to appoint the commencement date of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 235 to introduce the definition of "customs ruling" into that section.

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce a new Part IIA into Act 235 that provides for an application for a customs ruling, the making of the ruling and its applicability. This clause allows the Director General to amend, modify or revoke a customs ruling. The Director General is also empowered to declare a customs ruling to be null, void and of no effect if the ruling has been obtained by way of fraud, misrepresentation or falsification of facts. An applicant is not allowed to hold two or more different customs rulings on a same subject matter.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 135 of Act 235 to specify the minimum fines that can be imposed for offences committed under that section.

6. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new Part XIVA into Act 235. Amongst others, this clause provides for the establishment of the Customs Appeal Tribunal ("Tribunal") and appointment of its members. This clause also provides for the jurisdiction, sitting and powers of the Tribunal.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 142 of Act 235 to empower the Minister to make regulations in respect of customs rulings.

<!-- page 18 -->

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 143 of Act 235 to enable for any person aggrieved by the decision of the Director General, other than decisions relating to compound, to appeal to the Tribunal.

9. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor and consequential in nature.
