EXPLANATORY STATEMENT This Bill seeks to amend

the Registration of Engineers Act 1967 ("Act 138").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 138 to further provide in respect of the functions of the Board. In the proposed clause 4(l)(ef), the Board may appoint a body to advise the Government and the public on matters relating to engineering education, including the certification of such programmes.

4. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 7A of Act 138 by increasing the penalty which can be imposed by the Board on a sole proprietorship, partnership or body corporate which practises as an Engineering consultancy practice who fails to comply with the provisions of the Act.

5. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new Part IIIA that deals with the establishment, powers and conduct of proceedings of a Disciplinary Committee. Under the new Part IIIA, the Disciplinary Committee is empowered to conduct a hearing of any misconduct or complaint made against any registered Engineer and the Disciplinary Committee may make an order under section 15 against the registered Engineer.

<!-- page 10 -->

The new subsection 14c has been introduced to ensure that no element of bias exists against a registered Engineer or an Engineering consultancy practice of which he is a sole proprietor, partner, director, shareholder or employee, when a hearing involving any misconduct or complaint against that registered Engineer is being conducted. The new subsection 14c provides that where a member of the Board has been appointed as a member of the Disciplinary Committee to conduct a hearing against a registered Engineer, that member of the Disciplinary Committee shall not sit as a member of the Board when it conducts a hearing under section 7A against an Engineering consultancy practice of which that registered Engineer is a sole proprietor, partner, director, shareholder or employee.

6. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 15 of Act 138 by replacing the Board with the Disciplinary Committee as being the body empowered to make orders against a registered Engineer who is found guilty under any of the circumstances stated in paragraphs (a) to (o) of that section. The offences stipulated in paragraph (a) are extended to include an offence involving the false or negligent certification by a registered Engineer. Under the proposed amendments, the Disciplinary Committee may impose an order that includes an increased fine of fifty thousand ringgit and an increased period of suspension of registration of two years.

7. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 17 of Act 138 to further provide that the Board may reinstate the registration of a registered Engineer, other than a Graduate Engineer, or Engineering consultancy practice who fails to renew his or its registration, upon paying the prescribed fees and satisfying such conditions as may be determined by the Board.

8. Clauses 14 and 16 seek to enhance the penalties that may be imposed under sections 24 and 25, respectively, of the Act. Section 24 of the Act is amended by increasing the fine for offences committed under that section to fifty thousand ringgit, while the general penalty for offences committed under subsection 25(1) of the Act is enhanced to ten thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or to both.

9. Clause 19 contains saving and transitional provisions.

10. Any amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement involve amendments that are consequential or minor in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
