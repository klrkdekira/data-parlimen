## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2006.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment)(Amendment) Act 2007.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister under subsection 1(2) of the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2006 [Act A1274].

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2006, which is referred to as the "principal Act" in this Act, is amended in paragraph 2(b), in the English language text, in the definition, by substituting for the words "youth offender" the words "youthful offender".

28A. (1) A person arrested without a warrant shall be informed as soon as may be of the grounds of his arrest by the police officer making the arrest.

(2) A police officer shall, before commencing any form of questioning or recording of any statement from the person arrested, inform the person that he may —

(a) communicate or attempt to communicate, with a relative or friend to inform of his whereabouts; and

(b) communicate or attempt to communicate and consult with a legal practitioner of his choice.

(3) Where the person arrested wishes to communicate or attempt to communicate with the persons referred to in paragraphs (2) (a) and (b), the police officer shall, as soon as may be, allow the arrested person to do so.

(4) Where the person arrested has requested for a legal practitioner to be consulted, the police officer shall allow a reasonable time —

(a) for the legal practitioner to be present to meet the person arrested at his place of detention; and

(b) for the consultation to take place.

(5) The consultation under subsection (4) shall be within the sight of a police officer and in circumstances, in so far as practicable, where their communication will not be overheard.

(6) The police officer shall defer any questioning or recording of any statement from the person arrested for a reasonable time until the communication or attempted communication under paragraph 2(b) or the consultation under subsection (4) has been made.

(8) The requirements under subsections (2), (3), (4), (5), (6) and (7) shall not apply where the police officer reasonably believes that-

(a) compliance with any of the requirements is likely to result in-

(i) an accomplice of the person arrested taking steps to avoid apprehension; or

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2006 (Act A1274).

2. Clause 1 of the Bill contains the short title and provision on the commencement of the proposed Act. This Act is intended to come into operation simultaneously with Act A1274.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend paragraph 2(b) of the English language text of Act A1274 to replace the definition of "youth offender" with "youthful offender".

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 of Act A1274 to correct the sections referred to in the amendment.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 of Act A1274. This amendment seeks to substitute for the words "Third Schedule" the words "Fourth Schedule" since the deletion of the Third Schedule in the Criminal Procedure Code [Act 593] by the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act 2002 [Act A1132] has not come into operation.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 7 of Act A1274 by renumbering section 28A of Act 593 into subsections for ease of reference and also to correct the usage of punctuation marks.

<!-- page 8 -->

7. Clause 6 seeks to substitute section 10 of Act A1274 to correct the usage of the punctuation mark of full stop throughout subsection 107(3) of Act

593.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 14 of Act A1274 to insert paragraphs 32(0 and (j) of the Evidence Act 1950 [Act 56] into subsection 113(4) of Act 593 as they were inadvertently omitted.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 27 of Act A1274 to refine the meaning of the "community service" in subsection 293(1) of Act 593.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend paragraph 33(e) of Act A1274 to insert the words "Fine of that have been omitted in the item relating to section 225B in the First Schedule of Act 593 in the substitution of fine with a new penalty.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 34 of Act A1274 to substitute "Third Schedule" with "Fourth Schedule" as a consequential amendment to clause

4.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
