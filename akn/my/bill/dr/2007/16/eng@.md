## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Explosives Act 1957.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1 . ( 1 ) This Act may be cited as the Explosives (Amendment) Act 2007.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette, and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### New sections 4A, 4B, 4C and 4D

4. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 4 the following sections:

### "Prohibition on manufacture of unmarked plastic

### explosives

4A. Any person who manufactures any unmarked plastic explosive shall, on conviction, be liable to imprisonment for five years, or to a fine of fifty thousand ringgit, or to both.

### Prohibition on possession and transfer of possession of

### unmarked plastic explosives

4B. (1) Subject to subsection (2), any person who possesses or transfers possession of any unmarked plastic explosive shall, on conviction, be liable to imprisonment for five years, or to a fine of fifty thousand ringgit, or to both.

(2) Subsection (1) shall not, for the relevant period, apply to any unmarked plastic explosive which is manufactured in or imported into Malaysia before the appointed day and which is possessed or transferred during the relevant period b y -

(a) an authorized military or police personnel in the course of his duty; or

(b) any other person authorized by the Minister in accordance with this Act and any regulations made under this Act.

(3) For the purposes of this section —

"appointed day" means the day section 4 of the Explosives (Amendment) Act 2007 [Act A ] comes into operation;

"relevant period" means —

(a) a period of fifteen years from the appointed day in the case of an authorized military or police personnel; or

(b) a period of three years from the appointed day in the case of any other person authorized under paragraph (2)(b).

### Prohibition on importation and exportation of unmarked

### plastic explosives

4c. ( 1 ) Subject to subsection (2), any person who imports or exports any unmarked plastic explosive shall, on conviction, be liable to imprisonment for five years, or to a fine of fifty thousand ringgit, or to both.

(2) Subsection (1) shall not, for the relevant period, apply to the import or export by an authorized military or police personnel in the course of his duty of any unmarked plastic explosive which has been manufactured in or imported into Malaysia before the appointed day.

(3) If any vehicle, vessel or aircraft is used for the import or export of any unmarked plastic explosive in contravention of this section or for the receipt or storage of any unmarked plastic explosive imported or exported in contravention of this section—

(a) the owner, master or captain of the vehicle or vessel or the owner or pilot-in-command of the aircraft shall, on conviction, be liable to imprisonment for five years, or to a fine of fifty thousand ringgit, or to both unless it is proved to the satisfaction of the court that the said owner, master, captain or

<!-- page 7 -->

pilot-in-command was not implicated in the placing of the unmarked plastic explosive on board the vehicle, vessel or aircraft and that the offence in question was committed without his knowledge, consent or connivance; and

(b) the vehicle, vessel or aircraft may be detained by order of the court until security has been given for such sum as the court orders, not exceeding fifty thousand ringgit.

(4) For the purposes of this section—

"appointed day" means the day section 4 of the Explosives (Amendment) Act 2007 [Act A ] comes into operation;

"relevant period" means a period of fifteen years from the appointed day in the case of an authorized military or police personnel.

### Report of possession of plastic explosives

4D. (1) Any person possessing any plastic explosive on the appointed day shall report to the Inspector General of Police within one hundred and twenty days after such appointed day —

(a) the quantity of such plastic explosives possessed;

(b) the manufacturer or importer of such plastic explosives;

(c) any marks of identification on such plastic explosives; and

(d) such other information as the Minister may by regulations prescribe.

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) shall, on conviction, be liable to imprisonment for five years, or to a fine of ten thousand ringgit, or to both.

(3) For the purpose of this section, "appointed day" means the day section 4 of the Explosives (Amendment) Act 2007 [Act A ] comes into operation.".

EXPLANATORY STATEMENT This Bill

seeks to amend the Explosives Act 1957 ("Act 207").

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 207 to introduce definitions for certain expressions used in Act 207.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 207 to extend the scope of the provision to the exportation of explosives and to enhance the fines from ten thousand ringgit to fifty thousand ringgit.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce new sections 4A, 4 B, 4 C and 4 D into Act 207. The new sections are to give effect to the Convention on the Marking of Plastic Explosives for the Purpose of Detection concluded in Montreal on 1 March 1991.

The new section 4A prohibits any person from manufacturing any unmarked plastic explosive while the new section 4B prohibits any person from possessing or transferring possession of any unmarked plastic explosive. The new section 4c prohibits any person from importing or exporting any unmarked plastic explosive. Certain exceptions are provided for unmarked plastic explosives that have been imported into or manufactured in Malaysia before the appointed day.

<!-- page 11 -->

The new section 4D seeks to require all persons possessing any plastic explosive on the appointed day to report to the Inspector General of Police within one hundred and twenty days after such appointed day the quantity of such plastic explosives possessed, the manufacturer or importer of such plastic explosives, any marks of identification on such plastic explosives, and such other information as the Minister may by regulations prescribe.

6. Clauses 5 and 6 seek to amend sections 11 and 14 of Act 207 respectively to enhance the fines imposed.

7. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 26 of Act 207 to empower the Minister to make regulations to regulate the marking of plastic explosives.

8. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 27 of Act 207 to empower the Minister, on application in writing, to exempt by order published in the Gazette any unmarked plastic explosive, from the application of Act 207, that is held in limited quantities for research, development, testing and training purposes or for forensic science activity or that has been incorporated as an integral part of an authorized military device and is possessed or transferred in the course of his duty by an authorized military or police personnel or a member of a visiting force lawfully present in Malaysia.

9. Clause 11 seeks to amend the Schedule and to introduce a new Second Schedule into Act 207 to set out the substances that are to be used as detection agents in plastic explosives for the purposes of Act 207.

10. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
