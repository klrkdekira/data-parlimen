## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 2007 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year.

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply (2007) Act 2007.

### Further expenditure for the year 2007

2. The issue is hereby authorized of a sum not exceeding nine billion seven hundred eighty-six million seven hundred forty-three thousand five hundred and ten ringgit (RM9,786,743,510) from the Consolidated Fund for expenditure on the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2007 not provided for or not fully provided for by the Supply Act 2007 [Act A1276].

### Supplementary Supply (2007)

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of nine billion seven hundred eighty-six million seven hundred forty-three thousand five hundred and ten ringgit (RM9,786,743,510) for the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2007.
