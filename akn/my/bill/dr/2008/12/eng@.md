## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Universities and University Colleges Act

1971. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Universities and University Colleges (Amendment) Act 2008.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette, and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Universities and University Colleges Act 1971 [Act 30], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 2—

(a) by substituting for the definition of “Campus” the following definition:

‘ “Campus”, in relation to a University or University College, means the Campus or Branch Campus of the University or University College;’;

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) by substituting for the definition of “Minister” the following definition:

‘ “Minister” means the Minister charged with the responsibility for higher education;’;

(c) by inserting after the definition of “Minister” the following definition:

‘ “registrable offence” has the meaning assigned to it under the Registration of Criminals and Undesirable Persons Act 1969 [Act 7];’;

(d) by substituting for the definition of “student” the following definition:

‘ “student” means a registered student, other than a student at an institution allied to the University or University College, who is following a course of study, instruction, training or research of any description at the preparatory, under-graduate, post-graduate or post-doctoral level on a full time or part-time basis in, by or from the University or University College, and includes a distance-learning, off-campus, exchange and non-graduating student;’; and

(e) by substituting for the definition of “University or University College education” the following definition:

‘ “University or University College education” means education provided by a University or University College at the preparatory, under-graduate, post- graduate or post-doctoral level and includes research and educational development and the commercialization of such research and development;’.

### “ Suspension of student charged with registrable offence

### and matters relating to detention, etc.

15d. (1) Where a student of the University is charged with a registrable offence—

(a) he may, in the discretion of the Vice-Chancellor, be suspended from being a student; and

(b) if so suspended, he shall not during the pendency of the criminal proceedings, remain in or enter the Campus.

(2) Where a student of the University charged with a registrable offence under subsection (1) is convicted of that offence, the student shall be liable to disciplinary action.

(3) Where a student of the University is detained or is subjected to any order imposing restrictions on him under any written law relating to preventive detention or internal security, the student shall be liable to disciplinary action.

(4) A student of the University who is detained or is subjected to any order imposing restrictions on him under any written law relating to preventive detention or internal security, or is imprisoned or detained for whatever reason, may, with the consent of the Senate, be permitted to sit for the examination of the University subject to such order made or approval given by the Minister responsible for internal security or any other competent authority, as the case may require.

(5) A student of the University who is suspended from being a student of the University under subsection (1) may, while he is so suspended, be admitted as a student of any

University established under this Act with the written approval of the Minister, and if the Minister grants such approval, the Minister may impose such terms and conditions as he thinks fit.

(6) A student of the University who ceases to be a student under this Act may be admitted as a student of that or any other University established under this Act with the written approval of the Minister, and if the Minister grants such approval, the Minister may impose such terms and conditions as he thinks fit.

(7) If the determination of any application, appeal or other proceedings by the court in respect of any criminal proceedings against a student of the University for a registrable offence, results in his discharge or acquittal, the student, if suspended from the University or has served any period of imprisonment, as the case may be, shall be allowed to resume his studies at the University and the period of suspension or imprisonment, as the case may be, shall not be taken into consideration in calculating the maximum duration permitted to complete the course of study and in the computation of his results.

(8) If the determination of any application, petition, appeal or other proceedings by the court or any competent authority in respect of the detention order against or order imposing restrictions on a student of the University under any written law relating to preventive detention or internal security, results in his release or the restrictions imposed on him being revoked, the student shall be allowed to resume his studies at the University and the period of detention and restriction he was subjected to shall not be taken into consideration in calculating the maximum duration permitted to complete the course of study and in the computation of his results.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Universities and University Colleges Act 1971 (“Act 30”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the power of the Minister to appoint the commencement date of the proposed Act.

<!-- page 31 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 30 to introduce new definitions and to amend existing definitions used in the Act.

The defintion of “Campus” is redrafted to include Branch Campus.

The definition of “Minister” is substituted for the purpose of clarification to reflect the creation of two Ministries on education, namely Ministry of Education and Ministry of Higher Education.

A new definition of “registrable offence” is essentially intended to replace the words “criminal offence” used in Act 30.

The definition of “student” is amended to include students who are following courses of instruction, practical training and research in, by or from the University, whether part-time or full time and, whether by conventional methods or through distance learning.

The substitution of the definition of “University or University College education” seeks to expand the scope of the term to include research and educational development and commercialization of such research and development whether or not they lead to the award of a degree, diploma, or certificate.

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce new sections 4a and 4b into Act 30. Section 4a enables the Minister to appoint a committee to advise him on qualified and suitable person for the appointment of Vice-Chancellor or any other post to which he has the power to appoint under the Act. The appointment of such committee is to promote transparency in the selection process for the said posts. Section 4b empowers the Minister to second or transfer a University employee to the service of any requesting University, statutory authority or organization in Malaysia, subject to the consent of the said employee. If there is any claim for compensation arising out of the said secondment or transfer, the claim shall be met by the requesting party.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 30 to ensure that no higher educational institution with the status of a University is established except in accordance with the provisions of Act 30 or the Private Higher Educational Institutions Act 1996 (“Act 555”).

6. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new subsection (1 a) into section 6 of Act 30 with regard to the power of the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to amend, vary or revoke an Incorporation Order. Similar provision is also proposed in clause 18 respecting an order establishing a University College.

7. Clause 7 seeks to substitute section 12 of Act 30 to empower the Minister to establish a Campus of the University after consultation with the Board. The power to make a Campus Order currently exercised by the Yang di- Pertuan Agong is proposed to be given to the Minister in view of the various administrative arrangements the University is involved in due to the expansion of students population. In the spirit of internationalization, this amendment permits the establishment of campuses abroad. This clause also allows the Vice-Chancellor to approve the use of any area for purposes of the University on a temporary basis. The provisions of Act 30, the Constitution, Rules, etc., shall apply to that area as they apply to the Campus of the University in order to facilitate the application of the University’s disciplinary and other regulations, etc. therein.

<!-- page 32 -->

8. Clause 8 seeks to substitute section 15 of Act 30 to confer on students and student organizations freedom of association subject to restrictions in relation to political parties, unlawful organizations and organizations, body or group of persons identified by the Minister as unsuitable to the interest and well-being of the students or the University. In addition to that, the Vice- Chancellor may, on the application of a student, exempt the student from the restrictions mentioned in the proposed paragraph (1)(a). This will enable a serving politician from any political party to enroll for a course at a University without giving up his political career. The proposed amendment subsection (6) seeks to expand the scope of freedom of speech and expression on academic matters by a student. The proposed amendment to subsection (7) on the other hand, seeks to remove the criminal penalties provided under this section and to substitute them with disciplinary action. Decriminalization proposed in this section is in line with the spirit of Act 30 which governs higher education.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 15a of Act 30. The criminal penalty in subsection (2) is substituted with disciplinary action and in order to provide autonomy to the Universities, the power to grant exemption under subsection 15a(3) is now given to the Vice-Chancellor.

10. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 15b of Act 30 to provide that if a registrable offence has been committed in the name or on behalf of any organization, body or group of students of a University, any student convicted of such offence shall be liable to disciplinary action whilst the organization, body or group of students liable to suspension or dissolution.

11. The substitution of criminal offence with disciplinary offence in the proposed Act renders section 15c of Act 30 no longer necessary and hence is deleted in clause 11.

12. Clause 12 seeks to replace section 15d of Act 30. The new section 15d proposes reliefs to students facing criminal charges, detention, restriction order or imprisonment. Subsection (1) seeks to confer discretion on the Vice- Chancellor to decide whether or not to suspend a student who is charged with a registrable offence. Subsections (2) and (3) render a student who is convicted of registrable offence, or detained or subjected to an order of restriction under any written law relating to preventive detention or internal security, liable to disciplinary action instead of him immediately ceases to be a student under such circumstances by virtue of the existing provisions. Subsection

(4) seeks to allow a student who is placed under detention or subjected to an order imposing restriction on him or who is being imprisoned, to sit for his examination if consented to by the Senate and subject to an order being made or approval given by the Minister responsible for internal security or any competent authority, as the case may require. Subsections (5) and (6) seek to respectively allow a student who is suspended from the University or a student who ceases to be a student of the University under Act 30 to pursue his studies at any other University with the written approval of the Minister. These amendments are in line with the right to education for all. Subsections (7) and (8) seek to provide that the period of suspension shall not be considered in calculating the maximum duration permitted to complete the course of study and in the computation of his result when a student is discharged or acquitted of a registrable offence or released from detention or restrictions imposed on him are revoked.

<!-- page 33 -->

13. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 30 to give the organization, body or group of students of a University the right to make a written representation before any suspension or dissolution is made against it by the Vice-Chancellor. It also creates a right of appeal to the Minister.

14. Clause 14 seeks to delete section 16a of Act 30 on discipline of staff, officers and employees of University as the matter is now regulated by the Statutory Bodies (Discipline & Surcharge) Act 2000 [Act 605].

15. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 16b of Act 30 to improve the procedural safeguards for students in disciplinary proceedings.

16. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 16c of Act 30 by removing the power of the Board to make rules for employee discipline as the matter is now regulated by Act 605. With the amendment, section 16c is confined to rules for student discipline.

17. Clause 17 seeks to delete section 16d of Act 30 on surcharge as the matter is now regulated by Act 605.

18. Clause 22 seeks to amend section 24 of Act 30. The proposed amendment to subsection (1) seeks to ensure that no person is allowed to establish, manage or maintain a higher educational institution with the status of “University” or “University College” unless it is a higher educational institution established in accordance with Act 30 or Act 555. The amendment to subsection(2) seeks to ensure that no higher educational institution or person shall issue to or confer on any person any degree or diploma purporting to be degree or diploma issued or conferred by a University or University College unless the issue or conferment is in accordance with the provisions of Act 30 or Act 555.

19. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 26 of Act 30 to empower the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to exempt any University or University College from any provisions of Act 30 or the First and Second Schedules, or to vary the Schedules in their applications to any University or University College.
