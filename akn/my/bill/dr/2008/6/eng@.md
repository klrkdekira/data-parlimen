## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Education Act 1996. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Education (Amendment) Act 2008.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### General amendments

2. The Education Act 1996 [Act 550], which is referred to as the "principal Act" in this Act, is amended—

(a) by substituting for the words "Registrar General of Schools and Teachers" wherever appearing the words "Registrar General of Educational Institutions and Teachers";

(b) by substituting for the words "teacher education colleges" wherever appearing the words "institutes of teacher education";

(c) by substituting for the words "Registrar of Schools and Teachers" wherever appearing the words "Registrar of Educational Institutions and Teachers";

<!-- page 2 -->

(d) by substituting for the words "Assistant Registrar of Schools" wherever appearing the words "Assistant Registrar of Educational Institutions"; and

(e) by substituting for the words "Deputy Registrar General of Schools and Teachers" wherever appearing the words "Deputy Registrar General of Educational Institutions and Teachers".

### Amendment of section 2

3. Section 2 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) by inserting after the definition of "higher educational institution" the following definition:

' "institute of teacher education" means an educational institution providing teacher education leading to the award of a certificate, a diploma, a teaching degree or the equivalent thereof, or any other qualification as prescribed;';

(b) in the definition of "Registrar General", by substituting for the word "Schools" the words "Educational Institutions";

(c) in the definition of "special education", by substituting for the words "the special educational needs of pupils" the words "pupils with special needs"; and

(d) in the definition of "Assistant Registrar", by substituting for the word "Schools" the words "Educational Institutions".

### Amendment of section 5

4. Section 5 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (3), by inserting after the words "numbers of the words "Deputy Registrars of Educational Institutions and Teachers,";

(b) in subsection (4), by inserting after the words "The Deputy Registrar General, the Registrars," the words "the Deputy Registrars"; and
