## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Pensions Act 1980 ("Act 227") in order to provide for a new compulsory age of retirement of fifty-eight years for officers on full time employment in the public service. The new age of retirement is proposed by the amendment to subsection 10(1) of Act 227 as set out in clause 3.

Such officers however are given an option to retire at the present compulsory age of retirement of either fifty-five years or fifty-six years as the age that he had opted before 1 July 2008. Clauses 2 (except for subclause (d)), 4, 5 and 6 propose amendments to sections 6A, 12, 12A and 23 respectively of Act 227 consequential to the proposed change in the compulsory age of retirement. By subclause 2(d), an officer who has opted for the Employees Provident Fund Scheme may be required to retire on ground of national interest or in the interest of the public service.
