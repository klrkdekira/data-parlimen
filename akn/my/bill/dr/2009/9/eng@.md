## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Human Rights Commission of Malaysia Act

1999. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Human Rights Commission of Malaysia (Amendment) Act 2009.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Substitution of section 5

2. The Human Rights Commission of Malaysia Act 1999 [Act 597], which is referred to as “the principal Act” in this Act, is amended by substituting for section 5 the following section:

### “Members of the Commission and term of office

5. (1) The Commission shall consist of not more than twenty members.

### New section 11A

3. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 11 in Part II the following section:

### “Committee to be consulted with regard to appointment

11A. (1) For the purpose of subsection 5(2), there is established a committee consisting of the following persons:

(a) the Chief Secretary to the Government who shall be the Chairman;

(b) the Chairman of the Commission; and

(c) three other members, from amongst eminent persons, to be appointed by the Prime Minister.

(2) The members of the committee referred to in subsection (1) shall serve the committee for such period and on such terms and conditions as may be specified in the instrument of appointment.

(3) The members of the committee referred to in paragraph (1)(c) may include former judges and former members of the Commission.

(4) The following persons shall not be appointed as members of the committee under paragraph (1)(c):

### Savings and transitional

4. (1) Notwithstanding subsection 5(4) of the principal Act as introduced by this Act, a member of the Commission who was appointed or reappointed before the coming into operation of this Act and who has not completed his term of office immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, may continue to hold office for the remainder of his term of office.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection 5(4) of the principal Act as introduced by this Act –

(a) a member of the Commission who was appointed for the first time before the coming into operation of this Act and is holding office at the time of the coming into operation of this Act is eligible to be reappointed once for a period of three years under subsection 5(4) of the principal Act as introduced by this Act upon the expiry of his term of office;

(b) a member of the Commission who has been reappointed before the coming into operation of this Act is not eligible to be reappointed under

subsection 5(4) of the principal Act as introduced by this Act upon the expiry of his term of office under the reappointment.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Human Rights Commission of Malaysia Act 1999 (“Act 597”) to make the process of appointment of the members of the Human Rights Commission of Malaysia (“Commission”) more transparent.

2. To that end, section 5 of Act 597 is proposed to be amended in clause 2 of the Bill. With the proposed amendment, the Prime Minister is required to consult the committee referred to in section 11A as proposed by this Bill before tendering his advice to the Yang di-Pertuan Agong.

The amendment in clause 2 also seeks to provide that members of the Commission must be men and women of various religious, political and racial backgrounds and have knowledge of, or practical experience in, matters relating to human rights. In addition, members are to hold office for three years instead of two years presently.

The proposed amendment also seeks to allow the Prime Minister to determine suitable mechanisms

3. Clause 3 seeks to provide for the establishment of a committee that will be consulted by the Prime Minister with regard to the appointment of members of the Commission under subsection 5(2) as proposed by this Bill.

The committee shall consist of the Chief Secretary to the Government who shall be the Chairman, the Chairman of the Commission and three other persons of eminence appointed by the Prime Minister. The eminent persons may include former judges and former members of the Commission. However, no person who is actively involved in politics and registered with any political party, and is or was an enforcement officer shall be appointed to be members of the committee.

The members of the committee shall serve the committee according to such period and on such terms and conditions as specified in their instruments of appointment.

For the purpose of reappointment, a serving member of the Commission at the time of the coming into operation of the amendment proposed by this Bill is eligible to be reappointed if, before the coming into operation of the amendment proposed by this Bill, he has never been reappointed.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
