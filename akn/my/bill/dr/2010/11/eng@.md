ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This act may be cited as the Commercial Vehicles licensing board (amendment) act 2010.

(2) This act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Interpretation

2. in this act, “appointed date” means the date appointed by the Minister under subsection 1(2).

3. The Commercial Vehicles licensing board act 1987 [Act 334], which is referred to as the “principal act” in this act, is amended by substituting for section 1a the following section:

### “ Application

1a. (1) This act shall apply only to Sabah, Sarawak and the Federal Territory of labuan.

### Non-application, dissolution, transitional and saving

9. (1) On the appointed date—

(a) the Commercial Vehicles licensing board act 1987, hereinafter referred to as “the act”, shall cease to apply to Peninsular Malaysia; and

(b) the Commercial Vehicles licensing board Peninsular Malaysia, hereinafter referred to as the “dissolved board”, is dissolved.

(2) Notwithstanding the non-application of the Commercial Vehicles licensing board act 1987 to Peninsular Malaysia, all subsidiary legislation made or deemed to have been made or having effect under the act and in force or having effect on the appointed date shall, in so far as they are not inconsistent with the land Public Transport act 2010 [Act ], continue to be in force and have effect in Peninsular Malaysia as if they had been made under the land Public Transport act 2010 until they are amended, revoked or replaced by subsidiary legislation made under the land Public Transport act 2010.

(3) The powers, rights, privileges, liabilities, obligations and duties which immediately before the appointed date were those of the dissolved board shall devolve on the Suruhanjaya Pengangkutan awam Darat, hereinafter referred to as the “Commission”, as from that date.

(4) all decisions, directions and notifications made or given by the dissolved board in accordance with the act shall be deemed to have been made or given by the Commission under the land Public Transport act 2010 and shall continue in force until the decisions, directions and notifications are amended, revoked or replaced or until the date upon which they expire.

### Status of existing licences etc. under the Act

10. (1) any licence or exemption issued or granted under the act in respect of the licensing of commercial vehicles prior to the appointed date and expiring at any time after the appointed date shall, on the appointed date be deemed to be issued or granted under the land Public Transport act 2010 until the expiry of the licence or exemption if the licence or exemption is registered with the Commission within six months from the appointed date or within such other period as may be extended by the Commission.

EXPlaNaTORY STaTEMENT

This bill seeks to amend the Commercial Vehicles licensing board act 1987 (“act 334”) to provide for consequential changes to act 334 pursuant to the proposed Suruhanjaya Pengangkutan awam Darat act 2010 and the proposed land Public Transport act 2010. The proposed Suruhanjaya Pengangkutan awam Darat act 2010 seeks to establish the Suruhanjaya Pengangkutan awam Darat (“Commission”) as the principal regulator for land public transport, which includes public service vehicles and goods vehicles for Peninsular Malaysia, whilst the proposed land Public Transport act 2010 sets out the regulatory framework for land public transport for Peninsular Malaysia. Thus, after the commencement of the proposed Suruhanjaya Pengangkutan awam Darat act 2010 and the proposed land Public Transport act 2010, act 334 will only apply to Sabah, Sarawak and the Federal Territory of labuan.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and enables the Minister to appoint the commencement date of this bill.

3. Clause 2 seeks to introduce the definition of “appointed date” of this bill.

4. Clause 3 seeks to make amendments to section 1 of act 334 to clarify that act 334 will only apply to Sabah, Sarawak and the Federal Territory of labuan.

5. Clauses 4 and 5 seek to amend sections 2 and 3 respectively of act 334 to delete references made to the Commercial Vehicles licensing board Peninsular Malaysia which is dissolved by this proposed act.

6. Clauses 6, 7 and 8 seek to propose further consequential amendments to sections 4, 9 and 27 respectively of act 334.

7. Clause 9 seeks to provide for the non-application of act 334 to Peninsular Malaysia, the dissolution of the board and transitional matters and savings.

8. Clause 10 provides for the status of existing licences under act 334.

9. Clause 11 provides for the status of pending applications under act 334.

10. Clause 12 provides for the status of pending appeals under the act 334.
