### “Presumption as to the business of moneylending

### Evidence and records of previous conviction

10ob. (1) For the purposes of sentencing under this Act, when it is necessary to prove in any court that the offence for which a person is being convicted is a second or subsequent offence, a certificate purporting to be a record of his previous conviction, signed by—

(a) the Registrar; or

(b) any police officer authorized in writing in that behalf by the Chief Police Officer or Commissioner of Police,

having custody of the records in which such conviction is kept, shall be admissible as evidence and shall constitute prima facie proof of any fact stated therein.

### Diary of proceedings in investigation

10oc. (1) Every Inspector or police officer making an investigation under this Act shall day by day enter his proceedings in the investigation in a diary setting forth—

## HURAIAN

Rang Undang-Undang ini bertujuan untuk meminda Akta Pemberi Pinjam Wang 1951 (“Akta 400”)—

(a) untuk memperjelas ruang lingkup Akta 400;

(b) untuk memperketat pengawalseliaan perjalanan perniagaan pemberi pinjam wang oleh pemegang lesen dengan memasukkan peruntukan kesalahan dan obligasi yang baru;

(c) untuk menambah baik kuasa Pendaftar Pemberi Pinjam Wang dan Inspektor Pemberi Pinjam Wang serta anggota Polis Diraja Malaysia berhubung dengan penguatkuasaan Akta 400; dan

(d) untuk membanteras pemberi pinjam wang yang menyalahi undang- undang dengan memasukkan peruntukan kesalahan yang baru dan menaikkan penalti bagi kesalahan yang serius, iaitu menjalankan perniagaan pemberi pinjam wang tanpa lesen dan melakukan gangguan serta ugutan terhadap peminjam.
