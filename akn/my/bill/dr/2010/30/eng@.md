## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Valuers, Appraisers and Estate Agents Act 1981 (“Act 242”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 242 to substitute certain definitions and to introduce new definitions consequent upon the amendments proposed in this Act.

4. Clause 3 seeks to clarify further the scope of research services that may be provided by the Director General under the existing provisions.

5. Clause 4 seeks to provide for additional functions of the Board. These proposed functions are the power to prescribe rules on the taking out of professional indemnity insurance and to prescribe standards, directives, circulars and guidelines for the purposes of Act 242.

6. Clause 5 seeks to introduce a new section 10A into Act 242 to enable the Board to appoint committees comprising members of the Board or any other persons to assist it in performing its functions. The Board is also empowered to determine the functions and procedures of the committees under the proposed

7. Clause 6 is a consequence of the amendment made in clause 3. The proposed provision seeks to enable utilization of the Fund of the Board for the payment of the expenses for registration and other expenses for any research, investigation, study, conference or seminar that is not conducted by the Board.

8. Clause 7 seeks to empower the Board to prescribe the conditions to practise. This clause also seeks to provide that an individual registered valuer, appraiser or estate agent is only allowed to practise in accordance with section 23 of Act 242.

9. Clause 8 seeks to provide further conditions for the registration of valuers and appraisers.

10. Clause 9 seeks to provide for the registration of probationary valuers.

11. Clause 10 seeks to expand the scope of valuation practice under the existing section 19 of Act 242 by authorizing a registered valuer or appraiser to value all interest in lands or buildings including furniture, fixtures, trade stocks, plant, machinery and equipment and other effects.

12. Clause 11 seeks to provide further restrictions on valuation practice.

13. Clause 12 seeks to provide further conditions for the registration of estate agents.

14. Clause 13 seeks to provide further restrictions for estate agency practice.

<!-- page 19 -->

15. Clause 14 seeks to provide for the registration of probationary estate agents.

16. Clause 15 seeks to allow a sole proprietorship to operate as a valuer, appraiser or estate agency firm. Subclause 15(c) seeks to allow the opening up of the profession regulated under the Act to allied professionals and to allow natural persons as well as a combination of registered valuers, appraisers and estate agents, partnerships and bodies corporate to hold equity in firms practising valuation, appraisal or estate agency. Subclause 15(d) empowers the Board to cancel the registration of a firm if the Board finds that the firm or any of its partners or directors has breached or failed to carry out any of the terms and conditions imposed by the Board. Additionally, subclause 15(e) seeks to secure the interest of the public in the case where a registered person is removed or suspended from the Register or has passed away.

17. Clause 16 seeks to provide further reasons for the cancellation, suspension and admonition of valuers, appraisers and estate agents as well as probationary valuers and probationary estate agents. This clause also seeks to increase the fine that may be imposed by the Board against the valuer, appraiser or estate agent or probationary valuer or probationary estate agent under paragraph 24(1)(iv) and (v) from five thousand ringgit to ten thousand ringgit and from ten thousand ringgit to twenty-five thousand ringgit respectively.

18. Clause 17 seeks to provide further grounds for the removal of registered valuers, appraisers and estate agents and probationary valuers and probationary estate agents from the Register.

19. Clause 18 seeks to provide for a notice of appeal to be made in the prescribed form.

20. Clause 19 seeks to create an offence for aiding and abetting in the commission of an offence under Act 242. This clause also creates an offence for practising as a valuer, appraiser or estate agent without lawful authority. This clause also proposes that the maximum fines to be increased from twenty- five thousand ringgit to three hundred thousand ringgit and from ten thousand ringgit to twenty-five thousand ringgit.

21. Clause 20 seeks to increase the maximum fine from ten thousand ringgit to two hundred thousand ringgit.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
