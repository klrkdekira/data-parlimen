An Act to amend the Securities Commission Act 1993. [ ]

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Securities Commission (Amendment) Act 2011.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette, and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of—

### New section 31EA

9. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 31E the following section:

### “Audit Oversight Board may establish committees

31EA. (1) The Audit Oversight Board may establish such committees as it considers necessary or expedient to assist in the performance of its responsibilities as specified under subsection 31E(1).

(2) The Audit Oversight Board may appoint any person to be a member of a committee.

(3) A committee established under this section may elect any of its members to be chairman and may regulate its own procedures and, in the performance of its responsibilities, shall be subject to and act in accordance with any direction given to the committee by the Audit Oversight Board.

<!-- page 6 -->

(4) Meetings of a committee shall be held at such times and places as the chairman of the committee may determine.

(5) A committee may invite any person who is not a member to attend any meeting of the committee for the purpose of advising it on any matter under discussion but that person is not entitle to vote at the meeting.

(6) Members of a committee or any person invited under subsection (5) to attend any meeting of a committee may be paid such allowances and other expenses as the Audit Oversight Board may determine.

(7) A member of a committee shall hold office for such term as may be specified in his instrument of appointment and is eligible for reappointment.

(8) The Audit Oversight Board may at any time revoke the appointment of any member of a committee.

(9) A member of a committee may at any time resign his office by a written notice addressed to the executive chairman of the Audit Oversight Board.

(10) A committee shall cause—

(a) minutes of all its meetings to be maintained and kept in proper form; and

(b) copies of the minutes of all its meetings to be submitted to the Audit Oversight Board as soon as practicable.

(11) Any minutes made of a meeting of a committee shall, if duly signed, be admissible in evidence in all legal proceedings without further proof.

(12) Every meeting of a committee in respect of the proceedings of which minutes have been made in accordance with subsections (10) and (11) shall be deemed to have been duly convened and held and all members of the committee have been duly qualified to act.

<!-- page 7 -->

(13) A member of the committee who having directly or indirectly, by himself or a member of his family, any interest in any matter under discussion by the committee shall disclose to the committee the fact and nature of his interest.

(14) A disclosure under subsection (13) shall be recorded in the minutes of the committee, and after the disclosure, the member having an interest in the matter—

(a) shall not take part nor be present in any deliberation or decision of the committee; and

(b) shall be disregarded for the purpose of constituting a quorum of the committee relating to the matter.

(15) Notwithstanding subsections (13) and (14), no act or proceedings of the committee shall be invalidated on the ground that any member of the committee has contravened the provisions of those subsections.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Securities Commission Act 1993 (“Act 498”).

2. The proposed amendments seek to clarify and expand the Commission’s regulatory functions, to provide a framework for the recognition of foreign auditors and foreign audit firms by the Audit Oversight Board, to streamline the Audit Oversight Board’s operational processes, to enhance investigation and evidence gathering processes, miscellaneous and consequential amendments.

3. Clause 1 contains the short title. It also seeks to empower the Minister to appoint a date of coming into operation of the Act and to appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act, or all or different provisions of this Act in respect of different classes or categories of persons or securities.

4. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 to substitute for the words “futures contracts”, “futures exchange” and “futures market” the words “derivatives”, “derivatives exchange” and “derivatives market”. Further, subsection 2(2) is deleted as it is replaced with the new section 159A.

5. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 15 as follows:

(a) in paragraphs (a), (b), (g), (i), (j), (k), (l) and (n) to expand the Commission’s regulatory oversight to cover derivatives;

(b) in paragraph (e) to extend the Commission’s oversight over all fund management activities;

(c) in paragraph (m) to enable the Commission to supervise all persons engaging in regulated activities or providing capital market services;

(d) in paragraph (o) to enable the Audit Oversight Board to provide for recognition of foreign auditors and foreign audit firms;

(e) to insert a new paragraph (p) to expand the Commission’s regulatory function to include the monitoring, mitigating and managing of systemic risk in the capital market;

(f) to insert a new paragraph (q) to recognise the Commission’s existing role in promoting and regulating corporate governance and approved accounting standards in respect of listed corporations; and

(g) to insert a new paragraph (r) to enable the Commission to set and approve standards for professional qualification in the capital market.

6. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 22A in line with the expansion of the Commission’s regulatory oversight over derivatives.

7. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 24 by removing the reference to the words “long position” and “short position”.

8. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 31A to insert the definition of “relevant foreign authority” which is currently found in subsection 31ZD(2) and to insert the definitions of “recognised auditor” and “registered auditor”.

<!-- page 19 -->

9. Clause 7 seeks to insert a new subsection 31C(7) to clarify that the executive chairman will carry out the day-to-day administration of the Audit Oversight Board.

10. Clauses 8, 11, 12, 13 and 14 seek to amend sections 31E, 31N, 31O and 31P, and the shoulder note of Division 4 of Part IIIA to extend the remit of Audit Oversight Board to provide recognition to foreign auditors and foreign audit firms.

11. Clause 9 seeks to insert a new section 31EA which enables the Audit Oversight Board to establish committees to assist it in the performance of its responsibilities as specified in Act 498.

12. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 31L to provide clarity that it is the Commission who will keep proper accounts of the Audit Oversight Board fund.

13. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 31Q to establish a framework for the Audit Oversight Board to withdraw or suspend the recognition granted to a foreign auditor or a foreign audit firm.

14. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 31R to require auditors to notify the Audit Oversight Board of any event that may affect their fit and properness.

15. Clauses 17 and 18 are consequential to the amendments made in clauses 8, 11, 12, 13 and 14 relating to recognised auditors.

16. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 31W to enable the Audit Oversight Board to conduct inquiry on auditors without having to conduct prior inspections.

17. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 31x to enable substituted service of notice if the said notice cannot be served under situations set out in subsection 31X(8) or (9).

18. Clause 21 seeks to amend section 31Y to expand the Audit Oversight Board’s powers of inspection and inquiries over entities whose financial statements are consolidated into the financial statements of a public interest entity.

19. Clause 22 seeks to delete section 31ZA as the power of the Minister to amend schedules is now provided in the new section 159A.

20. Clause 23 seeks to amend section 31ZB to enable a recognised auditor to appeal against the decision of the Audit Oversight Board.

21. Clause 24 seeks to amend section 31ZD to delete the definition of “relevant foreign authorities” which has been moved to section 31A. Further, the new subsection (2) will enable the Audit Oversight Board to seek assistance from a relevant foreign authority to conduct any inspection or inquiry on a foreign auditor or foreign audit firm if necessary.

<!-- page 20 -->

22. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 126 to enable the Commission to conduct examination of registered persons or any other person engaging in regulated activities or providing capital market services in line with the amendments made to paragraph 15(m) in clause 3.

23. Clause 26 seeks to amend section 134 to enable the recording of evidence by audio or visual recording or a combination of both when the statement is recorded under subsection 134(1) and to enable substituted service of notice if the said notice cannot be served under situations set out in subsection 134(6) or (8).

24. Clause 27 seeks to amend section 146 to streamline all general appeal provisions under the securities laws into Act 498.

25. Clause 28 seeks to amend section 147 to streamline existing provisions on the finality of the Minister’s and the Commission’s decisions under the securities laws into Act 498.

26. Clause 29 seeks to amend subsection 150A(4) to enable the admission of oral evidence recorded by a foreign supervisory authority by means of audio or visual recording or combination of both and the admission of an affidavit affirmed by a person who is a witness but is residing outside Malaysia, in court. Further subsection 150A(5) is amended to enable an affidavit to be admitted in court when it is either sworn or affirmed by a witness.

27. Clause 30 seeks to insert section 150B to enable admissibility of statements taken and documents seized from a person who is dead or cannot be traced, etc., in court.

28. Clause 31 seeks to amend section 152A to enable the Commission to publish information on actions taken by the Commission under section 125 or 220 Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 (Act 671).

29. Clause 32 seeks to amend section 159 to consolidate existing powers to make regulations under securities laws into Act 498.

30. Clause 33 seeks to insert section 159A to consolidate the Minister’s powers to amend schedules.

31. Clause 34 seeks to substitute for the words “futures contracts” the word “derivatives” in Schedule 1.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
