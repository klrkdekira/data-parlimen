## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Pensions Act 1980. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Pensions (Amendment) Act 2011.

(2) This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2012.

### Amendment of section 6

2. The Pensions Act 1980 [Act 227], which is referred to as “the principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 6 by inserting after subsection (2) the following subsection:

“(3) Any pension, gratuity or other benefit granted to an officer who retires under section 11 shall be computed in accordance with the regulations where the total period of his reckonable service shall be counted as if he had served until his compulsory age of retirement, whichever is applicable to him.”.

### Savings and transitional

12. (1) The amendments in paragraphs 5(a) and (b) of this Act shall not affect the grant of a pension to an officer who before 1 January 2012—

(a) has optionally retired;

(b) has exceeded the age of fifty-five years; and

(c) has attained his compulsory age of retirement.

(2) The grant of a pension to an officer who before 1 January 2012—

(a) has optionally retired;

(b) has exceeded the age of fifty-five years; but

(c) has not attained his compulsory age of retirement,

shall only be made from 1 January 2012.

(3) The payment of derivative pension or derivative retiring allowance in consequence of the amendments made under sections 7 and 11 of this Act to any person prescribed in the regulations, if the period of twelve and a half years has expired before 1 January 2012, shall be dealt with as follows:

(a) the period of twenty years shall be calculated from the date of the retirement of the officer;

(b) the persons shall not be entitled to the payment of derivative pension or derivative retiring allowance for the period after the expiration of twelve and a half years until 31 December 2011; and

(c) the payment of derivative pension or derivative retiring allowance shall only be made from 1 January 2012.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Pensions Act 1980 (“Act 227”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the commencement date of the proposed Act.

<!-- page 6 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 227 to include a provision to allow the computation of retirement benefit of a retired officer on the ground of national interest or in the interest of the public service to be calculated according to the period of reckonable service as if the officer had served until the age of compulsory retirement which applies to him.

4. Clauses 3, 6 and 10 seek to amend sections 6a, 12a and 23 of Act 227 respectively which are consequential to the proposed extension of the compulsory age of retirement to sixty years as amended in clause 4. However, such officers are given an option to retire at the present compulsory age of retirement of either fifty-five, fifty-six or fifty-eight years before 1 January

2012.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend the age at which pension may begin to be paid to an officer appointed in service on or after 12 April 1991 who has optionally retired, namely from the compulsory age of retirement of fifty-five years, fifty-six years or fifty-eight years whichever is applicable to the officer, to fifty-five years of age.

6. Clauses 7 and 11 seek to amend sections 15 and 25 of Act 227 respectively regarding the extension of the grant of derivative pension and derivative retiring allowance from twelve and a half years to twenty years from the date of retirement of an officer to persons prescribed in the regulations, other than those specified in the proviso to sections 15 and 25 of Act 227.

7. Clauses 8 and 9 seek to amend sections 19 and 20 of Act 227 respectively to enable the grant of pension, derivative pension, gratuity or other benefit to be made to an officer, a pensioner or a recipient of derivative pension who is a bankrupt. For that purpose, the deletion of section 20 of Act 227 is proposed in clause 9. However, for the discharge of the court order issued in relation to bankruptcy matters under any written law relating to bankruptcy, amendment to section 19 of Act 227 is also made in clause 8 to enable the pension, derivative pension, gratuity or other benefit provided to be assignable, transferable, liable to be attached, sequestered or levied.

8. Clause 12 deals with savings and transitional provision.

9. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential amendments.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
