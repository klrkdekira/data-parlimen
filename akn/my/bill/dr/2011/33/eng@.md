Pensions (Amendment)

## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Statutory and Local Authorities Pensions Act 1980. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Statutory and Local Authorities Pensions (Amendment) Act 2011.

(2) This Act comes into operation on 1 January 2012.

### Amendment of section 5

2. The Statutory and Local Authorities Pensions Act 1980 [Act 239], which is referred to as “the principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 5 by inserting after subsection (2) the following subsection:

“(3) Any pension, gratuity or other benefit granted to an employee who retires under section 11 shall be computed in accordance with the regulations where the total period of his reckonable service shall be counted as if he had served until his compulsory age of retirement, whichever is applicable to him.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Statutory And Local Authorities Pensions Act 1980 (“Act 239”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the commencement date of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 239 to include a provision to allow the computation of retirement benefit of a retired employee on the ground of national interest or in the interest of the service of the statutory or local authority to be calculated according to the period of reckonable service as if the employee had served until the age of compulsory retirement which applies to him.

<!-- page 6 -->

4. Clauses 3, 6 and 10 seek to amend sections 6A, 13 and 26 of Act 239 respectively which are consequential to the proposed extension of the compulsory age of retirement to sixty years as amended in clause 4. However, employees are given an option to retire at the present compulsory age of retirement of either fifty-five, fifty-six or fifty-eight years applicable to them before 1 January 2012.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend the age at which pension may begin to be paid to an employee appointed in service on or after 12 April 1991 who has optionally retired, namely from the compulsory age of retirement of fifty- five years, fifty-six years or fifty-eight years whichever is applicable to the employee, to fifty-five years of age.

6. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 239 regarding the extension of the grant of derivative pension and derivative retiring allowance from twelve and a half years to twenty years from the date of retirement of an employee to persons prescribed in the regulations, other than those specified in the proviso to section 16 of Act 239.

7. Clauses 8 and 9 seek to amend sections 21 and 22 of Act 239 respectively to enable the grant of pension, derivative pension, gratuity or other benefit to be made to an employee, a pensioner or a recipient of derivative pension who is a bankrupt. For that purpose, the deletion of section 22 of Act 239 is proposed in clause 9. However, for the discharge of the court order issued in relation to bankruptcy matters under any written law relating to bankruptcy, amendment to section 21 of Act 239 is also made in clause 8 to enable the pension, derivative pension, gratuity or other benefit provided to be assignable, transferable, liable to be attached, sequestered or levied.

8. Clause 11 deals with savings and transitional provision.

9. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential amendments.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
