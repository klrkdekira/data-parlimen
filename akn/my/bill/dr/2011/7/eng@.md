## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority (Incorporation) (Amendment) Act 2011.

### Amendment of long title

2. The Malaysian Industrial Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965 [Act 397], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended by substituting for the long title the following long title:

“An Act to provide for the establishment and incorporation of the Malaysian Investment Development Authority and to provide for the administration, functions and powers of the Authority and for matters connected therewith.”.

### Amendment of section 1

3. Subsection 1(1) of the principal Act is amended by substituting for the word “Industrial” the word “Investment”.

### Amendment of section 2

4. Section 2 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in the definition of “Authority”, by substituting for the word “Industrial” the word “Investment”;

(b) by inserting after the definition of “Board” the following definition:

‘ “Chief Executive Officer” means the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority appointed under section 3D;’;

(c) by deleting the definition of “Director General”;

(d) in the definition of “Fund”, by substituting for the word “Industrial” the word “Investment”;

(e) by inserting after the definition of “Fund” the following definition:

‘ “Government Entity” means—

(a) the Federal Government, or any State Government, State Authority or local government; and

(b) any ministry, department, office, agency, authority, commission, committee, board, council or other body, corporate or unincorporate, of the Federal Government, or of any State Government, whether established under written law or otherwise;’; and

(f) in the definition of “Minister”, by substituting for the words “industrial development” the words “international trade and industry”.

### Savings and transitional provisions

19. (1) All references to the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965 in any written law or document shall, on the coming into operation of this Act, be construed as references to the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965.

(2) The change of name of “The Malaysian Industrial Development Authority” to “The Malaysian Investment Development Authority” upon the coming into operation of this Act shall not affect any rights or obligations of the Authority or render defective any legal proceedings by or against it, and any legal proceedings that could have been continued or commenced by or against it before the date of coming into operation of this Act may be continued or commenced under its new name.

(3) All actions, regulations, orders, directions, notifications, approvals, decisions, guidelines and other executive acts made, given or done under, or in accordance with, or by virtue of the principal Act before the coming into operation of this Act shall be deemed to have been made, given or done under, or in accordance with, or by virtue of, the corresponding provision introduced or amended by this Act, and shall continue to remain in full force and effect in relation to the persons to whom they apply until amended, repealed or rescinded under, in accordance with, or by virtue of, the corresponding provision introduced or amended by this Act.

(4) All references to the Director General of the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority in any written law or document shall, on the coming into operation of this Act, be construed as references to the Chief Executive Officer of the Malaysian Investment Development Authority.

(5) All moneys contained in or due to be paid into the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority Fund established under section 10 of the principal Act shall, on the coming into operation of this Act, be transferred to and be deemed to be part of the Malaysian Investment Development Authority Fund.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965 (“Act 397”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the long title of Act 397 to reflect the change of the name of the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority to the Malaysian Investment Development Authority.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 1 of Act 397 to amend the short title of Act 397. The Act is to be known after this as the “Malaysian Investment Development Authority (Incorporation) Act 1965”.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 397 to introduce new definitions and to amend existing definitions used in Act 397.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 3 of Act 397 to provide for the re- establishment of the Malaysian Industrial Development Authority as the Malaysian Investment Development Authority.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 3D in consequence of the change of title of the Director General to the Chief Executive Officer. This clause also seeks to empower the Chief Executive Officer to manage the annual budget of the Authority.

(Incorporation) (Amendment)

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 397 to list out the new extended functions of the Authority.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 6A of Act 397 to provide for the new powers of the Authority. The Authority now has the power to require Government Entities, companies and other bodies or persons responsible for or carrying out or intending to carry out development in Malaysia to submit reports to the Authority with regard to their activities.

10. Clause 12 seeks to introduce a new section 7C into Act 397 to empower the Authority to establish companies.

11. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 10A of Act 397 to allow the Fund to be expended for the giving of loans or any financial assistance to the private sectors and for the giving of financial assistance, credit facility or contribution to any person for any educational purposes, scholarships and any other purposes.

12. Clause 15 seeks to introduce a new section 10AA into Act 397 to allow the Authority to invest moneys of the Authority.

13. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 12 of Act 397 to allow the Authority to furnish its Annual Report to the Minister at a more practicable time.

14. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 13 of Act 397 to provide an increase in penalty for the offence of breaching the duty to maintain secrecy.

15. Clause 19 provides for savings and transitional provisions.

16. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
