## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Architects Act 1967. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Architects (Amendment) Act 2014.

(2) Except for paragraphs 3(d) and (p) and section 23, this Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

(3) Paragraphs 3(d) and (p) and section 23 come into operation on 1 June 2015.

<!-- page 2 -->

### General amendment

2. The Architects Act 1967 [Act 117], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended by substituting for the words “Professional Architects”, “Professional Architect” and “a Professional Architect” wherever appearing, except in the definition of “Professional Architect” in section 3, the words “Architects”, “Architect” and “an Architect” respectively.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Architects Act 1967 (“Act 117”). The proposed amendments seek to primarily liberalise the architectural profession in Malaysia and to provide for related matters.

2. Clause 1 provides for the short title and the power of the Minister to appoint different dates for the commencement of different provisions of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to provide for the general amendment to Act 117 by substituting for the words “Professional Architects”, “Professional Architect” and “a Professional Architect” wherever appearing, except in the definition of “Professional Architect” in section 3, the words “Architects”, “Architect” and “an Architect” respectively.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 117 to introduce new definitions and to amend existing definitions used in Act 117.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 3 of Act 117 to include an Inspector of Works and an Architectural Technologist as new architectural professions to be represented in the membership of the Board.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 117 to enhance the functions of the Board.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 117 to allow the registration of foreign architects.

<!-- page 44 -->

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 117 to allow the Registrar to publish in the Gazette or national newspaper the particulars of an Architect, a Graduate Architect, a foreign architect, a Building Draughtsman, an Inspector of Works, an Architectural Technologist, an Interior Designer, a Graduate Interior Designer, an architectural consultancy practice rendering architectural consultancy service, or an interior design consultancy practice rendering interior design consultancy service whose registration has been suspended, cancelled, removed and reinstated under Act 117.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 7 of Act 117 to provide restrictions on unregistered Architects.

10. Clauses 9 and 10 seek to amend sections 7a and 7b of Act 117 respectively to provide for matters relating to architectural consultancy practice and architectural consultancy services.

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 8 of Act 117 to provide that only an Architect or a foreign architect who is residing in Malaysia for not less than one hundred and eighty days in any one calendar year shall be entitled to submit plans or drawings to any person or authority in Malaysia.

12. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 10 of Act 117 to set out the qualifications for registration of Architects, etc.

13. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 10a of Act 117 to allow registration of foreign architects who are consultants to a project, wholly financed by a foreign government or implemented under any form of arrangement with the Government of Malaysia.

14. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 15a of Act 117 to enable the Board to appoint members of the Disciplinary Committee relating to the misconduct of or complaint against Architects, Graduate Architects, foreign architects and architectural consultancy practices.

15. Clause 19 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 117 to provide for the removal of Architects, Graduate Architects, foreign architects and architectural consultancy practices from the Register of Architects, etc.

16. Clause 20 seeks to amend section 17 of Act 117 to enable the Board to impose certain conditions for reinstatement of a foreign architect whose name has been removed from the Register of Architects, etc.

17. Clause 23 seeks to delete section 22 of Act 117 to discontinue new registration of Building Draughtsmen. This clause also provides for transitional provisions consequential to the deletion of section 22 of Act 117.

18. Clause 25 seeks to amend section 24 of Act 117 to reinforce the restrictions on Building Draughtsmen as provided under Act 117.

19. Clause 26 seeks to amend section 25 of Act 117 to increase the penalty that can be imposed by the Disciplinary Committee on Building Draughtsmen under this Act.

<!-- page 45 -->

20. Clause 33 seeks to amend section 27d of Act 117 to allow registration of Graduate Interior Designers and Interior Designers holding qualifications which are recognized by the Board.

21. Clause 34 seeks to amend section 27e of Act 117 to provide for registration of interior design consultancy practice.

22. Clause 36 seeks to amend section 27g of Act 117 to increase the penalty that can be imposed by the Disciplinary Committee on the Interior Designer under Act 117.

23. Clause 40 seeks to introduce Parts VB and VC into Act 117 on provisions relating to Inspectors of Works and Architectural Technologists respectively.

24. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
