An Act to amend the Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti- Smuggling of Migrants Act 2007. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti-Smuggling of Migrants (Amendment) Act 2015.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 2

2. ‘ “place of refuge” means a place of refuge declared by the Minister under subsection 42(1) of this Act.’.

11. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 51 the following section:

### “Permission to move freely and to work

51a. (1) Subject to any regulations made under section 66, any person to whom an interim protection order has been granted, or any trafficked person to whom a Protection Order has been granted, may be given permission by the Council—

(a) to move freely; or

(b) to be employed, engaged or contracted with to carry out work in any occupation during the period of the interim protection order or Protection Order, as the case may be.

(2) A foreign national who is granted permission to work under subsection (1) shall be subject to any restrictions and conditions as may be imposed by the relevant authorities relating to employment of foreign nationals in Malaysia.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti-Smuggling of Migrants Act 2007 (“Act 670”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 670 to introduce the definition of “place of refuge”.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 670 to add new members to the Council for Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti-Smuggling of Migrants.

5. Clause 4 seeks to introduce a new Part Ia relating to the High Level Committee. This new Part Ia contains 3 new sections, namely sections 5a, 5b and 5c.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 7 of Act 670 to enable the Council to pay allowance to the trafficked person subject to the availability of funds.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 11 of Act 670 to provide for the Minister to determine the functions of the Secretary of the Council.

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 43 of Act 670 to introduce subsection (1a) to enable the Minister to appoint any person as a Protection Officer.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 44(2) of Act 670 to extend the period of an interim protection order from fourteen days to twenty-one days.

10. Subclause 10(b) seeks to amend subparagraph 51(3)(a)(i) of Act 670 to shorten the period of a Protection Order from a maximum of two years to three months.

11. Clause 11 seeks to introduce a new section 51a into Act 670 to empower the Council to grant any person permission to move freely and to work during the period of the interim protection order or Protection Order.

12. Clause 13 seeks to amend paragraph 54(b) of Act 670 relating to the release and return of a trafficked person who is a foreign national to his country of origin.

13. Clause 16 seeks to introduce new sections 66a and 66b into Act 670 to enable the Court to issue an order for payment of compensation by the convicted person to the trafficked person, and an order for payment of wages in arrears in the case where there is no conviction to the alleged trafficked person.

14. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
