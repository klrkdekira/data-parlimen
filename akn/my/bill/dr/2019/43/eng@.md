### Transfer of employee’s contributions

53c. The contributions of a member of the Fund affected by the transfer into the account of another member of the Fund under this Part shall be the employee’s contributions of the first mentioned member of the Fund.

### Election by member of the Fund to transfer his contributions

53d. (1) Notwithstanding section 51 and subject to the conditions and criteria as specified in the Eighth Schedule, a member of the Fund may elect to transfer his contributions at the rate specified in the Eighth Schedule into the account of another member of the Fund who is or are his lawful wife or wives.

53e. (1) Upon the occurrence of any circumstances specified in the Eighth Schedule in relation to a transfer of contributions, a member of the Fund who has elected to transfer his contributions under section 53d may apply to the Board to cease the transfer.

(4) Any amount that has been transferred into the account of the member of the Fund who receives the contributions transferred before the date of cessation of transfer of the contributions shall be retained in the account of such member of the Fund.

### Re-transfer of contributions

53f. Where contributions under section 53d have been erroneously transferred, the Board may re-transfer the contributions, including any dividend, into the account of the member of the Fund who has elected to transfer the contributions, subject to the amount standing to the credit of the member of the Fund who has erroneously received the contributions transferred.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employees Provident Fund Act 1991 (“Act 452”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title of the proposed Act and the power of the Minister to appoint the commencement date of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 452 regarding the membership of the Board.

Subclause 2(a) seeks to amend paragraph 4(1)(d) of Act 452 to increase the total number of members of the Board to be appointed under that paragraph from not more than eighteen to not more than twenty.

Subclause 2(e) seeks to amend subparagraph 4(1)(d)(iv) of Act 452 to increase the number of members of the Board to be appointed under that subparagraph from not more than three to not more than five. The amendment

<!-- page 9 -->

further provides that the members under that subparagraph shall be appointed from amongst persons who have knowledge or experience in finance, business, economy, social security or other relevant knowledge or experience.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 452 to introduce a new subsection 6(2) into Act 452. The new subsection 6(2) provides that a member of the Board, other than the chief executive officer, shall be eligible for reappointment but no member shall hold office for a total term of more than twelve years.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 9(1) of Act 452 to increase the number of members of the Board from six to eight, excluding the Chairman or Deputy Chairman, to form a quorum at all meetings of the Board.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 18 of Act 452.

Subclause 5(a) seeks to delete paragraph 18(1)(c) of Act 452 to remove the membership of the Governor of Bank Negara Malaysia or his representative from the Investment Panel for the purpose of avoiding any conflict of interest in discharging his duties.

Subclause 5(b) seeks to amend paragraph 18(1)(e) of Act 452 to increase the number of members of the Investment Panel to be appointed under that paragraph from three to four. The amendment further provides that the members under that paragraph shall be appointed from amongst persons who have knowledge or experience in finance, business, economy or other relevant knowledge or experience.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 39 of Act 452.

Subclause 6(a) seeks to amend paragraph 39(1)(a) of Act 452 to provide for measures to prevent a person from leaving Malaysia if the chief executive officer has reason to believe that such person is about or is likely to leave Malaysia without paying any outstanding and due contributions under Act 452.

Subclause 6(b) seeks to introduce new subsections 39(3) and (4) into Act 452. The proposed subsection (3) seeks to make it an offence for any person who leaves Malaysia without paying the outstanding and due contributions or furnishing security for its payment. The proposed subsection (4) provides that for the purposes of section 39 of Act 452, “contributions” includes any dividend or late payment charges due on any contributions.

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend paragraphs 43(8)(c) and (d) of Act 452 to allow a person, whether or not he is an employer or employee within the meaning of Act 452, to elect to pay monthly contributions. An example of this category would be a housewife.

9. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new Part Vb into Act 452. The new Part contains four new provisions namely sections 53c, 53d, 53E and 53f.

The proposed section 53c provides that the contributions of a member of the Fund affected by the transfer into the account of another member of the Fund under Part Vb shall be the employee’s contributions of the first mentioned member of the Fund.

<!-- page 10 -->

The proposed section 53d provides that a member of the Fund who fulfills the conditions and criteria as specified in the Eighth Schedule may elect to transfer his contributions at the rate specified in the Eighth Schedule into the account of another member of the Fund who is or are his lawful wife or wives.

The proposed section 53E further provides that upon the occurrence of any circumstances specified in the Eighth Schedule, a member of the Fund who has elected to transfer his contributions under section 53d may apply to the Board to cease the transfer.

Currently, under the Eighth Schedule, transfer of contributions is allowed for the purpose of transferring contributions of a member of the Fund into the account of his lawful wife or wives, who is or are also a member or members of the Fund. If the marriage between him and his wife has been dissolved or his wife has died, the member of the Fund may apply to cease the transfer.

The proposed section 53f seeks to empower the Board to re-transfer any contributions transferred, including any dividend, into the account of the member of the Fund who has elected to transfer the contributions if the contributions have been erroneously transferred.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 55a(2) of Act 452 to enable a member of the Fund who has attained the age of fifty-five years to withdraw all sums of money standing to his credit under section 54 of Act 452 if he is physically or mentally incapacitated from engaging in an employment or he is not a Malaysian citizen and is about to leave Malaysia.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 55b of Act 452 to introduce a new subsection 55b(3) into Act 452. The proposed subsection (3) provides that section 55b of Act 452 shall not apply to a member of the Fund who has attained the age of fifty-five years who falls within the category of the member of the Fund under paragraph 54(1)(c) or (e) or paragraph 70c(1)(b) or (c).

12. Clause 11 seeks to introduce a new Eighth Schedule to deal with matters relating to transfer of contributions under Part Vb.

13. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Explanatory Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
