## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 2022 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply (2022) Act 2023.

### Additional expenditure for the year 2022

2. The issue is hereby authorized of a sum not exceeding sixty billion one hundred and seventy-four million nine hundred and twenty-eight thousand ringgit (RM60,174,928,000) from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure on the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2022 not provided for or not fully provided for by the Supply Act 2022 [Act A1638].

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of sixty billion one hundred and seventy-four million nine hundred and twenty-eight thousand ringgit (RM60,174,928,000) for the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2022.
