## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) (No. 2) Bill 2023 (“the proposed Act”) seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (“Act 593”) consequential to the amendments made to the Penal Code [Act 574], among others, to include an abetment of the commission of an attempted suicide by a child or a person who lacks mental capacity, or by any person, as a criminal offence.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the First Schedule to Act 593 consequential to the amendments made to sections 305 and 306 of the Penal Code and the deletion of section 309 of the Penal Code.
