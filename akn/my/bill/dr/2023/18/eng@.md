## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Renewable Energy Act 2011. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Renewable Energy (Amendment) Act 2023.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 1

2. The Renewable Energy Act 2011 [Act 725] is amended in section 1 by inserting after subsection (2) the following subsection:

“(3) The Minister may, by order published in the Gazette, suspend the operation of the whole or any of the provisions of this Act in different parts of Malaysia.”.

<!-- page 2 -->

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Renewable Energy Act 2011 (“Act 725”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 1 of Act 725 by introducing a new subsection (3) to empower the Minister to suspend the operation of the whole or any of the provisions of Act 725 in different parts of Malaysia.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
