## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Suruhanjaya Perkhidmatan Air Negara Act 2006. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Suruhanjaya Perkhidmatan Air Negara (Amendment) Act 2023.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 9

2. The Suruhanjaya Perkhidmatan Air Negara Act 2006 [Act 654], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in subsection 9(2) by substituting for the word “five” the word “three”.

<!-- page 2 -->

### Amendment of section 14

3. Subsection 14(3) of the principal Act is amended in the English language text, by substituting for the words “shall be guilty of” the word “commits”.

### Amendment of section 16

4. Subsection 16(2) of the principal Act is amended by deleting paragraph (d).

### Amendment of section 17

5. Subsection 17(1) of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (b), by deleting the word “or” at the end of the paragraph;

(b) in paragraph (c), by substituting for the full stop at the end of the paragraph the words “; or”; and

(c) by inserting after paragraph (c) the following paragraph:

“(d) any employee of the Commission.”.

### New section 19a

6. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 19 the following section:

### “Restriction on the use of the symbol or representation

### of the Commission

19a. (1) The Commission shall have the exclusive right to the use of any symbol or representation as the Commission may select or create and thereafter display or exhibit such symbol or representation in connection with its activities or affairs.

(2) The symbol or representation selected or created by the Commission shall be published in the Gazette.

<!-- page 3 -->

(3) Any person who uses a symbol or representation of the Commission without obtaining the written approval of the Commission, or uses any symbol or representation resembling the symbol or representation of the Commission, or creates any symbol or representation which refers to the Commission so as to deceive or cause confusion, or to be likely to deceive or to cause confusion, commits an offence and shall, on conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding one hundred thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or to both.”.

### Amendment of section 23

7. Section 23 of the principal Act is amended by deleting the words “, with the approval of the Minister,”.

### New section 23a

8. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 23 the following section:

### “Discipline

23a. (1) The Commission shall have disciplinary authority over all its employees and shall exercise disciplinary control in respect of all such persons.

(2) The Commission may make regulations with respect to the discipline of its employees.”.

### Amendment of section 31

9. Section 31 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) by deleting the words “after consulting the Minister;”; and

(b) by substituting for the words “upon as far as practicable by cheques signed by any person as may be authorized” the words “in such manner as may be authorized”.

<!-- page 4 -->

### Amendment of section 34

10. The principal Act is amended by substituting for section 34 the following section:

### “Limitations on contracts

34. The Commission may enter into any contract under which the Commission is to pay or receive an amount—

(a) not exceeding ten million ringgit;

(b) exceeding ten million ringgit but not exceeding fifty million ringgit, with the approval of the Minister; or

(c) exceeding fifty million ringgit, with the approval of the Minister and the concurrence of the Minister of Finance.”.

### Amendment of section 35

11. Section 35 of the principal Act is amended by deleting the words “and the approval of the Minister”.

### Amendment of section 41

12. Subsection 41(2) of the principal Act is amended in the English language text, by substituting for the words “shall be guilty of” the words “commits”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Suruhanjaya Perkhidmatan Air Negara Act 2006 (“Act 654”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and seeks to empower the Minister to appoint the date of commencement of the proposed Act including the power to appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of the proposed Act.

<!-- page 5 -->

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 9(2) of Act 654 to reduce the tenure of office of a member of the Commission from five terms to three terms.

4. Clause 4 seeks to delete paragraph 16(2)(d) of Act 654. With the proposed amendment, the Commission may grant loans and scholarships to employees of the Commission under section 24 of Act 654 without the approval of the Minister.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend subsection 17(1) of Act 654 to enable the Commission to delegate any of its functions and powers under the water supply and sewerage services law to an employee of the Commission, except the power to make subsidiary legislation.

6. Clause 6 seeks to introduce a new section 19a into Act 654 to give the Commission the exclusive right to the use of a symbol or representation of the Commission and to prohibit the misuse of such symbol or representation.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 23 of Act 654 to be consistent with section 22 of Act 654. With the proposed amendment, the Commission may determine the conditions of service of its employees without the approval of the Minister.

8. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new section 23a into Act 654 to empower the Commission to make regulations with respect to the discipline of its employees.

9. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 31 of Act 654 to enable the Commission to determine all matters pertaining to its bank account without consultation with the Minister.

10. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 34 of Act 654 to stipulate the limits based on the value of the contracts where approval is required for the Commission to enter into a contract.

11. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 35 of Act 654 to enable the Commission to determine its own financial procedure without the approval of the Minister.

12. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

The Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
