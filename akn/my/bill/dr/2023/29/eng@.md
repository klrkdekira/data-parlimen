## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Electricity Supply Act 1990. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Electricity Supply (Amendment) Act 2023.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of long title

2. The Electricity Supply Act 1990 [Act 447], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in the long title by deleting the words “and the efficient use of electricity”.

### Amendment of section 4

3. Section 4 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (k), by deleting the words “efficient use of electricity,”;

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) in the English language text, in paragraph (ga), by substituting for the word “efficient” the word “effective”; and

(c) by deleting paragraph (la).

### Amendment of section 11

4. Subsection 11(1) of the principal Act is amended by substituting for the word “efficient” the word “effective”.

### Deletion of Part Va

5. The principal Act is amended by deleting Part Va.

### Amendment of section 53

6. Subsection 53(1) of the principal Act is amended by deleting paragraphs (xa), (xj) and (xk).

### Saving and transitional

7. (1) Any application for and issuance, of a Certificate of Approval in respect of the efficient use of electricity under the principal Act which is pending on the date of the coming into operation of this Act shall be deemed to have been made and shall be dealt with by the Commission under the relevant provisions of the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023 [Act...].

(2) Any Certificate of Approval in respect of the efficient use of electricity which has been issued under the principal Act and is in force immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, on the date of the coming into operation of this Act, be deemed to have been issued under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023 and shall remain valid until the Certificate of Approval is expired or revoked by the Commission.

<!-- page 3 -->

(3) Any energy performance testing report, made in respect of the efficient use of electricity under the principal Act immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall be deemed to have been validly made under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023 and remain valid for the period of five years from the date of the report or until the report is revoked by the Commission.

(4) Any notice, order or prohibition issued by the Commission in respect of the efficient use of electricity immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, on the date of coming into operation of this Act, be discontinued.

(5) Any action, seizure, prosecution or proceedings taken, made, commenced or pending in respect of the efficient use of electricity immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, on the date of coming into operation of this Act, be continued as if the principal Act had not been amended by this Act.

(6) Any provision in the subsidiary legislation made under the deleted paragraphs 53(xa), (xj) and (xk) of the principal Act shall remain in operation until revoked or replaced by the subsidiary legislation made under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Electricity Supply Act 1990 (“Act 447”) following the enactment of the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023. All matters relating to the efficient use of electricity will be provided in the said Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the long title of Act 447 to delete the words “and the efficient use of electricity” because all matters relating to the efficient use of electricity will no longer be under the scope of Act 447.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 447.

Subclauses 3(a) and (c) respectively seek to amend paragraph 4(k) by deleting the words “efficient use of electricity” and deleting paragraph (la). The proposed amendments seek to remove all matters relating to the efficient

<!-- page 4 -->

use of electricity from the functions and duties of the Electricity Commission and such matters will be provided under the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act 2023.

5. Clause 5 seeks to delete Part Va of Act 447 to remove all the provisions in respect of the efficient use of electricity under Act 447.

6. Clause 6 seeks to delete paragraphs 53(xa), (xj) and (xk) of Act 447 to remove the power of the Minister to make regulations in respect of the efficient use of electricity under Act 447.

7. Clause 7 seeks to provide for the saving and transitional provisions.

8. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Explanatory Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
