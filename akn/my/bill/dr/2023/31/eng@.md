## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 2024 and to appropriate that sum for the service of that year. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supply Act 2024.

### Issue and appropriation for the service of the year 2024

2. The issue of a sum not exceeding two hundred and fourteen billion five hundred and fifty-four million two hundred and sixty-four thousand three hundred ringgit (RM214,554,264,300) from the Consolidated Fund for the service of the year 2024 is hereby authorized and that sum is appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund a sum of two hundred and fourteen billion five hundred and fifty-four million two hundred and sixty-four thousand three hundred ringgit (RM214,554,264,300) for the service of the year 2024 and for that sum to be appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.
