## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Employment Insurance System Act 2017. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Employment Insurance System (Amendment) Act 2024.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 16

2. The Employment Insurance System Act 2017 [Act 800], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in subsection 16(2) by substituting for the words “five thousand ringgit” wherever appearing the words “six thousand ringgit”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Employment Insurance System Act 2017 (“Act 800”). The proposed amendments seek to improve social security coverage under the Employment Insurance System that will give benefit to the employees.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 800 to increase the maximum limit of the insurability amount of wages of an employee under Act 800 from five thousand ringgit a month to six thousand ringgit a month.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend the Second Schedule to Act 800 to introduce new contribution rates payable by the employer and employee in the Second Schedule when the wages of an employee exceed five thousand ringgit and six thousand ringgit.

<!-- page 5 -->

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend the Third Schedule to Act 800 to introduce new total contribution rates and corresponding assumed monthly wages for the month when the wages of an employee exceed five thousand ringgit and six thousand ringgit.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any financial expenditure.
