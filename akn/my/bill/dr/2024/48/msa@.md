### “Deduction for individual on interest expended

46b. (1) Subject to this section, in the case of an individual who is a citizen and resident for the basis year for the relevant year, there shall be allowed for that relevant year personal deduction in respect of interest expended in that basis year by the individual to finance the purchase of a residential property:

Provided that—

(a) the residential property is the first residential property purchased by the individual to be occupied as his place of residence and limited to only one unit;

(b) the Sale and Purchase Agreement for the purchase is executed on or after 1 January 2025 but not later than 31 December 2027; and

(c) the individual shall not derive any income in respect of that residential property.

(2) Subject to subsection (3)—

(a) there shall be allowed to that individual a deduction of a maximum amount of—

(i) seven thousand ringgit, in relation to a residential property where the purchase price is not more than five hundred thousand ringgit; or

<!-- page 14 -->

(ii) five thousand ringgit, in relation to a residential property where the purchase price is more than five hundred thousand ringgit but not more than seven hundred and fifty thousand ringgit; and

(b) the deduction under paragraph (2)(a) shall be for each basis year for a year of assessment for a period of three consecutive basis years beginning from the basis year in which the interest referred to in subsection (1) is first expended by that individual.

(3) Where—

(a) two or more individuals are each entitled to claim deduction for the relevant year under this section for interest expended in respect of the same residential property; and

(b) the total amount of interest expended by those individuals in the basis year for that relevant year exceed the amount of deduction allowable for that relevant year under subsection (2),

there shall be allowed to each of those individuals for that relevant year an amount to be determined in accordance with the following formula:

B A X C

where A is the total amount of deduction allowed under subsection (2) for that relevant year;

B is the total interest expended in the basis year for that relevant year by that individual; and

C is the total interest expended in the basis year for that relevant year by all such individuals.

(4) For the purposes of subsection (1), any amount expended by the wife or the husband in the relevant year—

(a) where subsection 45(2) applies, shall be deemed to have been expended by the husband of the wife who elects or by the wife of the husband who elects, as the case may be; or

(b) where the wife or the husband has no total income, shall be deemed to have been expended by the husband of that wife or the wife of that husband, as the case may be:

Perenggan 33(f) bertujuan untuk meminda subseksyen 173(6) Akta 53 untuk menjelaskan bahawa pelarasan yang disebut dalam subseksyen itu ialah pelarasan yang dibuat di bawah subseksyen 173(1) hingga (5) Akta 53.

Perenggan 33(g) bertujuan untuk memotong subseksyen 176(2) Akta 53 berbangkit daripada pindaan yang dibuat kepada seksyen 164 Akta 53.

Subperenggan 33(h)(i) bertujuan untuk meminda formula berhubung dengan butiran C bagi subseksyen 177(1) Akta 53 untuk menjelaskan bahawa subseksyen 169(3) dan (4) Akta 53 hendaklah dibaca bersama bagi menentukan jumlah yang dianggap sebagai Cukai Tokokan Multinasional Semasa Tambahan.

Subperenggan 33(h)(ii) bertujuan untuk meminda formula berhubung dengan butiran D bagi subseksyen 177(1) Akta 53 untuk menjelaskan bahawa butiran D ialah amaun yang boleh dibayar di bawah suatu Cukai Tokokan Domestik Bersyarat yang dikenakan terhadap Entiti Konstituen yang terletak di negara lain.

Perenggan 33(i) bertujuan untuk meminda subseksyen 180(9) Akta 53 untuk menjelaskan bahawa kerugian kemerosotan nilai hendaklah termasuk dalam pengiraan nilai buku semasa Aset Ketara Yang Layak.

Subperenggan 33(n)(i) dan (iii) bertujuan untuk meminda subseksyen 220(1) dan (5) Akta 53 untuk menjelaskan rujukan dalam subseksyen itu.

Subperenggan 33(n)(ii) bertujuan untuk meminda subseksyen 220(4) Akta 53 untuk memperuntukkan bahawa pemakaian subseksyen itu tertakluk kepada subseksyen 220(6) Akta 53.
