## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Labuan Limited Partnerships and Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2010. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Labuan Limited Partnerships and Limited Liability Partnerships (Amendment) Act 2024.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Labuan Limited Partnerships and Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2010 [Act 707], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 2, in the definition of “resident”, by substituting for the words “pursuant to section 43 of the Exchange Control Act 1953 [Act 17]” the words “pursuant to paragraph 214(6)(a) of the Financial Services Act 2013 [Act 758] and paragraph 225(6)(a) of the Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 [Act 759]”.

### Amendment of section 9

3. Subsection 9(4) of the principal Act is amended by inserting after the words “at its previous registered office” the words “or is sent by electronic means to the address or numbers provided by the Labuan limited partnership.”.

### Amendment of section 22

4. Section 22 of the principal Act is amended by substituting for subsection (4) the following subsection:

“(4) For the purposes of this Act, any process, document or notice served under this Act shall be deemed to have been served on a Labuan limited partnership if the process, document or notice—

(a) is left at, or sent by ordinary or registered post to the last known address of the registered office of the Labuan limited partnership; or

(b) is sent by electronic means to the address or numbers provided by the Labuan limited partnership.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Labuan Limited Partnerships and Limited Liability Partnerships Act 2010 (“Act 707”). The main purpose of the amendment is to introduce new provisions into Act 707 relating to the reporting framework and disclosure of beneficial ownership information.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the definition of resident.

4. Clauses 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 seek to provide for the service of process, notice or documents either by post or electronic means.

5. Clause 8 seeks to introduce a new Part IVa into Act 707.

The proposed part IVa contains the provisions relating to beneficial ownership of a Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership.

The proposed section 69a seeks to lay down the basic criteria for identifying a beneficial owner of Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership.

The proposed section 69b seeks to provide for the obligation to keep a register of beneficial owners of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership and file the relevant beneficial ownership information with the Authority. The Authority may direct the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership to provide information in relation to the beneficial owner kept by the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership. Only persons as specified by the Authority may access the register of beneficial owners of Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership and the relevant beneficial ownership information.

The proposed section 69c seeks to empower Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership to require the disclosure of beneficial ownership information of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership. The proposed amendment enables the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership to obtain information of its beneficial owner or confirm the accuracy of the information from any person whom the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership knows or has reasonable grounds to believe to be a beneficial owner of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership.

<!-- page 14 -->

The proposed section 69d seeks to provide for the duty of a beneficial owner of a Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership. With this amendment, a beneficial owner of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership has the duty to notify the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership when he becomes a beneficial owner of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership and shall notify the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership if there are any changes in the beneficial ownership information of the Labuan limited partnership or Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership’s beneficial ownership.

The proposed section 69e seeks to provide that a Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership shall maintain and keep the register of beneficial owners updated from time to time. The proposed section 69e also seeks to provide that the Labuan trust company which has been appointed by the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership shall ensure that the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership comply with the proposed section.

The proposed section 69f seeks to empower the Authority to conduct examination as provided under section 28c of the Labuan Financial Services Authority Act 1996 [Act 545] on the register of beneficial owners of the Labuan limited partnership, Labuan limited liability partnership or recognized limited liability partnership.

6. Clauses 10 and 11 seek to amend sections 77 and 78 of Act 707 to increase the maximum amount of the general penalty and administrative penalty from five and ten thousand ringgit respectively to fifty thousand ringgit.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
