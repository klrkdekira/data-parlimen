intituled

An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Code. [ ]

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Code

(Amendment) (No. 2) Act 2024.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed

by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of First Schedule

the First Schedule by inserting after the item relating to section 507a

of the Penal Code [Act 574] and the particulars relating to it

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

The Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) (No. 2) Bill 2024 seeks to amend the Criminal Procedure Code (“Act 593”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend the First Schedule to Act 593 consequential to the introduction of the new sections 507b, 507c, 507d, 507e and 507f into the Penal Code [Act 574].

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
