## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Countervailing and Anti-Dumping Duties Act 1993. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Countervailing and Anti-Dumping Duties (Amendment) Act 2025.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Countervailing and Anti-Dumping Duties Act 1993 [Act 504], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in section 2—

(a) in the definition of “subject merchandise”, by substituting for the words “imported or sold for importation into” the words “exported or sold for exportation from the exporting country to”;

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) in the definition of “dumping”, by substituting for the words “the importation of merchandise into Malaysia at” the words “the exportation of merchandise from the exporting country to Malaysia at a price”;

(c) in the definition of “Minister”, by substituting for the words “international trade” the words “investment, trade”;

(d) by inserting before the definition of “country” the following definition:

‘ “circumvention” means an action of an exporter or a producer of the subject merchandise to avoid or undermine the countervailing or anti-dumping duties imposed by the Government;’; and

(e) by inserting after the definition of “interested party” the following definition:

‘ “investigating authority” means any public officer or person, who is appointed and authorized in writing by the Minister under subsection 30(2a);’.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Countervailing and Anti-Dumping Duties Act 1993 (“Act 504”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and commencement of the proposed Act and empowers the Minister to appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 504.

<!-- page 17 -->

Countervailing and Anti-Dumping Duties (Amendment) 17

Subclauses 2(a), (b) and (c) seek to amend the definitions of “subject merchandise”, “dumping” and “Minister”.

Subclauses 2(d) and (e) seek to provide for new definitions of “circumvention” and “investigating authority”.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 9(1) of Act 504 in relation to the period of investigation to be consistent with Article 17.1 of the Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures as Malaysia is one of the Member States under the World Trade Organization.

5. Clause 4 seeks to substitute subsection 12(1) of Act 504 to provide that the Government may suspend any investigation if the Government accepts the undertakings, from the exporting Member who agrees to eliminate or limit the subsidy, or from any exporter who agrees to revise its prices.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 12a of Act 504 to provide that a countervailing duty imposed under Act 504 shall be imposed for not more than five years either from the date of its imposition or from the date of the conclusion of the most recent expiry review.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 13 of Act 504.

Subclause 6(a)(i) seeks to amend subsection 13(1) of Act 504 to provide that the submission of any information to the Government in relation to the administrative review shall be made by way of a petition.

Subclause 6(a)(ii) and subclause 6(b) seek to delete paragraph 13(1)(d) and subsections 13(2), (3), (4), (5), (6), (7) and (8) of Act 504, because the matters relating to expiry review and determination of review of countervailing duty will be provided separately under the proposed new sections 13c and 13d.

Subclause 6(c) seeks to introduce new subsections (9), (10), (11), (12) and (13) into section 13 of Act 504 to provide specifically for matters relating to administrative review of countervailing duty initiated under Act 504 and its procedures.

8. Clause 7 seeks to introduce new sections 13c and 13d into Act 504.

The proposed new section 13c seeks to provide for the circumstances when the Government is required to conduct an expiry review and requirement to submit sufficient evidence substantiating the need for an expiry review in relation to countervailing duty.

The proposed new section 13d seeks to provide for the determination relating to all reviews of countervailing duty initiated under Act 504 and its procedures.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection 17(1) of Act 504 to provide that the export price for the subject merchandise shall be the price actually paid or payable for such subject merchandise from the exporting country. This clause also seeks to provide that in cases where there is no export price or where it appears that the export price is unreliable, the export price may be constructed on any reasonable basis the Government may determine and not solely by the exporters or producers.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend subsection 24(1) of Act 504 in relation to the period of investigation to be consistent with Article 7.1 of the Agreement on Implementation of Article VI of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade 1994 as Malaysia is one of the Member States under the World Trade Organization.

11. Clause 10 seeks to substitute subsection 27(1) of Act 504 to provide that the Government may suspend any investigation if the Government accepts the undertakings from any exporter to revise its prices or to cease exports into Malaysia at dumped prices and the Government is satisfied that the injurious effect of dumping is eliminated.

12. Clause 11 seeks to amend section 27a of Act 504 to provide that an anti-dumping duty shall be imposed for not more than five years either from the date of its imposition or from the date of the conclusion of the most recent expiry review.

13. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 28 of Act 504.

Subclause 12(a)(i) seeks to amend subsection 28(1) of Act 504 to provide that the submission of any information to the Government in relation to the administrative review shall be made by way of a petition.

Subclause 12(a)(ii) and subclause 12(b) seek to delete paragraph 28(1)(e) and subsections 28(2), (3), (4), (5), (6), (7) and (8) of Act 504 because the matters relating to expiry review and determination of review of anti-dumping duty will be provided separately under the proposed new sections 28c and 28d.

Subclause 12(c) seeks to introduce new subsections (9), (10), (11), (12) and (13) into section 28 of Act 504 to provide specifically for matters relating to administrative review of anti-dumping duty initiated under Act 504 and its procedures.

14. Clause 13 seeks to introduce new sections 28c and 28d into Act 504.

The proposed new section 28c seeks to provide for the circumstances when the Government is required to conduct an expiry review and requirement to submit sufficient evidence substantiating the need for an expiry review in relation to anti-dumping duty.

The proposed new section 28d seeks to provide for the determination relating to all reviews of anti-dumping duty initiated under Act 504 and its procedures.

15. Clause 14 seeks to introduce a new subsection (2a) into section 30 of Act 504 to empower the Minister to appoint and authorize any public officer or person to be the investigating authority to conduct any investigation or take any action under Act 504. This clause also seeks to substitute subsection 30(4) of Act 504 to provide that the Minister shall then make recommendation to the Minister of Finance who shall decide on the imposition of countervailing or anti-dumping duties and the collection of duties under Act 504.

16. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 31 of Act 504 consequent to the amendment made to section 30 of Act 504.

17. Clause 16 seeks to amend section 34a of Act 504 consequent to the amendment made to sections 13 and 28 of Act 504. This clause also seeks to introduce a new subsection (3a) into section 34a of Act 504 to provide that pending the decision of any judicial review filed under subsection 34(1) of Act 504, no request for a review under section 13, 13a, 13b, 13c, 28, 28a, 28b or 28c shall be filed under Act 504.

The proposed new section 37a seeks to provide for the circumstances when the Government is required to conduct an anti-circumvention investigation and requirement to submit sufficient evidence of the existence of such circumvention.

The proposed new section 37b seeks to provide for determination relating to the anti-circumvention initiated under section 37a of Act 504, and its procedures.

19. Clause 21 deals with saving provision.

20. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Explanatory Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
