## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Hire-Purchase Act 1967. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Hire-Purchase (Amendment) Act 2025.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette and the Minister may appoint different dates for the coming into operation of different provisions of this Act.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Hire-Purchase Act 1967 [Act 212], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in subsection 2(1)—

(a) by deleting the definition of “base lending rate”;

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) by inserting after the definition of “dealer” the following definitions:

‘ “effective interest rate” refers to—

(a) a rate that reflects the actual finance cost of a hire-purchase in the case of a hire-purchase agreement where the terms charges are at a fixed rate; or

(b) a rate that reflects the actual finance cost of a hire-purchase which is determined by using the reference rate as a benchmark in the case of a hire-purchase agreement where the terms charges are at a variable rate;

“electronic” has the same meaning assigned to it in the Electronic Commerce Act 2006 [Act 658];’;

(c) by inserting after the definition of “prescribed” the following definition:

‘ “reference rate” refers to the benchmark interest rate that forms the basis for determining the pricing of variable rate financing facilities, whereby the benchmark rate is the published prevailing overnight policy rate;’; and

(d) by deleting the definition of “statutory rebate”.

### Saving and transitional

22. (1) Any action, investigation or proceedings for any offence under the principal Act committed before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, after the date of coming into operation of this Act, be continued as if the principal Act has not been amended by this Act.

(2) Any obligation, liability, penalty or punishment imposed, made, accrued or incurred under the principal Act on the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, after the date of coming into operation of this Act, continue to be in force and have effect as if the principal Act has not been amended by this Act.

(3) For the purposes of section 14 of the principal Act, where a hirer and owner has entered into a hire-purchase agreement prior to and which continues to exist after the date of coming into operation of this Act, such hirer and the owner may mutually agree to elect the method of calculating the net balance due under the hire-purchase agreement in accordance with the principal Act as amended by this Act.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Hire-Purchase Act 1967 (“Act 212”) by providing fairer outcomes to consumers under a hire-purchase agreement particularly the replacement of the existing method for terms charges calculation. Other amendments include streamlining the maximum limit for terms charges in relation to hire-purchase agreements using fixed or variable rate, enabling the adoption of new technologies to execute hire-purchase agreements, and updating outdated terms to align with prevailing regulatory standards and technological developments.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and provisions on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 2(1) of Act 212.

<!-- page 13 -->

Subclause 2(a) seeks to delete the definition of “base lending rate”.

Subclause 2(b) seeks to introduce the definitions of “effective interest rate” and “electronic”.

Subclause 2(c) seeks to introduce the definition of “reference rate”. Reference rate refers to the benchmark interest rate that serves as the basis for determining the pricing of variable rate financing facilities, whereby the benchmark rate is the published prevailing overnight policy rate as set out in the Monetary Policy Statement of the Monetary Policy Committee of Central Bank of Malaysia. The Monetary Policy Committee is responsible for formulating monetary policy including deciding on the appropriate overnight policy rate which influences the lending and financing rates in Malaysia.

Subclause 2(d) seeks to delete the definition of “statutory rebate”.

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend subsection 4(2) of Act 212 to delete the words “by delivering it in person” and “under his hand”.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 4b of Act 212 to introduce a new subsection (1a) which requires an owner to undertake a due diligence process to verify the identity of the hirer.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 4c of Act 212 to substitute for the words “base lending rate” the words “reference rate”, and to update the references to “annual percentage rate” to “effective interest rate”.

7. Clause 6 seeks to amend section 4e of Act 212 to ensure the references to “registration certificate” are consistent with the Road Transport Act 1987 [Act 333].

8. Clause 7 seeks to amend section 5 of Act 212 to introduce new subsections (1b) and (1c).

The proposed new subsections (1b) and (1c) seek to impose obligations on the owner in relation to the service of the hire-purchase agreement through electronic means.

9. Clause 8 seeks to amend subsection 6a(2) of Act 212 to substitute for the words “base lending rate” the words “reference rate”.

10. Clause 9 seeks to amend section 6b of Act 212 in consequence of the adoption of the new method for the calculation of terms charges.

This clause further seeks to provide the timeline for the owner to inform the hirer on the changes to the hirer’s financial obligations following any revision to the effective interest rate.

<!-- page 14 -->

This clause also seeks to make it an offence if the owner fails to comply with the notification requirement due to the importance of ensuring that the hirer is well informed of the changes.

11. Clause 10 seeks to amend section 6c of Act 212 to substitute for the words “base lending rate” the words “effective interest rate”.

12. Clauses 11, 12 and 13 seek to amend sections 14, 15 and 18 of Act 212 respectively in consequence of the adoption of the new method for calculating the net balance due or the balance outstanding under the hire-purchase agreement when there is early completion or termination of the agreement, or where the owner takes possession of the goods.

13. Clause 14 seeks to amend section 43 of Act 212 to make electronic means as one of the methods of service for notices.

14. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 45 of Act 212 to ensure that the formatting requirements for any prescribed documents under this section shall apply irrespective of the form of the documents.

15. Clause 16 seeks to introduce a new section 45a into Act 212 to recognize the legality of a relevant notice or document which is made in electronic form.

16. Clause 17 seeks to introduce a new subsection 57a(3) into Act 212 to provide a provision that allows flexibility for hirer and owner who have entered into hire-purchase agreement before the coming into force of this amendment to elect the method of calculating the net balance due under the hire-purchase agreement in accordance with the changes made to the Sixth Schedule to Act 212 for the purpose of early completion of the agreement, as provided under section 14 of Act 212.

17. Clause 18 seeks to amend Part I of the Second Schedule to Act 212.

Subclause 18(a) seeks to substitute for the word “Rate” in subsubparagraph 4(a)(i), in Part I of the Second Schedule the words “Effective interest rate”.

Subclause 18(b) seeks to amend subsubparagraph 4(b)(i), in Part I of the Second Schedule by substituting for the word “BLR” the words “reference rate”.

Subclause 18(c) seeks to delete the words “Annual Percentage Rate**” and the words “RM..........................” appearing against it.

Subclause 18(d) seeks to substitute for the word “BLR” in the note column of Part I of the Second Schedule the words “reference rate”.

18. Clause 19 seeks to amend paragraph (b) of the Fifth Schedule to Act 212 to reflect the revised cost computation methodology applicable to repossessed goods where the same calculation is used regardless of whether the terms charges are at a fixed or variable rate. The amendment also seeks to reflect the incorporation of the surrender value of the contract of insurance which is required to be deducted from the balance due under the hire-purchase agreement.

<!-- page 15 -->

19. Clause 20 seeks to substitute the Sixth Schedule to Act 212 to reflect the calculation of the terms charges where the same calculation is used regardless of whether the terms charges are at a fixed or variable rate.

20. Clause 21 seeks to delete the Seventh Schedule to Act 212 following the use of “effective interest rate” which replaces “annual percentage rate”.

21. Clause 22 deals with saving and transitional provisions.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
