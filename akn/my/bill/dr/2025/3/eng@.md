## A BILL

intituled

An Act to apply a sum from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure for the service of the year 2024 and to appropriate that sum for certain purposes for that year. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title

1. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Supply (2024) Act 2025.

### Additional expenditure for the year 2024

2. The issue is hereby authorized of a sum not exceeding twenty billion one hundred and ninety-one million two hundred and ninety-nine thousand five hundred ringgit (RM20,191,299,500) from the Consolidated Fund for additional expenditure on the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2024 not provided for or not fully provided for by the Supply Act 2024 [Act A1697].

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to provide for the issue from the Consolidated Fund of a sum of twenty billion one hundred and ninety-one million two hundred and ninety-nine thousand five hundred ringgit (RM20,191,299,500) for the services and purposes specified in the Schedule for the year 2024.
