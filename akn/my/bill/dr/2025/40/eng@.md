## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Perbadanan Kemajuan Filem Nasional Malaysia Act 1981. [ ]

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Perbadanan Kemajuan Filem Nasional Malaysia Act 1981 (“Act 244”).

1. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

2. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 244 to amend certain existing definitions.

3. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 4 of Act 244 to alter the membership of the Perbadanan so as to update its composition in accordance with the current needs. This includes the removal of the post Deputy Chairman from the membership of the Perbadanan.

4. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 6 of Act 244 to expand the powers of the Perbadanan. Through this amendment, the Perbadanan is given the power to provide training relating to film industry and to do such other things arising out of or connected with any of the functions of the Perbadanan under Act 244. This includes requiring that necessary information and document be provided to the Perbadanan in the course of performing its functions.

5. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 7 of Act 244 to provide that the Chairman or any person or body to whom the functions or powers of the Perbadanan under section 7 of Act 244 have been delegated shall observe and have regard to all conditions and restrictions imposed by the Perbadanan and all requirements, procedures and matters as specified by the Perbadanan.

6. Clause 6 seeks to amend subsection 8(2) of Act 244 in the national language text to rectify the reference to the Perbadanan.

7. Clause 7 seeks to amend subsection 13(3) of Act 244 to alter the composition of a committee appointed by the Perbadanan from not more than five members to not less than two but not more than six members.

<!-- page 11 -->

(Amendment)

8. Clause 8 seeks to amend section 15 of Act 244 to provide for the obligation imposed on the member of the Perbadanan or member of a committee of the Perbadanan to declare to the Perbadanan the nature and extent of share or interest which he has or acquires through his family member or his associate. This clause also seeks to increase the penalty for the offence under section 15 of Act 244 from imprisonment of five years to ten years and from a fine of twenty-five thousand ringgit to two hundred and fifty thousand ringgit.

9. Clause 9 seeks to introduce a new section 20a into Act 244 to provide for the power of the Perbadanan to establish companies under the Companies Act 2016 [Act 777] to carry out or be involved in activities of the Perbadanan which have been planned or undertaken by the Perbadanan in the course of performing the functions and exercising the powers of the Perbadanan under paragraphs 6(1)(f) and 6(2)(b), (c) and (h) of Act 244.

10. Clause 10 seeks to delete section 22a of Act 244 which provides for the granting of certificate of merit for short subject film by the Perbadanan as the Perbadanan no longer intends to grant such certificate.

11. Clause 11 seeks to introduce a new subsection 24b(2) into Act 244 to provide for a rebuttable presumption in respect of persons engaging in the production of a film.

12. Clause 12 seeks to amend section 25 of Act 244 to increase the penalty for the offence of contravening any of the provisions under Part V of Act 244 from a fine of fifty thousand ringgit to two hundred and fifty thousand ringgit and from imprisonment of two years to five years.

13. Clause 13 seeks to amend section 26h of Act 244 to provide that no prosecution for an offence under Act 244 or subsidiary legislation made under Act 244 shall be instituted except by or with the written consent of the Public Prosecutor.

14. Clause 14 seeks to amend subsection 26k of Act 244 to update the provision on the compounding of offences under Act 244 or subsidiary legislation made under Act 244.

15. Clause 15 seeks to amend section 29 of Act 244 to emphasize that any member, officer or servant of the Perbadanan, or any member of a committee which is appointed by the Perbadanan, shall maintain the secrecy of any information concerning the organization, business, finance, transaction or affairs of the Perbadanan or any of its committees whether during or after the tenure of office or employment of the member, officer or servant of the Perbadanan or member of the committee. This clause also seeks to increase the penalty for the offence under section 29 of Act 244 from imprisonment of two years to three years and from a fine of five thousand ringgit to one hundred thousand ringgit.

16. Clause 16 seeks to introduce new sections 29a and 29b into Act 244.

<!-- page 12 -->

The new section 29a makes it an offence for any person who makes, signs or furnishes untrue, inaccurate or misleading any declaration, return, certificate or any other document or information required to be given under Act 244 or subsidiary legislation made under Act 244.

The new section 29b empowers the Perbadanan to issue guidelines as may be expedient or necessary for the better carrying out of the provisions of Act 244.

17. Clause 17 seeks to amend section 34 of Act 244 to enhance the provision on regulations making power of the Perbadanan under Act 244.

18. Clause 18 seeks to amend section 34a of Act 244 to empower the Minister to exempt any person or class of persons from any or all of the provisions of subsidiary legislation made under Act 244.

19. Clause 19 seeks to amend the Schedule to Act 244 to introduce a new paragraph 1a into the Schedule to provide for the appointment of temporary Chairman of the Perbadanan and to amend subparagraph 3(2) of the Schedule to Act 244 consequential to the removal of the post of Deputy Chairman from the membership of the Perbadanan.

20. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Explanatory Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
