## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Passports Act 1966. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Passports (Amendment) Act 2025.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 2

2. The Passports Act 1966 [Act 150] is amended in section 2—

(a) in subsection (1), by inserting after the words “shall produce to an immigration officer” the words “or provide through an automated system,”; and

(b) in subsection (2), by inserting after the words “produce to that officer” the words “or provide through an automated system,”.

<!-- page 2 -->

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Passports Act 1966 (“Act 150”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 2 of Act 150 to provide that any person entering Malaysia or leaving Malaysia shall provide a passport through an automated system. This clause is a consequential amendment to the proposed amendments of the Immigration Act 1959/63 [Act 155] which requires every person entering Malaysia or leaving Malaysia to be examined by the immigration officer or through an automated system.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
