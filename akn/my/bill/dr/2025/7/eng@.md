## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Energy Commission Act 2001. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Energy Commission (Amendment) Act 2025.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 14

2. The Energy Commission Act 2001 [Act 610] is amended in subsection 14(1)—

(a) in paragraph (n), by deleting the word “and” at the end of the paragraph; and

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) by inserting after paragraph (n) the following paragraph:

“(na) to exercise regulatory functions in respect of the importation of electricity and exportation of electricity; and”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Energy Commission Act 2001 (“Act 610”) following the amendments to the Electricity Supply Act 1990 [Act 447]. Through the proposed amendments to Act 447, the Commission is charged with a regulatory function over the importation of electricity and exportation of electricity.

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 14(1) of Act 610 by introducing new paragraph 14(1)(na) into Act 610. With this proposed amendment, the Commission is charged with a regulatory function over the importation of electricity and exportation of electricity.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
