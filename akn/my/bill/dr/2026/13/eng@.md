## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Control of Padi and Rice Act 1994. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Control of Padi and Rice (Amendment) Act 2026.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 4

2. The Control of Padi and Rice Act 1994 [Act 522], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in paragraph 4(2)(c), by substituting for the words “to fix” the words “to enforce”.

<!-- page 2 -->

### Amendment of section 22

3. Section 22 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (1)—

(i) by substituting for the words “fifteen thousand ringgit” the words “two hundred and fifty thousand ringgit”; and

(ii) by substituting for the words “twenty-five thousand ringgit” the words “five hundred thousand ringgit”; and

(b) in subsection (2)—

(i) by inserting after the words “any regulation made thereunder” the words “in respect of which no penalty is expressly provided for,”;

(ii) by substituting for the words “twenty-five thousand ringgit” the words “five hundred thousand ringgit”; and

(iii) by substituting for the words “fifty thousand ringgit” the words “one million ringgit”.

### Amendment of section 29

4. Section 29 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) by renumbering the existing section as subsection (1);

(b) in subsection (1) as renumbered—

(i) in paragraph (c), by inserting after the words “retailers,” the words “padi purchasers,”;

(ii) by inserting after paragraph (d) the following paragraphs:

“(da) prescribe the grades of rice and the price control for the graded rice;

<!-- page 3 -->

(db) make provisions relating to the prohibition, regulation or control of the movement of padi or rice; and”; and

(iii) by deleting paragraph (e); and

(c) by inserting after subsection (1) as renumbered the following subsection:

“(2) Any regulations made under this Act may prescribe an act or omission in contravention of the regulations to be an offence and may prescribe penalties of a fine not exceeding fifteen thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both.”.

### Saving

5. Any investigation or proceedings commenced or pending before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, after the date of coming into operation of this Act, be continued as if the principal Act has not been amended by this Act.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Control of Padi and Rice Act 1994 (“Act 522”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend paragraph 4(2)(c) of Act 522 to empower the Director General, subject to the prior approval of the Minister, to enforce maximum or minimum price of padi or rice which also is consequential to the amendment made to section 29 of Act 522 which now empowers the Minister to prescribe the grades of rice and the price control for the graded rice.

<!-- page 4 -->

4. Clause 3 seeks to amend section 22 of Act 522.

Subclause 3(a) seeks to amend subsection 22(1) of Act 522 to increase the fine imposed on a person, other than a body corporate, but including a director or officer of a body corporate, who commits an offence under, or who fails to comply with, any of the provisions of Act 522 or any regulation made under Act 522 in respect of which no penalty is expressly provided for, from an amount of not exceeding fifteen thousand ringgit to an amount of not exceeding two hundred and fifty thousand ringgit and for a second or subsequent offence from an amount of not exceeding twenty-five thousand ringgit to an amount of not exceeding five hundred thousand ringgit.

Subclause 3(b) seeks to amend subsection 22(2) of Act 522 to increase the fine imposed on a body corporate which commits an offence under, or fails to comply with, any of the provisions of Act 522 or any regulation made under Act 522 in respect of which no penalty is expressly provided for, from an amount of not exceeding twenty-five thousand ringgit to an amount of not exceeding five hundred thousand ringgit and for a second or subsequent offence from an amount of not exceeding fifty thousand ringgit to an amount of not exceeding one million ringgit.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend section 29 of Act 522 to empower the Minister to make regulations to provide for matter relating to the licensing of padi purchasers, to prescribe the grades of rice and the price control for the graded rice and to provide for matter relating to the prohibition, regulation or control of the movement of padi or rice. This clause also seeks to provide that any regulations made under Act 522 may prescribe an act or omission in contravention of the regulations to be an offence and may prescribe penalties of a fine not exceeding fifteen thousand ringgit or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both.

6. Clause 5 deals with saving provision.

7. Other amendments not specifically dealt with in this Explanatory Statement are minor or consequential in nature.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
