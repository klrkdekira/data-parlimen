## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Witness Protection Act 2009. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Witness Protection (Amendment) Act 2026.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 7

2. The Witness Protection Act 2009 [Act 696], which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Act, is amended in subsection 7(3) by inserting after the words “under eighteen years of age” the words “or lacks legal capacity”.

<!-- page 2 -->

### New sections 10a and 10b

3. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 10 the following sections:

### “Agreement

10a. (1) A witness who has been included as a participant in the Programme by the Attorney General shall sign an agreement in accordance with this section.

(2) For the purposes of subsection (1)—

(a) where the participant is under eighteen years of age, a parent or guardian of the participant shall sign the agreement; or

(b) where the participant lacks legal capacity to sign the agreement, a parent or guardian of the participant shall sign the agreement.

(3) The form of the agreement in relation to a participant shall be as determined by the Director General and may contain—

(a) the terms and conditions on which protection and assistance is to be provided to the participant;

(b) an agreement by or on behalf of the participant not to compromise directly or indirectly the security of or any other aspect of the protection or assistance being provided to the participant; and

(c) an agreement by or on behalf of the participant that the participant shall comply with all reasonable directions given by the Director General in relation to the protection and assistance provided to the participant.

(4) An agreement shall be signed by, or on behalf of the participant in the presence of a person who holds or occupies a designated position as determined by the Director General.

<!-- page 3 -->

### Saving of existing agreement

10 b. Every agreement lawfully entered into between a participant and the Government before the coming into force of this Act, shall if it is still legally binding upon the parties, continue in force for such period as may be specified in the agreement and the parties shall be subject and entitled to the benefits under this Act.”.

### Amendment of subsection 13(2)

4. Subsection 13(2) of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (i), by deleting the word “and” at the end of the paragraph;

(b) by inserting after paragraph (i) the following paragraph:

“(ia) providing psychological assistance, including counselling to the participant; and”.

### Amendment of section 16

5. Section 16 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (1)—

(i) in paragraph (c), by deleting the word “or” at the end of the paragraph;

(ii) by inserting after paragraph (c) the following paragraph:

“(ca) the participant deliberately breaches any terms or conditions of the agreement; or”;

(b) by inserting after subsection 16(11) the following subsections:

“(12) Upon notification of the termination of the Programme to the participant under paragraph 11(a), the Director General may continue to provide the protection and assistance to the participant as

<!-- page 4 -->

he considers necessary and reasonable for the purpose of facilitating the participant’s safe transition to a new life.

(13) The protection and assistance provided to the participant may include, but is not limited to, relocation to a safe place, financial assistance, psychological assistance and job placement assistance.”.

### New section 29a

6. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section 29 the following section:

### “Standing Order

29a. (1) The Director General may issue Standing Order, not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, on the administration, management, control, training, duties and responsibilities of officers and for such other matters as may be necessary or expedient for the administration and the implementation of the Programme.

(2) Any directive or administrative order made by the Director General and in force immediately before the date of coming into operation of section 29a shall, so far as it is not inconsistent with this Act, continue to remain in force until it is revoked by the Director General.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Witness Protection Act 2009 (“Act 696”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend subsection 7(3) of Act 696 to enable an application for inclusion in the Programme to be made on behalf of a witness who lacks legal capacity by a parent or guardian.

4. Clause 3 seeks to introduce new sections 10a and 10b, into Act 696.

The new section 10a requires a participant, or a person acting on the participant’s behalf, to enter into a written agreement setting out the terms and conditions of protection and assistance of the programme, the participant’s obligations, and related matters, including execution of the agreement before a designated officer.

<!-- page 5 -->

The new section 10b provides for the continuation of existing agreements entered into between a participant and the Government before the coming into force of the Act, where such agreements remain in force, and provides that the parties to the agreement shall be subject to, and entitled to the benefits under the Act.

5. Clause 4 seeks to amend subsection 13(2) of Act 696 to introduce a new paragraph (ia) to include psychological assistance, including counselling, as part of the assistance provided to a participant.

6. Clause 5 seeks to amend section 16 of Act 696.

Subclause 5(a) seeks to amend subsection 16(1) of Act 696 to introduce a new paragraph (ca) to provide an additional ground for the termination of protection and assistance granted to a participant.

Subclause 5(b) seeks to introduce new subsections 16(12) and (13) into Act 696 to empower the Director General to continue providing protection and assistance to a participant upon notification of the termination of the Programme under paragraph 11(a), for the purpose of facilitating the participant’s safe transition to a new life and to specify the types of assistance that may be provided.

7. Clause 6 seeks to introduce new section 29a into Act 696 to empower the Director General to issue Standing Order for the administration and implementation of the Programme. The new section also provides for the continued operation of existing directives and administrative orders issued by the Director General.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure the amount of which cannot at present be ascertained.
