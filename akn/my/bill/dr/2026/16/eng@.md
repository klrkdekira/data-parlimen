## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Courts of Judicature Act 1964. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Courts of Judicature (Amendment) Act 2026.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Amendment of section 24

2. The Courts of Judicature Act 1964 [Act 91] is amended in section 24—

(a) by substituting for paragraph (b) the following paragraph:

“(b) jurisdiction under any written law relating to admiralty matters;”;

(b) in paragraph (c), by inserting after the word “bankruptcy” the words “, insolvency including cross-border insolvency”; and

<!-- page 2 -->

(c) by inserting after paragraph (c) the following paragraph:

“(ca) jurisdiction to hear any application under the International Settlement Agreements Resulting from Mediation Act 2026 [Act 881];”.

### Saving

3. Any admiralty proceedings commenced or pending immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act shall, on the date of coming into operation of this Act, be dealt with in accordance with the jurisdiction of the High Court immediately before the date of coming into operation of this Act.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Courts of Judicature Act 1964 (“Act 91”) following the enactment of the Admiralty Jurisdiction Act 2026, the Cross-Border Insolvency Act 2026 [Act 877] and the International Settlement Agreements Resulting from Mediation Act 2026 [Act 881].

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to amend section 24 of Act 91.

Subclause (a) seeks to provide for the new admiralty jurisdiction of the High Court to replace the jurisdiction provided under United Kingdom’s Senior Courts Act 1981.

Subclause (b) seeks to provide for the new jurisdiction of the High Court in relation to insolvency including cross-border insolvency.

Subclause (c) seeks to provide for the new jurisdiction of the High Court in relation to international settlement agreements resulting from mediation.

4. Clause 3 seeks to provide for a saving provision.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
