## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the provisions of Part I of the Tenth Schedule to the Federal Constitution. [ ]

WHEREAS by Clause (2) of Article 109 of the Federal Constitution it is provided that Parliament may from time to time by law vary the rates of the capitation grant appearing in Part I of the Tenth Schedule to the Constitution:

AND WHEREAS pursuant to Clause (4)(b) of Article 159 of the Federal Constitution any amendment to the Constitution incidental to or consequential on the exercise of any power to make law conferred on Parliament by any provision of the Constitution, other than Articles 74 and 76, is excepted from the provisions of Clause (3) of Article 159:

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Capitation Grant Act 2026.

(2) This Act comes into operation on 1 April 2026.

<!-- page 2 -->

### Amendment of Part I of the Tenth Schedule to the Federal

### Constitution

2. Subsection 1(1) of Part I of the Tenth Schedule to the Federal Constitution is amended by substituting for paragraphs (a), (b), (c) and (d) the following paragraphs:

“(a) for the first 100,000 persons at the rate of RM102.00 per person;

(b) for the next 500,000 persons at the rate of RM14.00 per person;

(c) for the next 500,000 persons at the rate of RM13.50 per person;

(d) for the remainder at the rate of RM13.00 per person,”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend Part I of the Tenth Schedule to the Federal Constitution to vary the rates of capitation grant to each State in accordance with the new rates structure. The purpose of the variation is to increase the capitation grants given to States and to ensure fairer treatment for the less populous States and at the same time the more populous States will also receive higher allocations.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will involve the Government in extra financial expenditure of up to RM548 million.
