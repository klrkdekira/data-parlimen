## A BILL

intituled

An Act to amend the Sexual Offences against Children Act 2017. [ ]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Malaysia as follows:

### Short title and commencement

1. (1) This Act may be cited as the Sexual Offences against Children (Amendment) Act 2026.

(2) This Act comes into operation on a date to be appointed by the Minister by notification in the Gazette.

### Substitution of section 3

2. The Sexual Offences against Children Act 2017 [Act 792] is amended by substituting for section 3 the following section:

### “Extra-territorial application

3. Where any offence under this Act, or any offence specified in the Schedule where the victim is a child is committed in any place outside Malaysia—

(a) by a citizen of Malaysia, or a permanent resident of Malaysia, or a person who has his habitual residence in Malaysia against any child; or

<!-- page 2 -->

(b) by any person against a child who is a citizen of Malaysia, or a permanent resident of Malaysia, or who has his habitual residence in Malaysia,

the offence may be dealt with as if it has been committed at any place within Malaysia.”.

## EXPLANATORY STATEMENT

This Bill seeks to amend the Sexual Offences against Children Act 2017 (“Act 792”).

2. Clause 1 contains the short title and the provision on the commencement of the proposed Act.

3. Clause 2 seeks to substitute section 3 of Act 792 to expand the extra-territorial application of Act 792. The existing section 3 of Act 792 provides for the extra-territorial application of Act 792 where any offence under Act 792, or any offence specified in the Schedule to Act 792 where the victim is a child, is committed outside Malaysia by a citizen of Malaysia against any child. The proposed substitution of section 3 of Act 792 therefore seeks to expand the extra-territorial application of Act 792 to offences under Act 792 and offences specified in the Schedule where the victim is a child, committed outside Malaysia, by a permanent resident of Malaysia or a person whose habitual residence is in Malaysia against any child, and by any person against a child who is a citizen of Malaysia or a permanent resident of Malaysia or whose habitual residence is in Malaysia. This will enable Act 792 to apply to a wider category of persons having connection to Malaysia in respect of offences committed outside Malaysia.

## FINANCIAL IMPLICATIONS

This Bill will not involve the Government in any extra financial expenditure.
